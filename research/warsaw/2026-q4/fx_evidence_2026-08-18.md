# FX Evidence: PLN → USD Conversion Basis

## Source
ECB euro foreign exchange reference rates
URL: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-daily.xml
Fetched: 2026-08-18
Cube time: 2026-08-18

## Rates
- EUR 1 = USD 1.1576
- EUR 1 = PLN 4.3190

## Derived Conversion
- 1 PLN = EUR (1/4.3190) = USD (1.1576/4.3190) = USD 0.268048
- 1 USD = PLN (4.3190/1.1576) = PLN 3.73122

## Usage
Every venue cost in PLN is converted to USD using this dated basis.
The rate_estimate_note on each venue record cites this exact source and date.

## Limitations
- This is a published reference rate, not a live market rate.
- The rate does not predict the 2026 Q4 rate at event time.
- Re-confirm the conversion basis at booking time.
