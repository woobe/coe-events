DevFest Budapest is a globally recognized developer conference that is part of the broader DevFest conference series organized by Google Developer Groups across the globe.

The conference is going to take place in 2027, in Budapest. (Participation: in-person)

This event aims to unite Developers from Hungary and diverse corners of the globe, fostering mutual learning and an enjoyable experience for all participants.

The event is coordinated by a globally diverse team of developers, comprising members from the Google Developer Groups Budapest community, the GDG On Campus communities at BME, ELTE, and Debrecen Universities, and the International Investment Club, and Nextgen Data Science Club at Corvinus University.

Our mission is to unite the brightest emerging talent from Hungary's top universities with seasoned industry professionals.

We invite software engineers, architects, Googlers, Google Developer Experts, and tech leaders to share their practical experiences, architectural deep dives, and cutting-edge implementations.


We are particularly looking for talks across seven key focus areas:


1. Software Development

Show us the foundations of great engineering. We are looking for sessions on modern software architecture, advanced design patterns, API design, and backend performance optimization. Share your insights on writing clean, maintainable code, managing technical debt, building robust enterprise applications, and adopting developer productivity tools.


2. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Dive deep into the models and applied research shaping the future. We want practical, developer-focused sessions on building real-world AI applications. Topics include the Gemini ecosystem, open-weight models (like the Gemma family), designing autonomous AI agents, advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, on-device AI, and the implementation of Responsible AI principles.


3. Cloud Computing

Share your expertise in building scalable, secure, and resilient cloud-native solutions. We are seeking talks on Google Cloud infrastructure, serverless computing, modern modernization strategies, and cloud security. Show us how you leverage managed cloud services to solve complex architectural challenges from the ground up.


4. Data Science & Engineering

Turn raw data into actionable insights. This track focuses on big data processing, building robust data engineering pipelines, advanced analytics, MLOps, and statistical modeling. We highly welcome case studies that demonstrate how to bridge the gap between academic data theory and real-world business applications.


5. FinOps & Cloud Economics

Where finance meets engineering. As cloud environments scale, managing costs becomes a core architectural requirement. Share your frameworks for cloud financial management, optimizing resource spend, cost allocation strategies, and fostering a culture of financial accountability and transparency within technical teams.


6. Mobile Application Development

Shape the future of mobile experiences. We are looking for technical deep-dives into modern mobile frameworks like Flutter and Kotlin Multiplatform, native Android development, cross-platform architecture, state management, and integrating on-device machine learning seamlessly into mobile applications.


7. DevOps, Microservices & Kubernetes

Show us the engine of modern platform engineering. We want hands-on sessions covering container orchestration, the Kubernetes ecosystem, and managing distributed microservices. Dive into CI/CD automation, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), observability stacks (Prometheus/Grafana), and implementing strict GitOps workflows (such as ArgoCD) for secure, reproducible deployments.


What we are looking for:

We're looking for experience-based talks—the information that cannot be found in the documentation or easily googled. We want your stories, your struggles, root-cause analyses, and experiences with frontier tools or complex enterprise migrations.


We are interested in anything that squarely fits into the spectrum of:

  • Cutting-edge technological advances within the above topics
  • Wild ideas, open-source tooling, & clever architectural hacks others can learn from
  • Improving developer life, operational autonomy, and productivity
  • Lessons learned from successful deployments and disastrous project launches
  • Whatever you consider highly useful for the engineering and student community


Session formats:

You can submit as many talks as you want. This year we accept submissions in the following format:

  • 15-minute Lightning talks: Quick, punchy insights or highly focused technical demos.
  • 30-minute Sessions: In-depth architectural breakdowns, case studies, or applied engineering deep-dives.
  • 60-minute Workshops: Hands-on workshops


Talks that will not pass initial review:

  • Talks whose primary focus is pitching a company or a commercial product. Mentioning your company is welcome, but it should not be the core focus of the presentation.
  • Proposals that are not in English.
  • Recruitment talks designed purely to attract talent.
