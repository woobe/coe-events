About us
Welcome to the Hungarian Ethereum community 👋
Please be kind and respectful, and follow the community guidelines.
This group is for:
Discussing news and events closely related to Ethereum and its ecosystem.
Learning and exchanging ideas about Ethereum and decentralized, open source technologies, as well as related economic and social topics.
Meeting like-minded people and finding ways to connect and collaborate.
This group is NOT for:
Discussing investment strategies and price speculation.
Soliciting, and promoting any project or product. This includes contacting other community members.
Any form of violation of the community rules may result in removal from the group.
All are welcome, including developers, enthusiasts, newbies, skeptics, and more 🙂
If you are new to Ethereum, you can do some reading here: Ethereum.org
Join our Hungarian community chat
Follow us on Twitter
For more info visit the community's Notion site: Ethereum Hungary
