About us

This Budapest Databricks Meetup Group is dedicated to bringing together database professionals, business intelligence experts, and technology enthusiasts to discuss the latest trends and developments in data management. We will explore topics such as business intelligence, data platform modernization and data warehousing focusing mainly on Databricks - one of the most popular data platforms of the present. Join us for informative discussions, workshops, and networking opportunities.
