AI & Digital Transformation 2026
26th November 2026 Marriott Hotel Budapest
Our lives—and with them, the way companies operate—are becoming digitized at an astonishing pace; paper-based processes are disappearing, and we only visit branch offices for in-person transactions when dealing with the most complex matters; and we communicate and handle matters via digital channels 24/7. But now, the AI revolution and the agentic AI trend are turning the digital world, internal operations, and customer-facing front-ends upside down. AI agents capable of acting autonomously, as well as AI tools and enterprise solutions that support specific business, compliance, and administrative processes, offer companies previously unimaginable speed and extraordinary potential for efficiency gains. What do we do with the hours of work we’ve gained and the labor we’ve saved? Is artificial intelligence disrupting core business as well? What is vibe coding good for? At our event for both large corporations and SMEs, we’ll be seeking and providing answers to these questions and more!

Agricultural Conference 2026
2nd–3rd December 2026 Siófok, Hotel Azúr Wellness **** Siófok
The Portfolio Group's Agricultural Sector Conference is already the largest agribusiness event and summit in Hungary in terms of content. The aim of the conference is to summarize and analyze the year's outstanding domestic and international agribusiness events and to provide a forecast for the coming years to help agribusiness players make successful business and investment decisions. The conference offers a three-day professional programme: the event will start with a festive professional evening, followed by two further days of highly complex and exhaustively detailed professional content. The conference will feature top leaders from the national government, banking, corporate and advocacy sectors who will provide first-hand, relevant information that will be useful for all players in the agricultural economy - producers, food manufacturers and traders. It will also provide a wide range of showcasing and market-building opportunities for businesses serving the agricultural sector - input manufacturers, integrators, machinery distributors, finance and other service providers. In addition to a rich programme of events, the conference will provide an opportunity for professional networking, networking and business meetings, with high-quality technical presentations and round-table discussions, and an entertainment programme to keep participants energised and relaxed. The Portfolio Group will present the annual awards in eleven categories at the Agribusiness Conference, which recognize the most outstanding professional achievements and accomplishments in the agribusiness sector. The prizes are awarded by a jury of leading figures in the agricultural sector on the basis of applications submitted by the operators in the sector.

Insurance 2026
18th March 2027 Hotel InterContinental Budapest Budapest
In 2027, players in the Hungarian insurance market will face both new risks and new opportunities for growth. The Portfolio Insurance 2027 conference will focus on how new growth areas in the domestic health insurance market are taking shape, what directions the new government and the central bank may set for the insurance and savings markets, and what business and strategic outlooksinsurance sector leaders anticipate. The conference’s panel discussions will provide a comprehensive assessment of the challenges facing the market, current regulatory and sales issues, and the most important questions concerning life, property, and liability insurance. The conference will focus not only on domestic developments but also on the international outlook: it will cover the career paths of Hungarian professionals in Europe and the market challenges observed in countries to the west of Hungary. Another key topic will be the role of AI in the insurance market: what the technology promises in terms of sales, operations, and customer service, and what risks and threats it entails. True to Portfolio’s traditions, the event is an important meeting place for insurers, brokers, consultants, and technology providers, where networking plays a central role alongside professional content. The Portfolio Insurance 2027 conference, a major gathering of Hungarian insurers and brokers, will take place on March 18—it’s definitely worth attending!
