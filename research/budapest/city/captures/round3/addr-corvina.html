Event venues

 

 

We offer several premium event venues across the country to provide the perfect setting for your event, whether it is a corporate or a private one.

From exclusive gala dinners and relaxed family days to smaller and larger events, we find the option that will impress our guests with its gastronomy.

Our event venues can host from 100 to 3000 guests. Besides elegant and modern spaces, we also offer unique, distinctive options.
