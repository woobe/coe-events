About us
The Budapest Quant Group is a forum and network for industry practicioners, students, and non-professionals who are interested in quantitative finance and financial modeling.
BQG aims to create a society by organising top-quality meetups, giving opportunity to gain and share knowledge, discuss compelling topics, meet experts from the field, all in an informal atmosphere.
