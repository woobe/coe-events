Event Venues and Vendors in California
California's diverse cultural heritage and geography make it one the most sought after event destinations in the country. With a nearly unlimited selection of sights to see and activities to choose from, California is the ultimate destination.
With eight major metropolitan cities sprinkled along its 840 miles of coast, California is home to the largest number of
venues
in the country, featuring everything from quaint castles to estates, to mansions and resorts
that feature luxurious spas, exclusive activities and amenities, and everything in between.
California offers opportunity for unlimited adventures in the form of World class golfing, deep sea fishing, hiking trails, surfing, shopping, and exciting nightlife. Explore nature's wonders in the snow-covered Sierra Nevada Mountains, get a taste of old-world charm in California's wine country, or marvel at the view from San Francisco's famous Golden Gate Bridge.
The options are endless in the Golden State. If you can dream it, you can do it in California. However you want to delight your guests for your wedding, party, or meeting, it can be done here. Visit
California Tourism
and discover the 12 beautiful regions of California.
