We ensure the success of your event, however big!
Organising a high-profile professional programme or corporate event always comes with a lot of responsibility. Success boils down to the details, and so it’s good to choose a venue that’s easily reachable, where the material requirements are in place, and where the bulk of the tasks related to organisation and execution are entrusted to an experienced, professional team.
The Danubius Hotel Hungaria City Center is located near the Papp László Budapest Sports Arena, the Puskás Ferenc Stadium and the BOK Sports and Conference Centre (formerly SYMA) just 5 km from the HUNGEXPO international exhibition centre and 6 km from the Tüskecsarnok event centre, and is thus an ideal place to stay even if you’re holding or attending an event somewhere in Budapest other than our hotel.
Our 12 air-conditioned event rooms, most benefiting from natural light, and boasting modern technical equipment, coupled with our well-trained staff, guarantee the success of any business, company or even family event. Our connecting conference rooms have a maximum capacity of 500 people, and we recommend them for seminars, smaller training events, lectures or business meetings. And thanks to its excellent features, our Beatrix Restaurant is in turn superbly equipped to provide catering for corporate events, client parties, receptions or gala dinners.
Our Danubius me: concept guarantees exceptional locations, modern AV solutions and event spaces that can be adapted to your needs. Find out more here.
