A38 Ship
A38 Ship is anchored next to Petőfi bridge, Buda side on the lower quay. It is accessible by stairs located next
to the tram stop. Please use the pedestrian crossing when you arrive!

Parking
We recommend parking your car at Pázmány Péter sétány, in front of the Eötvös Loránd University building, or
on the lower quay about 100 meters south of A38 Ship. Please note that there are limited parking spaces
directly in front of the ship.
Bicycle
You can reach the ship via the bike path on Műegyetem quay or Pázmány Péter sétány. While we don't have a
dedicated bike locker, you can secure your bike to the railings near the ship's terrace.
Motorboat, Small Boat
You can also arrive by motorboat or small boat! We offer docking at A38 Ship, but please call us at
+36-30/592-8772 before you plan to arrive
this way. See above for details on public transport ships.
