Location
The event space operating as the A38 Bistro is located on the ship’s deck level, immediately to the right of the main entrance. The adjoining terrace opens toward the Pest side and can be accessed both from the restaurant and via a separate entrance.
The restaurant is spacious and bright, with sound-insulated panoramic glass walls running along both longitudinal sides, offering a unique view of the Millennium City Center, the National Theatre, and the Palace of Arts.
The atmosphere of the interior is defined by the warm tones of natural wood surfaces and carefully placed plants. The green features create a fresh, nature-inspired ambience that harmoniously offsets the industrial character of the venue, while providing a calm and inspiring setting. The restaurant’s panoramic windows can be darkened as required.

Location
The Schweppes Terrace, located on the deck level of the newer section of the ship, is one of the A38 Ship’s most distinctive event spaces. It is easily accessible both from the main entrance and through the restaurant.
Renovated in the spring of 2020, the terrace features a metallic, progressive design language that gives the space a strong character while remaining fully aligned with the ship’s overall identity. The stunning Danube panorama is complemented by Schweppes Hungary’s bold design elements, creating a unique visual experience for guests.
More than just a cocktail bar, the Schweppes Terrace offers an exclusive atmosphere and a direct connection to the restaurant, making it an ideal venue for social gatherings, private events, business meetings, press events, or even film and photo shoots.

Location
The concert hall, located in the central section of the ship within the hull, is one of the A38 Ship’s iconic venues and is accessible from the main entrance.
The space is equipped with world-class built-in sound and lighting systems, unique acoustic solutions, comprehensive sound insulation, and a professional broadcast recording system. The single, open-plan interior and its technical setup can be flexibly adapted to meet the specific requirements of each event.
The concert hall is ideally suited for hosting conferences, award ceremonies, fashion shows, large-scale press events, receptions, and live music performances. A key advantage is its versatility as a plenary hall, while the adjoining spaces – the Motor Bar and the Bow Studio – can function as distinctive breakout or section rooms.

Location
The Bow Terrace is located on the same deck level as the restaurant and is accessible from the main entrance. From here, the rooftop terrace can also be reached.
The partially covered terrace at the bow of the ship offers a unique view of the Danube and Budapest’s cityscape, with a panorama extending as far as the Chain Bridge. The deck area is complemented by maritime relics, creating a distinctive and authentic nautical atmosphere.
The Bow Terrace is an ideal venue from spring to autumn for birthday parties, friendly gatherings, and other relaxed events.

Location
The Bow Studio (Orr Stúdió) is located in the bow section of the ship, within the hull, and can be accessed both from the main entrance and from the concert hall.
Completely renovated in 2021, this small-scale venue with a distinctive atmosphere is defined by minimalist and industrial interior design elements. Concert posters preserved on one of the walls pay tribute to the A38 Ship’s iconic events.
The Bow Studio is an ideal choice for smaller concerts, more informal corporate events, and private social gatherings.

Location
The Motor Bar is located in the aft section of the ship, inside the hull, behind the concert hall, and can be accessed from there.
Converted from the ship’s former engine room, this distinctive space retains the vessel’s original character. The combination of industrial design elements, the preserved control panel, the diesel engine, custom furnishings, and ambient lighting creates a bold and unique atmosphere.
The Motor Bar can be connected to the Concert Hall via a corridor, or used as a fully independent space if required. It is an ideal venue for small-scale workshops, press events, presentations, lectures, private gatherings, and birthday celebrations.
Catering
We provide comprehensive catering services for our events, ranging from hot and cold kitchen offerings to welcome bites, finger food menus, and buffet-style selections. Our beverage packages, cocktail bar concepts, welcome drink options, and drink voucher systems can be flexibly tailored to the client’s needs.
Additinal catering
Food
Beverages
Pastries: A carefully curated selection of sweet and savory bites, ideal for welcoming guests.
Sandwich Menus: Open-faced sandwiches prepared on fresh baguette, offering an elegant and practical solution for conferences, press events, or as a midnight selection at private gatherings. Available in vegetarian, vegan, as well as gluten- and lactose-free options.
Cold Platters: Light and classic selections prepared for weddings, birthdays, and festive occasions.
Finger Food: Flexible bite-sized menus for standing receptions. From mini burgers to sushi selections, the offering can be tailored to the style of the event.
Hot Buffet Catering: Buffet-style selections prepared from seasonal ingredients, featuring a variety of starters, main courses, and desserts. Ideal for both corporate and private events.
Welcome drink: A welcome beverage served upon arrival, which may include a cocktail, sparkling wine, non-alcoholic refreshment, or pálinka, according to preference.
Drink vouchers: Redeemable at the bar, drink vouchers provide a practical solution for corporate events, either as a standalone option or combined with beverage packages.
Unlimited beverage packages: Tailor-made drink packages offering unlimited consumption from the selected menu for a pre-agreed duration. Ideal for weddings, birthdays, and corporate events.

Technical equipment
The event spaces of A38 Ship are equipped with a high-level technical infrastructure, enabling the professional execution of a wide range of event formats, including conferences, press events, private functions, workshops, and exclusive receptions. Some of the venues feature built-in sound and lighting systems, a stage, DJ booth, and ambient lighting, while others can be flexibly equipped with installable audio, lighting, video, and camera technology.
