Meetings & Events in Budapest
1,238 sq m of purpose-built event space in downtown
Kempinski Hotel Corvinus Budapest offers organisers 1,238 sq m of purpose-built event space on the first floor. The setting keeps each programme together in one clearly defined part of the hotel. The venues are flexible and separable, each room sized to fit the capacity and creative needs of the event. The Grand Room holds up to 450 guests, and works for meetings, conferences, weddings and exhibitions. Technical support, catering and a committed, creative banquet team stand behind every event.
Guests have a central city address, easy to move between the day's agenda and an evening in the capital.
The result: memories your delegates and guests will remember you for.
Location: Erzsébet Square, downtown Budapest
Event space: 1,238 sq m on the first floor
Largest conference capacity: Up to 450 guests
Planning resources: Floor plan, image gallery and virtual tour
Event services: Technical support and catering
How do I contact Kempinski Hotel Corvinus Budapest to plan an event?
Contact Kempinski Hotel Corvinus Budapest through the 'Request for Proposal' form on this page. You can also email [email protected] with your event brief.
Where can I access the floor plan, image gallery and virtual tour?
You can access the floor plan, image gallery and virtual tour directly from the Meetings & Events page for Kempinski Hotel Corvinus Budapest. Select each planning link to preview the spaces before making an enquiry.
What types of events can Kempinski Hotel Corvinus Budapest host?
Kempinski Hotel Corvinus Budapest can host meetings, conferences, exhibitions, weddings and social gatherings. From focused business discussions to joyful celebrations, every occasion can have its own character.
