Events
Host memorable meetings and celebrations at Budapest Marriott Hotel, where 16 versatile meeting and event spaces in Budapest combine natural daylight, inspiring city views, and a prime Danube location in the heart of the city. From conferences and corporate gatherings to weddings, gala dinners, and special occasions, the Budapest Ballroom welcomes up to 650 guests, supported by modern AV technology, high-speed internet, tailored catering, and Marriott's attentive service. Business travelers can make use of the coworking space, while eligible guests enjoy exclusive access to the M Club Lounge with breakfast at DNB Restaurant and evening refreshments. Li Yu certified, the hotel offers dedicated services for Chinese travellers, making it an ideal choice for international meetings, events, and group stays.

Additional Wedding Services
Dream weddings don't just happen, they're planned. Marriott Certified Wedding Planners are trained to help. After completing demanding coursework, each Marriott Certified Wedding Planner is qualified to coordinate weddings of all types, including ethnic and military weddings. Relying on experience, training, tradition and old-fashioned intuition, Marriott Certified Wedding Planners can help you determine an overall vision for your wedding and help you execute each detail. That includes setting an event budget; deciding on a menu; arranging table settings; and finding florists, photographers, a band and other entertainment for the big day.
With over 16,500 sq ft of space, our wedding venues offer sweeping city and river views.
