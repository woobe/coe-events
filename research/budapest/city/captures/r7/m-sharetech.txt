About us
## ShareTech | AI & DeepTech Community
(formerly Budapest AI Hub)
Welcome to Budapest’s high-signal AI and DeepTech community.
This group was founded as Budapest AI Hub and has grown into a space where engineers, founders, researchers and tech leaders explore the future of artificial intelligence.
Today, as part of ShareTech Foundation, we are expanding the scope beyond pure AI — into robotics, embodied intelligence, hardware innovation and agent-based systems.
//Follow ShareTech Foundation on LinkedIn for future events & speaker
We focus on:
• Frontier AI & foundation models
• Agent-based systems & autonomous infrastructures
• Robotics & embodied intelligence
• Hardware innovation & scalable manufacturing
• Real-world industrial AI applications
Our goal is simple:
Create a serious, engineering-driven community around deep technologies shaping the next decade.
This is not a beginner meetup.
This is where builders meet builders.
***
## What to expect
• Talks from engineers working at leading global AI labs and startups
• Founders building next-generation deeptech companies
• Practical insights — not hype
• High-quality networking with technical depth
We care about signal over noise.
***
## About ShareTech Foundation
ShareTech Foundation is a Budapest-based non-profit building European tech communities across AI, software engineering and hardware innovation.
We operate meetups, educational initiatives and cross-border collaborations — including serving as the Hungarian chapter lead of the Agentics Foundation.
This meetup is part of the ShareTech ecosystem.
***
If you're building, researching or investing in AI and deep technologies — you're in the right place.
