Possibility for Team Rotation
Do you need a modern, flexible and adaptable office space?
Do you want to keep your team engaged, save money and decide how many people can access the office?
Our Team Rotation Membership is the solution!
Extra access cards give your team the opportunity to choose when they work from the office on a rotation-based system. It allows you to be as flexible as you want without paying for empty office space.
