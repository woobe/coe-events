Pesti Vigadó is committed to preserving cultural diversity and maintaining high artistic standards for its audiences.
Since opening in 1865, Pesti Vigadó has served as a central venue for social life in every era.
It has hosted concerts by Strauss, Liszt, Erkel, Wagner, Bartók and Dohnányi, the first art exhibition in Hungary organised by the Pest Art Society and the first representative National Assembly, as well as a presentation of the Blériot XI aircraft and some of the most popular balls of each period.

Get acquainted with the Pesti Vigadó!
Located on the Danube Embankment in the heart of the capital city of Hungary, the Pesti Vigadó functions as a centre of Gesamtkunstwerk, and is open seven days a week to welcome visitors and audiences of various events.
Services
The Pesti Vigadó, an interdisciplinary cultural centre located on the banks of the Danube, provides a distinguished venue for cultural and corporate events, gala evenings, and representative functions. Its historic interiors offer an elegant setting for events hosted by Hungarian and international partners, supported by a high professional standard and meticulous organisation.
