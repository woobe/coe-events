CEU Nádor Event Center
We Create Space for Memorable Moments
A Unique Event Venue
in the Heart of Budapest
Just a few steps from St. Stephen’s Basilica and the Danube River, we welcome our partners and clients in a stunning location. Opened in 2016, CEU Nádor Street site features modern and eco-friendly buildings, making it an ideal venue for events and conferences. The award-winning facilities include a 400-seat auditorium, session-/breakout rooms accommodating 16 to 75 guests, a rooftop garden, and catering services—all to ensure that your event is of the highest standard and delivers a truly unique experience.
Why Choose Us?
The CEU Nádor Event Center is more than just a traditional event venue—it’s a vibrant community space that adds unique value to every event. Our core values include quality, international engagement, openness, knowledge, and inclusivity, which create an inspiring environment for all our clients.

Venue Rental
Located in the heart of Budapest, the CEU Nádor Event Center is an easily accessible venue, situated near the UNESCO World Heritage area. Combining unique architectural solutions with modern technology, our spacious areas provide a truly memorable experience for all visitors.
Our auditorium is modern and elegant, with natural wood flooring and acoustic wall paneling. Depending on the type and size of the event, it is versatile and can be arranged with a stage and technical equipment, making it an ideal location for presentations, conferences, and cultural events for up to 400 people.
Our conference rooms are equipped with 3 built-in PCs and smartboards and can accommodate 34-75 people. Our IT services include video conferencing, broadcasting, and recording.
Our session-/breakout rooms and meeting rooms can accommodate 16-55 people and are equipped with 2 built-in PCs and smartboards, with some featuring a projector. Video conferencing is also available in these rooms.

Additional Services
Sometimes, a successful event requires extra services. If you want to make your event even more memorable and professional, we wholeheartedly recommend the services of our trusted partners.
