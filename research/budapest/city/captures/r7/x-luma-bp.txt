Az eseményről
​💥The premise is: Come and share where you’re currently feeling a block in your life... stuck in a relationship, triggered at work, etc... and I will offer my superpowers to help you notice the gift in the block.
​9 times out of 10 this leads to crazy freedom where big shifts start happening in your life.
​Thus we go from blocked to partying in celebration of all that has opened up!
​If that sounds up your alley, join me at the Block Party on Zoom!
​Others will be on the call and we will work through blocks with a few of you. Every time I do this, someone else received freedom and a gift by going through the process with the person in the hot seat. Freedom and liberation for all! 💥
