About us
Applied AI Budapest is a community for people who want to move beyond AI hype and explore how artificial intelligence creates real value in business.
Our purpose is to bring together professionals, founders, engineers, product leaders, researchers, and organisations that are working with AI in practice. Through recurring meetups, talks, panels, and networking events, we share real-world use cases, lessons learned, and proven approaches from across industries.
We believe the best way to understand AI is through practical examples: what works, what does not, and how teams can apply it responsibly and effectively. Our goal is to help the Budapest business and technology community learn from the best practices of leading companies and turn AI opportunities into measurable results.
Applied AI Budapest is a place to connect, learn, and build the future of practical AI together.
