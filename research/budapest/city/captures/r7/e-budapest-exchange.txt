What is The Budapest Exchange?
An international conference in Budapest on digital ticketing, payments and the practical challenges of running better public transport.
Ticketing, payments & mobility
Contactless fares, digital wallets and connected journeys — alongside AI, cybersecurity and accessible passenger services.
Ideas tested in practice
Discuss integration with existing systems, rollout decisions and everyday operation. Compare what worked, what did not and what could transfer to your network.
Connections beyond the sessions
Exchange experience with transport authorities, operators, payment providers and technology companies from across Europe — in the sessions and beyond.
2026 speakers included BKK, ATM Barcelona, Rejsekort & Rejseplan, FAIRTIQ, Wizz Air and many more.

Thematic Headlines · 2027
Detailed programme to be announced. In the meantime, here are the Thematic Headlines for 2027
01
AI & Transport
Where can AI make a real difference in public transport, from predicting demand to keeping passengers informed when things go wrong?
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE · TRANSPORT OPERATIONS · PASSENGER INFORMATION
02
Cybersecurity
What challenges does the public transport sector face in terms of cybersecurity?
Security · IT
03
Accessibility & Inclusive Design
How can we design apps, ticket machines and passenger information to allow upfront for a better customer experience?
ACCESSIBILITY · INCLUSIVE DESIGN · PASSENGER EXPERIENCE
04
Ticketing, Payments, Banking
How are ticketing schemes, digital payments and banking partnerships shaping mobility? Explore new ways to pay and create value for passengers and operators.
ACCOUNT-BASED TICKETING · DIGITAL WALLETS · BANKING PARTNERSHIPS · MOBILITY FINANCE
05
Blockchain &
Crypto
What can blockchain add to ticketing? Is it a solution or just another complication?
Blockchain · crypto
06
Connected Mobility
How can we better integrate planning and booking of journeys across different operators, modes of transport and borders by keeping users always informed?
MULTIMODAL TRAVEL · INTEROPERABILITY · CROSS-BORDER MOBILITY
