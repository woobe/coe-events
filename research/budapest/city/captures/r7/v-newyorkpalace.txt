The New York Palace is one of Budapest’s best-known and most impressive historic buildings, and for more than 130 years it has been an iconic venue for the city’s elegance and social elite.
The event rooms on the first floor of the building combine classic architectural values with all the technical and comfort requirements of modern business events.
The venue offers 12 event rooms of various sizes, that can be flexibly combined, providing a fitting setting for a wide range of events—from small executive meetings to conferences with hundreds of attendees and even large-scale gala dinners.

Twelve rooms of various sizes that can be flexibly combined are at your disposal, ranging from private meeting rooms to a ballroom that can accommodate up to 500 guests. Our dedicated event planning team handles the technical aspects, design, and food and beverage concepts all under one roof.

Frequently Asked Questions
We've compiled a list of the most frequently asked questions and answers about the events.
Do you have any other questions?
Our event planning team is happy to assist you and provide personalized guidance to help bring your ideas to life.
Contact Us
What is the maximum capacity of the events they host?
The New York Palota’s event spaces offer flexible venue solutions ranging from exclusive business meetings for a minimum of 15 people to large-scale events accommodating up to 500 guests. The exact capacity varies depending on the selected room, the planned program, and the desired layout.
How can you choose the event space that best suits the occasion?
Our event planning team will help you select the most suitable venue based on the type of event, expected number of attendees, program, planned duration, and technical and catering requirements. If necessary, they will also prepare a customized proposal for the combined use of multiple rooms or an adjoining foyer.
What types of room layouts are available to choose from?
Depending on the characteristics of the event hall, we can arrange the seating in a theater-style, classroom-style, U-shape, conference-style, round-table, or standing reception layout. In every case, we tailor the final layout to the event’s program, number of attendees, and catering concept.
Can the event rooms be connected to one another?
Yes. Some of our event rooms and foyers can be connected to one another, allowing us to create multi-room events, parallel sessions, workshops, exhibitions, or separate reception and catering areas. Our staff will help you plan the optimal layout for your event.
Are the venues suitable for hosting conferences and concurrent programs?
Yes. The rooms at the New York Palace are suitable for hosting plenary sessions, smaller breakout sessions, workshops, and business meetings simultaneously. The interconnected rooms and shared lobby areas allow participants to move around comfortably and seamlessly.
Do the event rooms have natural light and the option to darken the room?
Lighting conditions and blackout options vary by room. Our selection includes rooms with natural light as well as rooms that can be completely darkened, so you can choose a venue suitable for presentations, film screenings, conferences, and evening events alike.
Can the event venues be viewed online?
Yes. You can view several of our event spaces through an interactive virtual tour, which will give you a more detailed picture of the spaces’ design, atmosphere, and layout options.
Can I request an in-person site visit?
Of course. Upon prior appointment, our event planning team will arrange a personal site visit during which they will show you the available venues and discuss in detail the event’s setup, technical, and catering options.
Can catering and event equipment be provided for the rooms?
Yes. In addition to the event hall, you can also order a customized food and beverage selection as well as professional technical support. Our in-house event technology team can assist with the planning and implementation of sound systems, lighting, projection equipment, and audiovisual solutions, among other services.
Can an outside event technology service provider be hired?
The New York Palace has its own, exclusive event technology services. It may be possible to engage an external technology service provider upon prior agreement and for an additional fee.
Is there parking available near the event venue?
Yes. The Anantara New York Palace Budapest Hotel’s indoor parking garage is accessible from Osváth Street. Parking is subject to a fee, so for larger events, we recommend coordinating the expected number of vehicles with our event planning team in advance.
