Meeting room rental in the centre of Budapest
The modern meeting rooms at bee@work ANKER provide the ideal venue for business meetings, client presentations, workshops, interviews, board meetings, and corporate events in the heart of Budapest.
Whether you need a meeting room for just one hour or an entire working day, we offer flexible rental options for small teams and larger groups. Choose the room that best suits your meeting and enjoy a professional environment equipped for productive collaboration.
Meeting Room Rental Prices (Gross)
BEE Meeting Room (up to 4 people)
1 hour HUF 5,000
1 working day HUF 30,000
BLUE Meeting Room (up to 12 people)
1 hour HUF 9,000
1 working day HUF 50,000
Conference Room (up to 50 people, Lower Ground Floor)
1 hour HUF 12,000
1 working day HUF 72,000
Book the meeting room that best fits your needs and host your next business meeting, presentation, or event in a professional setting in downtown Budapest.
What is included in the rental fee of a meeting room?
✓ Use of a meeting rooms for 4 to 60 occupants
✓ receiving guests at the reception of bee@work ANKER
✓ audio-visual equipment
✓ unlimited internet access (100 / 100 fibre + Wi-Fi support)
✓ use of the common areas of the building
✓ clients using other bee@work facilities and services are eligible for a discount on their meeting room bookings
