The big room is excellently suited for hosting larger events, press conferences, award ceremonies, parties, conferences, presentations, training sessions, and workshops.
The hall is soundproofed, air-conditioned, accessible from the guest area, and equipped with a projector and projection screen. The arrangement of furniture, whether in rows of chairs or with tables for smaller groups, can be customized in consultation with the Renter.
