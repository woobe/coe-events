## Coverage note

The AI and data layer is well evidenced (AI Tinkerers, PIP, HKML, PyData, Data Science & GenAI, GDG, HKPUG, AWS UG, Global AI, AI Builders). The Web3 layer is thinner and partly stale (Ethereum Hub, WiW3, BAHK, OSHK). There is **no explicit DeAI group** in Hong Kong with own-page activity in this sweep, and no quant group with a 2026 activity date — the honest position is that a Decentralized AI Day in Hong Kong would have to build its DeAI audience from the AI-builder and Web3 groups rather than inherit one.
