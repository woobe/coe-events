AIAIS 2027 | Hong Kong
2027 International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Intelligent Systems (AIAIS)
Event Format : Hybrid (In-person and Virtual)
AIAIS 2027 will be held on January 22 – 24, 2027 at the City University of Hong Kong. This premier event aims to bring together researchers, practitioners, and industry leaders from around the globe to discuss the latest advancements and innovations in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Intelligent Systems.
Participants will have the opportunity to present their research findings, engage in informative discussions, and network with experts in the field. The conference will feature keynote speeches from distinguished speakers, multiple parallel sessions, and workshops focusing on cutting-edge topics.
Conference Topics
► AI Algorithms and Computational Intelligence
► Data Analytics, Perception, and Intelligent Processing
► Intelligent Systems, Control, and Engineering Applications
► Trustworthy, Secure, and Emerging AI Systems
