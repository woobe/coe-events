Registration & Passes
What does my registration include?
Consensus passes grant access to the Consensus tradeshow, as well as all sessions, events and receptions, in accordance with pass level, and subject to capacity restrictions. Consensus passes are non-exchangable and non-refundable and subject to the Attendance Terms & Conditions .
Are there promo codes, discounts, and special rates?
CoinDesk offers various discounts and promotions for eligible groups, including Academia, Developer, Scholarship, Military, and Government officials. It has the right to void promotional and discount codes if the registrant is not the authorized user of the account that qualifies for the promotion or discount. Your registration will be "pending" until applications are reviewed and the appropriate fee has been paid.
There are no special rates for daily attendance, exhibit hall only, etc. If you register using a special rate that is not applicable to you, CoinDesk has the right to charge you for the difference upon notice that CoinDesk does not agree with your selection of the special or discounted rate. All determinations of the discounted/special rates are in the sole discretion of CoinDesk. CoinDesk reserves the right to not provide you with any special rate.
How can I pay for Consensus?
CoinDesk accepts American Express, VISA, MasterCard, Discover, and various cryptocurrencies for payment of registration fees. Payment by invoice can be requested by contacting our registration team at registration @coindesk .com . Please note that invoice requests cannot be processed within one month of the event.
Please note: All registration fees are non-refundable. For any questions regarding attendee registration, please contact us at registration @coindesk .com .
Registration materials will be released only to the individual whose name appears on the badge. Photo identification (driver's license or passport and/or conference registration confirmation (barcode) will be required. Business cards are not an acceptable form of identification.
Badges & Check-In
How do I pick up my badge and wristband at the event?
Please bring your email confirmation or registration QR code to any of the Event Registration Hubs. You must provide a government issued photo ID at registration in order to collect registration materials. You must collect your own individual registration. You cannot pick up passes registered to other individuals, only those assigned to you.

Can I replace my badge and/or wristband?
A US$200 non-refundable fee will be charged to replace a badge or wristband for any reason, including lost or stolen badges and/or wristbands.
Can I authorize someone else to pick up my badge and wristband?
No. Registration materials are released only to the individual named on the badge. You must present a government-issued photo ID.
What's the Badge and Wristband Ethics, Counterfeiting & Theft policy?
Only persons registered for this conference and wearing the proper badge and wristband will be admitted to the conference. For security and safety reasons, badges and wristbands must be worn at all times.
Anyone found to be counterfeiting, copying or sharing conference name badgesand/or wristbands, or using a guest badge and/or wristband when s/he is not eligible for a guest badge and/or wristband, may be subject to sanctions up to and including removal from the conference.
Can I give away my credentials as a prize?
Consensus registration credentials, including badges and wristbands, may not be used as a prize or incentive in any form of promotion, contest, game, or sweepstakes without advance written approval from CoinDesk.
Travel & Venue
When and where is Consensus Hong Kong?
Consensus will take place from Feb 2-3, 2027, at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre and various locations in Hong Kong. Continue to check CoinDesk.com/consensus for our most up-to-date map and information.
Do I need a travel visa to attend Consensus?
Attendees are responsible for checking potential visa requirements and application process in order to attend Consensus. Click here to learn more.
Will there be WiFi at the event?
Yes, complimentary wifi will be available to all registered attendees. The wifi network username and password (if applicable) will be distributed through various materials and signage throughout the event.
Is there a dress code?
Not for the general conference and festival. Specific events may have a dress code and will be noted in the event-specific details.
What if I misplace my personal belongings at the conference?
Personal belongings such as briefcases, backpacks, purses, coats, book bags, laptops, tablets or mobile devices, etc. should not be left unattended in meeting rooms or public areas. These items are the responsibility of their owner and CoinDesk will not be held responsible for their safekeeping should they become lost or stolen. Additionally, any unattended items may be subject to removal by security upon being discovered. If you lose or find an item, please ask about the lost and found at registration.
Sessions & Experiences
Will I get into the session I want?
All on-site events are first-come, first-served. Most sessions will be livestreamed and available on-demand.
What does accessibility look like?
Ramps and handrails are spread throughout our facility. Ramps provide a gradual incline accommodating wheelchairs and scooters. Automatic doors, requiring a simple push of the button, are available at select locations around the facility. All four levels within the facility can be accessed via elevators. Each elevator has a bumper safety system. Major buttons in the elevator are readable in Raised Braille. You can reach all buttons from a wheelchair or scooter. .
Are video and photography allowed?
Video recording and hybrid events (including webcams, webinars, streaming video content) are prohibited inside the HKCEC and at all Consensus events unless you have written permission from the CoinDesk Events Team.
What's the Photo/Video Release policy?
By registering for Consensus, you acknowledge and accept that photographs or video taken of you by CoinDesk or its service providers, may be used for promotional purposes, including but not limited to use in future conference programs, other promotional material and on CoinDesk.com and its service providers.
May I conduct business at the conference?
For the good of the show and the exhibitors supporting the show, the only legitimate place to conduct business during show hours is within a contracted exhibit space on the show floor. Exhibiting companies are encouraged to protect their investment and report any violations to Company management. Attendees observed soliciting business in the aisles or other public spaces, or in another company's booth, may be ejected from the conference and may be banned from future conferences in Company's sole discretion. If you witness this unethical practice during the conference, please immediately report the offense to show management.
What is the privacy policy for Consensus Hong Kong?
Your name, address, phone number, email address are stored in the bar code on your conference badge. If you allow an exhibitor to scan your badge, you will be releasing your information to that exhibitor. You should consider your name badge barcode as an electronic business card, enabling you to easily provide your information to an exhibitor if you choose to do so.
Cancellations & Transfers
Are passes refundable?
No refunds will be provided for cancellations of any kind. All registration fees are non-refundable regardless of circumstance. If you cannot attend in person, your pass grants access to virtual content, or you may transfer it to another person.
How do I transfer my registration?
Transfers are available at no charge by contacting our registration team at registration @coindesk .com by Monday, January 4, 2027. Requests after that date are not guaranteed.
What if I can't attend in person?
Passes are non-refundable. Most sessions will be livestreamed and available on demand. You are also able to transfer your pass to a colleague who can attend by reaching out to our registration team at registration @coindesk .com . Note: Transfer requests must be made by Monday, Jnauary 4, 2027. Requests after that date are not guaranteed.

Safety & Security
What is the bag policy?
Entrances to official event venues will have security checks, potentially including bag search, metal detection devices and screenings. All bags are subject to search. Attendees are encouraged to consider their bag type and use clear bags (mesh, plastic, vinyl, etc.) to expedite entry.
What policies are in place to ensure guest safety?
Safety is a top priority at Consensus. Please prepare for your entry to the event by early arrival. Please note the following precautions and procedures we have in place:
Entrances to official event venues will have security checks, potentially including bag search, metal detection devices and screenings. All bags are subject to search.
Attendees are encouraged to consider their bag type and use clear bags (mesh, plastic, vinyl, etc.) to expedite entry.
All attendees and guests must have a valid event pass or ticket. If you, or a friend, require medical assistance, please call 911 and locate your nearest medical tent or alert a staff member.
Remember, if you see something, say something. Flag down any staff member or public safety official if you notice something that doesn't seem right. Call 911 in the event of an emergency. All event access is subject to capacity and on a first-come, first-served basis.
May I bring my weapon to the conference?
No. Consensus is a private event and maintains a weapons-free policy for all events. Attendees are prohibited from carrying weapons of any kind, including, without limitation, concealed or displayed firearms, and are not permitted to bring weapons onto the conference premises. Attendees' bags may be checked prior to entering a physical, in-person venue by employees, contractors and/or representatives of: (a) Consensus; (b) the venue for the applicable event; and/or (c) CoinDesk clients or Consensus sponsors. CoinDesk reserves the right, in its sole discretion, without refund, deferral or credit to attendee, to deactivate and/or revoke the registration credentials of an attendee if such attendee violates this policy. Attendees must comply with this policy regardless of the terms of any signs posted on the premises or venue of any event.
What is the conduct policy?
CoinDesk reserves the right to exclude any prospective attendee who has engaged in prohibited conduct in prior Consensus conferences from registering or attending Consensus. Furthermore, Company reserves the right to cancel without refund any attendee's registration for Consensus if any attendee engages in such prohibited conduct. Details regarding prohibited conduct are available in the Terms & Conditions of Attendance .
