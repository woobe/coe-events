About Event
​ 🚀 Welcome to Asia’s first physical Ethereum Community Hub — supported by the Ethereum Foundation.
​ Join us as we celebrate a historic milestone
​ Built by builders, for builders , this is your new home base in Hong Kong — to connect, collaborate, and shape the next phase of Ethereum adoption.
​ Backed by the Ethereum Foundation’s Ethereum Everywhere (EE) initiative and operated by SNZ and ETHTAO , the Hub positions Hong Kong as a key gateway connecting Asia with the global Ethereum ecosystem.

​ About Hong Kong Ethereum Community Hub
​ The Ethereum Community Hub in Hong Kong is Asia’s first dedicated hub for the Ethereum ecosystem, supported by the Ethereum Foundation and operated by SNZ and ETHTAO.
​ Located in one of the world’s leading financial centers, the Hub serves as a strategic bridge between East and West — connecting builders, institutions, and innovators across Web2 and Web3.
​ More than just a space, it is a platform for collaboration — bringing together co-working, curated events, and ecosystem initiatives to accelerate real-world adoption of Ethereum and shape the next wave of decentralized innovation.
​ Join us at the Grand Opening and be part of the community shaping Ethereum’s future in Asia.
