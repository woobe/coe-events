About Open Source Hong Kong
Established in 2006, Open Source Hong Kong registered as an organization under Cap. 151 Society Ordinance in Hong Kong, registration number 54617. It is also a Community member of the Open Invention Network and has been an Open Source Initiative Affiliate Member since 2019. To learn more, please read the About section. You may also want to check out our Privacy Policy Statement .
