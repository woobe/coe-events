About Event

​Happy Hour! Bitcoin meetup in Hong Kong.

​Bitcoin Association of Hong Kong takes over Frank's Records the night before Bitcoin Asia opens.

​A record lounge on the 7th floor of 79 Wyndham Street, Central, with a living-room-house-party feel. Vinyl, cocktails, food to order.

​Pay in sats. Load your wallet and come connect.

​Thank you to Tangem Wallet for the support. Sponsors welcome, contact us.

​3 house rules:

  • ​Don't ask how much anyone holds.

  • ​Don't ask how long they've been in.

  • ​Usernames only, unless they offer more.

​See you all soon!
