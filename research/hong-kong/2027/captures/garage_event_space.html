Host Your Next Workshop, Product Launch or Townhall

Garage Society has two fully equipped event spaces on Hong Kong Island, in Causeway Bay and Wan Chai. Both offer seminar, classroom, team building and board meeting layouts, with the AV already set up when you arrive.

Our two venues

Causeway Bay, Tower 535

  • 16/F, 535 Jaffe Road, two minutes from MTR Exit D1.
  • Up to 40 people in 516 sq ft, with full A/V.
  • HK$2,000 per hour, or HK$10,000 for six hours.
  • WiFi, restroom and pantry on request, plus complimentary tea, coffee and water.

Wan Chai, Beverly House

  • 4/F, 93 Lockhart Road, two minutes from MTR Exit A1.
  • 70 seated or 100 standing in 2,248 sq ft.
  • HK$3,200 per hour, with a two hour minimum.
  • 110 inch video wall, 55 inch touchscreen, two HD projectors, and HDMI, Chromecast and Apple TV connectivity.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to hire an event space?

The Causeway Bay event space is HK$2,000 per hour, or HK$10,000 for six hours. The Wan Chai event space is HK$3,200 per hour, with a two hour minimum.

How many people can the event spaces hold?

The Causeway Bay event space in Tower 535 holds up to 40 people. The Wan Chai event space on Lockhart Road seats 70 and holds 100 standing.

What equipment is included?

Both venues include full AV and high-speed WiFi. Wan Chai has a 110 inch video wall, a 55 inch touchscreen and two HD projectors, with HDMI, Chromecast and Apple TV connectivity.

What kinds of events suit the space?

Workshops, seminars, product launches, townhalls, networking evenings and team building, with seminar, classroom, team building and board meeting layouts.

Where are the event spaces?

Our event spaces are at Tower 535 in Causeway Bay and Beverly House in Wan Chai, each about two minutes on foot from an MTR exit.
