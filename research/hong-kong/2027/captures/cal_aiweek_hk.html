About the Event

Where Asia's AI
Future Takes Shape

Hong Kong AI Week 2027 is a landmark gathering that brings together the brightest minds in artificial intelligence from across the globe. Set against the backdrop of one of Asia's most dynamic cities, the event spans multiple venues across Hong Kong, creating an immersive week-long experience.

From cutting-edge research presentations to high-level policy discussions, from startup showcases to enterprise transformation stories — Hong Kong AI Week covers the full spectrum of AI's impact on society and business.
