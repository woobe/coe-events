Registration & Passes

Consensus passes grant access to the Consensus tradeshow, as well as all sessions, events and receptions, in accordance with pass level, and subject to capacity restrictions. Consensus passes are non-exchangable and non-refundable and subject to the Attendance Terms & Conditions.

CoinDesk offers various discounts and promotions for eligible groups, including Academia, Developer, Scholarship, Military, and Government officials. It has the right to void promotional and discount codes if the registrant is not the authorized user of the account that qualifies for the promotion or discount. Your registration will be "pending" until applications are reviewed and the appropriate fee has been paid.

There are no special rates for daily attendance, exhibit hall only, etc. If you register using a special rate that is not applicable to you, CoinDesk has the right to charge you for the difference upon notice that CoinDesk does not agree with your selection of the special or discounted rate. All determinations of the discounted/special rates are in the sole discretion of CoinDesk. CoinDesk reserves the right to not provide you with any special rate.

CoinDesk accepts American Express, VISA, MasterCard, Discover, and various cryptocurrencies for payment of registration fees. Payment by invoice can be requested by contacting our registration team at registration@coindesk.com. Please note that invoice requests cannot be processed within one month of the event.

Please note: All registration fees are non-refundable. For any questions regarding attendee registration, please contact us at registration@coindesk.com.

Registration materials will be released only to the individual whose name appears on the badge. Photo identification (driver's license or passport and/or conference registration confirmation (barcode) will be required. Business cards are not an acceptable form of identification.

Sessions & Experiences

All on-site events are first-come, first-served. Most sessions will be livestreamed and available on-demand.

Ramps and handrails are spread throughout our facility. Ramps provide a gradual incline accommodating wheelchairs and scooters. Automatic doors, requiring a simple push of the button, are available at select locations around the facility. All four levels within the facility can be accessed via elevators. Each elevator has a bumper safety system. Major buttons in the elevator are readable in Raised Braille. You can reach all buttons from a wheelchair or scooter..
