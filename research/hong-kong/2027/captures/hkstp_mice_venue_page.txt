MICE Venues
Hong Kong Science Park MICE Venues
Introduction
Perceivable from miles away by its gleaming gold skin and peculiar shape, Charles K. Kao Auditorium, also known as the Golden Egg, is the iconic event
and conference venue in Hong Kong. As a premier destination for corporate meetings, incentives, conventions, exhibitions, seminars, training sessions,
product launches, forums and business gatherings in Hong Kong, it offers an inspiring setting for organisations seeking a professional venue space.
Complementing the iconic Golden Egg is a diverse selection of meeting rooms, conference halls, function rooms, and boardroom meeting facilities
designed to accommodate events of various sizes and formats. Equipped with high-speed fibre-optic, Wi-Fi connectivity, high-resolution screens,
projectors, and advanced multimedia systems, these versatile event spaces support seamless business events and conferences.
In addition to its contemporary conference and meeting facilities, the open-air Amphitheatre and waterfront outdoor venue are attractive venues
designed for a variety of purposes: team-building events, sports competitions, recreational events and community carnivals.
