HKPUG Year-End Feast 2026 — Python by the Slice
Wed, Dec 23 · 7:15 PM CST
Room Y4702, Yeung Kin Man Academic Building, City University, City University of Hong Kong, 83 Tat Chee Avenue, Kowloon Tong, Kowloon, Hong Kong SAR, Hong Kong, CN
Date: 23 December 2026 (Wednesday)
Time: 19:15–21:30 HKT
Coordinator: Alex Au
Join the Hong Kong Python User Group for three talks, a look back at 2026, and pizza. We’ll run Python in a browser, follow a request as a service scales up from zero, and hear how live translation worked at PyCon HK.
Due to university's admission policy, please fill in the admission form for registration.
Topics
Python in the Browser (Tommy Han, 15 minutes)
Tommy will show how MicroPython runs inside your browser with WebAssembly, using a small interactive page whose input and display are handled in Python.
So You Think This Is the Future of Kubernetes? (Alex Au, 15 minutes)
Our pizza quote endpoint has scaled to zero. Alex will walk through what the first request waits for, using a Python endpoint to compare a conventional Linux container with SpinKube.
Behind PyCon HK’s Live Translation (Iulian Arcus, 30 minutes including Q&A)
Iulian will share how the PyCon HK team got live translation from the room audio to the audience and what they learned from running it at the conference. Then he will deep dive into how to add production model support for novel hardware for tinygrad, looking at CPU and Tenstorrent accelerators.
Venue Info
Room Y4702, 4/F, Yeung Kin Man Academic Building (YEUNG), City University of Hong Kong, Kowloon Tong.

Speaker Bios
Tommy Han is a Hong Kong software engineer who works on native iOS and Android apps. He helps organise HKPUG meetups and served as a co-chair of PyCon Hong Kong 2025. His open-source work spans apps, editor tooling and compilers. At COSCUP, he has spoken about building a SwiftSyntax linter and how a compiler takes PyTorch workloads to specialised hardware. LinkedIn
Alex Au is a cloud platform engineer with experience in backend development, cloud-native infrastructure and production machine learning. He helps organise HKPUG and served as a PyCon Hong Kong co-chair in 2025 and 2026. His previous talks have covered Clean Architecture in Python, OpenTelemetry and Python dependency management with uv. LinkedIn
Iulian Arcus is the founder of nuGPU and an embedded software engineer with a background in software safety and autonomous vehicles. He has worked on on-premises AI inference with Tenstorrent accelerators. He helped bring live translation to PyCon Hong Kong 2026 and gave a lightning talk on the setup. LinkedIn
Audience Prerequisites
The talks are open to anyone curious about Python, WebAssembly or live translation. No prior experience with WebAssembly, Kubernetes or tinygrad is required.
How to Join
To attend, first submit the required Campus Admission Google Form and indicate whether you will stay for pizza at 20:30. Then click Attend on Meetup and confirm that you have submitted the form. Both steps are required. Final campus entry instructions will be announced here once confirmed.

Call for Sprint Projects | HKPUG Sprint Day
Sat, Feb 20, 2027 · 11:59 PM CST
· Online
Online
Sprint Day is taking place at CityU, Kowloon Tong, on 20 February from 10:00 to 18:00 HKT . Room to be confirmed.
Bring a project. Get to know your team.
Lead a small team at HKPUG Sprint Day. Pick a real issue and work through it together. By the end of the day, you’ll know how your teammates ask for help, test a change and follow through on review. You might find people you’d like to work with again.
What to propose
An open-source project with a task useful to Python users or contributors. Any implementation language is welcome. Think Python bindings, a bridge to a JavaScript tool, or better Python examples, docs or tests.
One task someone can pick up in a day, what they could finish, and how they can get started. Add an issue link if you have one.
A lead who can join us in person, answer questions and help review the work. Let us know how many people you can support.
Submit by 23:59 HKT on Saturday, 20 February 2027. Send your proposal early if you’d like it included in the daytime schedule.
