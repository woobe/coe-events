Registration

  1. Public Ballot is on a ballot basis while Disability Entry is on a first-come, first-served basis.
  2. Registrations of Priority Entry will only be confirmed after relevant results are verified by the Hong Kong, China Association of Athletics Affiliates Limited (“The Organiser”).
  3. Registrations of Disability Entry will only be confirmed with verification of a valid Registration Card for People with Disabilities issued by the HKSAR Government / Government of applicant’s country / region.
  4. The Organiser reserves the right to close entry before the deadline without prior notice.

Entry Requirements

  1. Marathon participants must be 20 years old or above as of 2027 (Count by Year of Birth).
  2. Applicants MUST have successfully completed at least one race (10km or longer distance) in the Standard Chartered Hong Kong Marathon or any local / overseas races between 1 January 2025 and 31 August 2026. Applicants are also required to provide factually the Finishing Time.
  3. Estimated Finishing Time must be submitted upon registration, which will be taken into consideration when the Organiser allocates the race category and start time to participants.

E-Certificates and Finisher Medal

E-Certificate of Achievements, Progress Award and Finisher Medal will be issued to runners who finish the race within the designated time limit. Details are as follows:

E-Certificate of Achievements and Finisher Medal
Participants who finish the race within the designated time limit will be entitled an e-Certificate of Achievements and Finisher Medal.

E-Certificate of Progress Award
Participants who finish with a faster Net Time comparing with the same race completed in Standard Chartered Hong Kong Marathon 2026 will be entitled an e-Certificate of Progress Award.
