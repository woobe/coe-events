About This Space
Industrial-Chic Workspace and Maker Hub on Smithfield Road Kennedy Town
Tucked into one of Hong Kong's most sought-after waterfront neighborhoods on Smithfield Road, The Hive Kennedy Town blends industrial-chic design with a maker-friendly hub built for creatives, freelancers, and small businesses looking for a functional, community-driven space in K-Town. Sunlight pours through floor-to-ceiling windows, illuminating warm timber finishes and open collaborative lounges that instantly inspire productivity. Beyond workspace options, our iconic garden terrace offers an unbeatable spot for networking coffees and evening events. Step inside today to grab a tour, meet our vibrant local community, and see where Kennedy Town's best ideas come to life.
