Welcome to the official Global AI Chapter page of Hong Kong in Hong Kong. We are a group of AI enthusiasts with a passion for AI, a desire to learn and to share knowledge. We look forward to meeting you at our next event.
Global AI Hong Kong is a dynamic community dedicated to exploring, learning, and sharing knowledge about artificial intelligence. Based in one of the most vibrant cities in the world, our chapter brings together individuals from all walks of life—AI practitioners, researchers, students, and enthusiasts—who share a common passion for innovation and technology.
We aim to foster a collaborative environment where ideas flourish, connections are made, and members grow both personally and professionally. From hosting hands-on workshops to organizing inspiring talks by industry leaders, our goal is to empower the AI community in Hong Kong and beyond.
Whether you're an experienced developer, a curious student, or someone just beginning their AI journey, we welcome you to join our events and be a part of this exciting journey. Together, we’re shaping the future of AI.
