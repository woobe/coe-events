Event Venue FAqs
Do you accommodate professional video or photo shoots?

Absolutely. Our uniquely curated design and stunning harbour views make Banyan a premier choice for commercial productions and professional photography.

Can I record a podcast at Banyan Workspace?

Yes, Banyan Workspace is perfect for podcast recording. We even host our own! We offer bespoke packages in collaboration with the experts at Munday Studios to ensure your recording is seamless and professional.

What additional services do you offer to enhance my event?

We provide a range of hospitable support services to make your experience effortless, including catering, professional photography, event management and expert workshop facilitation.

What amenities are included in the venue rental?

Your rental includes access to integrated audio-visual tech, versatile seating configurations, and our inviting lounge area. Additional premier services, such as specialised tech hire, are available upon request.

Are there any additional fees I should be aware of?

A security deposit is standard for most bookings to ensure the integrity of our space; this is fully refundable following a successful, damage-free event. Staff costs & cleaning charges may apply to your project depending on date and time of the booking.

Can I arrange my own catering?

You are welcome to bring your own catering, or we can introduce you to our preferred partners who specialise in conscious and local flavours.

Is the space accessible outside of standard business hours?

Yes, we offer 24/7 access for events, giving you the flexibility to host evening mixers or early-morning workshops at your convenience.

Can I tour the space before booking?

We highly encourage a visit! Our hospitable community team in Quarry Bay is happy to walk you through our signature spaces and help you visualise your layout.
