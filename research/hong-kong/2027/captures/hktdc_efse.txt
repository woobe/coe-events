Exhibitor
Chinese Mainland
July Lee
General Manager
Huizhou Jiayao Smart Technology Co., LTD
We are a professional manufacturer of cosmetic mirrors, exporting primarily to Europe, the United States, Japan and South Korea.
At this fair, we are showcasing classic LED cosmetic mirrors alongside our new AI-driven smart makeup mirrors featuring large-model AI technology, skin analysis and voice interaction. Within the span of 3 days, I identified potential buyers from Germany, Italy and Japan, plus a Malaysian buyer who flew in after seeing fair videos on social media. My target is to secure about 10 new overseas buyers and around US$200,000 in potential orders.
Hong Kong serves as a vital gateway between Chinese Mainland and global markets, attracting higher-quality international clients and enabling Chinese firms like ours to go global efficiently. The HKTDC platform delivers direct, high-value connections amid thousands of exhibitors, while this fair lets us debut AI innovations, gather buyer feedback and convert it into scalable business.
Buyer
Brazil
Atila Yan Mesquita
Product Development Analyst
Mondial
Mondial is a major electronics company from Brazil that offers a wide range of products, including small appliances and audio‑visual items. I am mainly sourcing products for our perimeter lights, party boxes, mini speakers and microphones at this fair.
Located in Hong Kong, this fair is one of the best places to meet new electronics suppliers and access high‑quality Chinese electronics efficiently, especially from the Chinese Mainland, such as manufacturers in Guangzhou. Not only did I successfully identify a headphone product from a Hong Kong supplier on the first day, but I also saw great potential to expand other collaborations with a budget of US$3 billion to relevant product categories throughout the fair.
With the help of Scan2Match, I was able to gather detailed information of potential suppliers conveniently, giving me a pleasant and effective experience. I would love to attend this fair again.
Exhibitor
Australia
Michael Lin
General Manager
Carmate Electronics Pty Ltd
Carmate Electronics Pty Ltd focuses on high-quality automotive electronics, such as TPMS for commercial vehicle fleets, patented actuators and remote handle locking systems.
The fair has proven highly effective for excellent business connections. Today alone, we've already attracted serious buyers from Brazil, Mexico, Belgium, and Indonesia. Our discussions with them indicate US$200,000 in potential transactions, with estimates reaching US$400,000-500,000 through repeat orders and follow-ups.
Hong Kong’s unique position serves as a crucial bridge between overseas firms and Chinese Mainland companies. With all the favourable conditions provided, we plan to return next year for sure.
Buyer
United Arab Emirates
Alex Tchablakian
CEO
Letstango
Letstango is a United Arab Emirates-based e-commerce marketplace that has been operating for around 13 years. We work with more than 1,000 vendors and offer a broad range of products, primarily in electronics, accessories and appliances, while continuing to expand into other categories.
I came to the fair mainly to identify new vendors and explore products that would be suitable for our market, especially accessories, mobile-related products and artificial intelligence-enabled consumer products. So far, the response has been very encouraging. HKTDC has arranged 10 businss matching meetings for us, and beyond that I have also identified around 10 additional exhibitors from the Chinese Mainland that are of strong interest.
What makes Hong Kong particularly valuable is its role as a highly efficient gateway for connecting international buyers with suppliers and manufacturers. For buyers like us, being here in person makes a real difference. It helps overcome communication barriers, speeds up decision-making and creates direct access to factories, products and service providers all in one place.
The fair has also opened up opportunities beyond sourcing. I have met service providers that could potentially support our logistics and even help us explore setting up a presence in Hong Kong to facilitate regional operations. That was not something I had originally planned, so it really shows the broader value of the platform.
We already have a sourcing budget of around US$1 million. Compared with my last visit around 10 years ago, the fair today feels much larger, more dynamic and far more diverse. I was also impressed by the RoboPark zone, which shows how quickly innovation is evolving.Overall, I think the fair is a very effective platform for business expansion, new partnerships and regional market development.

Congress Approves Mandatory Requirements for Lithium-Ion Batteries Used in Micromobility Products
Nov 2026
The U.S. Senate on 19 November unanimously approved without amendment bi-partisan legislation which had been previously approved by the House of Representatives that instructs the CPSC to adopt mandatory safety requirements for rechargeable lithium-ion batteries and electrical systems used in e-bikes, e-scooters and other micromobility devices. The legislation must still be signed by President Trump to enter into force.
Relocation and Sourcing in Thailand’s Manufacturing Ambitions
Nov 2026
Thailand is rapidly emerging as one of the ASEAN region’s AI and data centre hubs, a development supported by the strong growth of cloud computing, AI applications and digital infrastructure investment. The market is expected to expand significantly over the coming years, creating opportunities for Hong Kong-based providers of technology, infrastructure and professional services. Key demand drivers of data centre services include hyperscalers, AI-focused cloud providers, large enterprises and capacity sub-leasing operators. Industry players point out that demand exceeds available supply, so immediate capacity availability and service reliability have become key competitive factors, creating opportunities for new market entrants and service providers.
VIETNAM: Imports of Used Digital Technology Devices Banned from January 2027
Nov 2026
Vietnam will prohibit imports of various used digital technology and electronic products categories from 1 January 2027 under Circular No. 63/2026, issued by the Ministry of Science and Technology. The regulation replaces the previous list introduced in 2018 and aims to streamline the legal framework, protect consumers and strengthen the domestic digital technology market. The restrictions are also intended to mitigate information security, fire and explosion risks associated with degraded or untraceable equipment.
Amended Requirements for Consumer Products Using Button Cell/Coin Batteries
Nov 2026
The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission has issued a final rule establishing a mandatory safety standard for button cell or coin batteries that includes requirements for consumer goods using such batteries. The CPSC has also issued a separate final rule that requires warning labels on the packaging of such batteries.
