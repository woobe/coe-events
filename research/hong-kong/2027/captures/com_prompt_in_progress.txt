01 // Why we exist // 我們為何存在
Progress is an unfinished conversation.
A prompt is not a command. It is an invitation to iterate, question, and imagine what comes next.
Think back to your first prompt. It may have taken seconds, or minutes of fumbling. Today, that interaction is more intentional: an iterative exchange between human purpose and machine capability. That is what Prompt in Progress represents.
We are builders, creators, and innovators across Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area who refuse to sit on the sidelines of this technological shift. We are here to spark change in our region, together.
We embrace the uncertain in-between : the place where ideas are rough, people are learning, and real progress begins. You do not need all the answers to belong here.
We are not simply event organisers. Events are where connections begin; education deepens understanding; community sustains momentum. Each strengthens the next.
我們擁抱未完成的狀態，也接納探索中的不確定。你不需要知道所有答案，才有資格參與。進步來自一起提問、實踐，並持續向前。

06 // From the actuator // 從發起人出發
A community built by showing up.
“Hopefully, you will find a community and friends within this ecosystem.” Prompt in Progress grew out of work that started in 2024, when Timothy Chau began looking for Hong Kong's technical builders. Rejections became introductions, introductions became monthly rooms, and in 2026 those rooms became Prompt in Progress: one home for technical and non-technical builders, with a wider calling — to make AI progress more human and more inclusive.
Read Timothy's full story When I first started building an AI community in Hong Kong in 2024, I had no network, no resources, and no backing. What I had was sheer dedication and the belief that our city needed a technical community, and that builders were already here.
I went to conferences, waited until panels finished, and introduced what I was building to speakers in the hope they would host us. There were many rejections and cold shoulders. But the community grew quickly. We met friends who now call it home, and worked with partners I never imagined possible.
I kept our monthly meetups open and ran them on a pro-bono basis because events are not merely a value exchange. We can genuinely learn from each other and explore AI together. I want people to leave inspired, not feeling as though they attended just another event.
We are builders of people, not event organisers. We are about stories and feelings, not a name on a marketing list or a corporate KPI. After hosting these events, I felt a bigger calling: we can build an ecosystem that supports one another.
I do not know exactly what this ecosystem will become, which is why it is “in progress.” But if we build intentionally, just as we prompt with intention, we can create great things and bring our ambitions into reality.
We have a duty to be inclusive. Beyond technical meetups, we have a calling as humans to help others understand this technology and bridge the gap. Here, we embrace imperfection, confusion, and the in-between. Everything we are witnessing is new and disruptive, but we can navigate it together.
我們不只是籌辦活動，而是在培育人與關係。即使未來仍未清晰，只要有意識地共同建設，我們便能跨越技術與人的距離，一起找到方向。
Timothy Chau // Actuator
