​ ​活動簡介/ ​​About Event
​ 香港Web3嘉年華是由萬向區塊鏈實驗室與HashKey Group聯合推出的Web3活動品牌，由W3ME承辦。作為亞洲領先的Web3大會，活動自2023年起於每年4月在香港舉辦，過去四屆，嘉年華累計吸引超過500個項目參展，邀請逾1600位業界領袖深度交流，現場觀眾總數突破15萬人次，並配套舉辦600餘場周邊活動。香港特別行政區政府財政司司長陳茂波、以太坊聯合創始人Vitalik Buterin、ARK Invest CEO兼CIO Cathie Wood等重量級嘉賓，曾先後登上嘉年華舞臺，從政策、技術與投資等多元視角，分享對行業發展的前瞻觀察。
​ Hong Kong Web3 Festival is Asia’s premier crypto event co-hosted by Wanxiang Blockchain Labs and HashKey Group and organized by W3ME. This premier global Web3 gathering, reoccurring every April since 2023, has accumulated over 500 exhibiting companies, 1,600+speakers, 150,000+ attendees and more than 600 side events over the previous four editions. Distinguished guests including Paul Chan, Financial Secretary of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; Vitalik Buterin, Co-founder, Ethereum and Cathie Wood, CEO and CIO of ARK Invest, have graced the stage, sharing forward‑looking insights on industry development from diverse perspectives spanning policy, technology, and investment.
​ 2027香港Web3嘉年華將於2027年4月19日-21日在香港會議展覽中心舉辦， 預計將吸引超過50,000現場觀眾，超過300位極具全球影響力的演講嘉賓，以及超過150家熱門Web3項目、社區及媒體現場參展。
​ Hong Kong Web3 Festival 2027 will take place on 19-21 Apr. at HKCEC. This three-day event is expecting over 50,000 visitors, more than 300 influential speaker, 150 promising Web3 projects, communities and media from all over the world.
​ *本活動只供18周歲（含）或以上的業內人士參觀參展
​ * This event is open to visitors and exhibitors aged 18 and above.
