      Description

      AI-ESS-16/OSAKA

      AI-ESS 2027 Osaka Forum on AI-Driven Education and Social Symbiosis (AI-ESS-16)

      September 13–17, 2027 | Osaka, Japan

      At a Glance

      • Conference: AI-ESS 2027 Osaka Forum on AI-Driven Education and Social Symbiosis (AI-ESS-16)
      • Dates and city: September 13–17, 2027, Osaka, Japan (in-person conference)
      • Organizer: Applied and Advanced Science Exchange (AASE)
      • Scope: artificial intelligence in education, the social sciences and the natural sciences
      • Abstract / full paper submission deadline: July 12, 2027
      • Early Bird registration deadline: June 21, 2027
      • Registration deadline: August 13, 2027
      • Registration fees: Early Bird USD 350 · Regular USD 420 · AASE Member USD 380 · Student USD 380 · Listener USD 220
      • Publication: accepted abstracts and full papers appear in the AASE e-proceedings with ISBN; selected full papers are recommended to Scopus-indexed partner journals
      • Submit your paper: Online submission form

      We warmly welcome scholars, researchers, and professionals worldwide to join the AI-ESS 2027 Osaka Forum on AI-Driven Education and Social Symbiosis (AI-ESS-16), held from September 13 to 17, 2027, in the historic commercial capital of Osaka. This global academic event gathers thought leaders and interdisciplinary researchers to exchange knowledge, explore the rapid evolution of Artificial Intelligence, and tackle the complex challenges it presents across education and social structures.

      Conference Objectives

      AI-ESS-16 serves as a premier platform for scholars and experts to engage in meaningful dialogue, fostering interdisciplinary collaboration between AI technology and human-centric social research. We encourage participants to actively engage in discussions, providing insights that will enhance collective understanding of how AI is reshaping the future of our society and education systems.

      Keynote Speakers: By Invitation and Committee Review

      Keynote speakers at AASE conferences are primarily invited by the AASE Organizing Committee. We also welcome proposals from established senior scholars (Assistant Professor level or above, with a substantial publication record) whose work closely matches the conference theme. Submitting a proposal does not guarantee a keynote appointment: every proposal is rigorously reviewed by the AASE Committee, and keynote slots are limited.

      Submit a Keynote Proposal (Subject to Committee Review)
      Submit your proposed title, abstract and speaker bio. Eligibility and review criteria are listed on the application page.

      Privileges for Confirmed Keynote Speakers

      The privileges below apply only to keynote speakers confirmed by the AASE Committee.

      • Honorary Certificate & Commemorative Plaque — An official Keynote Speaker Certificate of Appreciation and an engraved commemorative plaque, presented by the AASE Committee in recognition of your scholarly contribution.
      • Fast-Track Journal Publication — Your paper or extended manuscript receives prioritized peer review and an expedited publication pathway in AASE partner journals.
      • Lifetime Membership & Registration Discounts — Complimentary Lifetime Membership to AASE, complete with an official Honorary Lifetime Member Certificate, granting exclusive discounted registration rates for all future AASE conferences and academic events.

      Keynote slots are limited per conference and reviewed on a rolling basis — early submissions receive priority consideration.

      Why Participate?

      Your participation is vital for promoting interdisciplinary innovation and collaboration, which are key to solving global challenges. Join us in Osaka, where new ideas will be explored and effective solutions developed to advance education and social sciences in the AI era. Together, we can make a lasting impact in these fields and contribute to their evolution on a global scale.

      Global Research Impact

      Maximizing Reach and Visibility for Scholarly Excellence

      AASE is committed to maximizing the reach and visibility of scholarly work submitted through our conference. Accepted papers undergo rigorous peer review and are published through a network of internationally recognized indexing platforms and partner journals.

      Strategic Publication & Indexing Pathways:

      • Global Database Indexing: All accepted submissions (abstract-only and full papers) are published in our ISBN-assigned digital proceedings, which are submitted for indexing in CiteSeerX, Google Scholar, Thomson Reuters, SSRN, and ResearchGate, ensuring broad discoverability across academic and professional communities worldwide.

      • Fast-Track Journal Publication: Outstanding papers are invited for fast-track publication in our prestigious partner journals, providing a streamlined path to high-impact recognition:

        • Business Systems Research (BSR)

        • International Journal for Quality Research (IJQR)

        • Proceedings on Engineering Sciences (PES)

        • Journal of Management and Business Education (JMBE)

        • Select titles from Emerald Publishing — a leading global academic publisher recognized for excellence in management, business, and social sciences research.

      • Open Access (DOAJ Verified): AASE strongly supports the open science movement. Published works are made freely available to readers worldwide in accordance with open access principles. Our partner journals are listed in the Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ), affirming our commitment to transparency and broad knowledge dissemination.


      Peer Review and Research Integrity

      All submissions to AI-ESS-16 go through the following review process:

      • Initial screening: The Secretariat checks each submission for scope, completeness and formatting, and screens it for similarity to previously published work.

      • Double-blind peer review: Each submission is reviewed by members of the AASE review committee with expertise in the relevant field. Authors and reviewers do not know each other’s identities.

      • Decision and feedback: Submissions are reviewed on a rolling basis, and decisions are typically sent within 2–3 weeks of submission. Reviewer comments are shared with the author where applicable.

      For details, please see our Review & Publication Ethics policy.

      Participation and Presentation Protocols

      Delegate Registration (Non-presenting Participants)

      Individuals attending as listeners are exempt from the submission and review process. Delegates may proceed directly to the registration portal for immediate enrollment and secure payment processing. No abstract or manuscript submission is required for this category.

      Call for Contributions (Presenting Authors)

      Authors seeking to present their research must first undergo a rigorous editorial evaluation. Prospective presenters are required to submit one of the following for peer review:

      • Abstract (published in the ISBN-assigned digital proceedings)

      • Full Research Paper (published in the ISBN-assigned digital proceedings; outstanding papers may be invited for fast-track journal publication)

      • Academic Poster (for interactive sessions)

      Post-Acceptance Procedures

      Upon official notification of acceptance following the review process, authors will be provided with comprehensive instructions to finalize their registration and fee settlement.

      Submission Format Guidelines


      Presentation Formats

      • Oral Presentations: Authors will be allocated 15 minutes, including 5 minutes for Q&A. Presentations will be grouped by related topics into thematic sessions.

      • Poster Presentations: Authors may present interactive posters in a designated session. Handouts may be provided for interested attendees.

      For detailed guidance on preparing and delivering your oral or poster presentation, please see our Presentation Guidelines.

      Experience Innovation in Japan's Commercial Heartland

      As Japan's historic commercial capital and a fast-growing hub for robotics, digital media, and health-tech research, Osaka provides a dynamic setting for academic inquiry. Participants can witness a city where centuries-old merchant traditions sit alongside cutting-edge innovation from institutions such as Osaka University and the wider Kansai research corridor. Swissôtel Nankai Osaka, located at Swissôtel Nankai Osaka on Japan Place, offers a modern and welcoming environment between the mountains and the sea.

      Osaka serves as a "living laboratory" where scholars can observe the real-world impact of AI on multilingual classrooms, immigration and settlement services, and climate-resilient urban planning. Moreover, Japan's early and sustained national investment in AI research, paired with an unusually active public conversation on responsible and equitable deployment, serves as a model for how AI can be integrated into social symbiosis without eroding public trust. This makes the city more than just a venue; it is a tangible benchmark for the very "Social Symbiosis" that AI-ESS-16 aims to explore and define for the global academic community.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      When and where is AI-ESS-16 held?
      AI-ESS 2027 Osaka Forum on AI-Driven Education and Social Symbiosis (AI-ESS-16) takes place on September 13–17, 2027 at Swissôtel Nankai Osaka, Osaka, Japan. It is an in-person conference organized by Applied and Advanced Science Exchange (AASE).

      What is the submission deadline?
      Abstracts and full papers are due by July 12, 2027. Review is rolling; decisions are typically sent within 2–3 weeks of submission. Presenting authors register and pay only after acceptance.

      How much is registration?
      Early Bird USD 350 (until June 21, 2027), Regular USD 420, AASE Member USD 380, Student USD 380 (proof of student status required), Listener USD 220. The registration deadline is August 13, 2027. Each author attending in person registers separately.

      Can I present online?
      No. AI-ESS-16 is in person only. Presentations can be oral or A0 poster.

      Will my paper be published?
      Yes. All accepted abstracts and full papers are published in the AASE e-proceedings with ISBN, and selected full papers are recommended to partner journals.

      Can I get an invitation letter?
      Yes. Invitation letters, receipts and certificates are issued to the registered attendee after acceptance and payment.
