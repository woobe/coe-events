  87| With a capacity of 70 people in a theatre setup or 54 in a classroom layout, Function Room 4-3 is designed for medium-sized events such as seminars and workshops.
  88| It features cutting-edge audio-visual equipment, including a projector, screen, speakers, and wireless microphones, to enhance your presentations. The room is adjacent to a stylish break-out area that encourages networking and informal discussions. Additionally, it includes a glass writing board, flip chart, and plenty of power sockets to support all your tech requirements.
