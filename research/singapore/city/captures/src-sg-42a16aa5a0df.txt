 347| Host rules
 348| Host responsibilities:
 349| - No overcrowding the space beyond the advised capacity
 350| - No alcohol, no gambling
 351| - No water games
 352| - No confetti, glitter, sparklers, party poppers, snow sprays, blowing soap bubbles
 353| - Please leave the space on time to avoid a late check out fee of $30 per 15 minutes
 354| - Please return the space in good and clean condition to ensure the full refund of your security and cleaning deposit
 355| - All equipment (mics, remote controls, etc) and chairs must be returned to the original storage areas after use.
 356| - All spills must be attended to promptly, using the blue absorbent pads and wet wipes provided in the pantry.
 357| - To-go coffee or tea boxes should be emptied off all fluids (into the toilet sink) before being thrown into the bin.
 358| - Bottles of unfinished drinks and water should be emptied off all fluids (into the toilet sink) before being thrown into the bin.
 359| - All cardboard boxes should be flattened and neatly placed next to the Green Bins outside the door
 360| - All trash bags within the venue should be tied up and placed into the Green Bins outside the door after your event.
 361| Caterer responsibilities:
 362| -Buffet caterers must provide their own tables (hot food warmers with metal trays should not be placed on our tables)
 363| - Caterers must provide their own bins and dispose their trash at the bin centre (not in our Green Bins outside the door)
 364| Vendor responsibilities:
 365| - Art & Craft Vendors must be responsible for any debris from materials generated from your workshop or activities. Please ensure everything is swept up and properly disposed.
 366| -Tables must be covered and protected if there are any activities that involve gluing, cutting or anything that may stain/damage the tables.
 367| - Backdrop vendors: all materials brought in must be cleared out from the venue and properly disposed at the bin centre
 368| For balloon vendors: Do not spray oil on the balloons. No pasting on the walls or floor. Balloons should be popped (within the space), swept up and properly disposed.

 422| FAQ
 423| Does the venue have lift access?
 424| Yes, there is one single commercial lift that reaches the 2nd floor where the venue is located. All other lifts in the building are for the residential floors above and do not serve the 2nd floor.
 425| Are power sockets and extensions provided?
 426| Yes, power sockets and extensions are included.
 427| Is a microwave provided?
 428| No. There is no microwave provided at the venue.
 429| What decoration restrictions apply at the venue?
 430| The venue does not allow piñatas, confetti, glitter, snow sprays or sparklers. Pasting is not allowed on furniture, the ceiling or floor, but decorations may be attached to white walls using Blu Tack, washing tape or painter’s tape.
 431| Are tables provided at the venue?
 432| Yes, 12 foldable tables are included, along with 54 folding chairs, a projector with screen, two wireless microphones with sound system, a 6m-wide whiteboard wall with markers, power sockets and extensions, an en-suite toilet, and a pantry counter with fridge and electric kettle.
