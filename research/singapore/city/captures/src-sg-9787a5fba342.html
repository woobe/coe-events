About this space

The interior is modern and neutral, so you can dress it up for a themed celebration or keep it simple for a more professional setup. You can arrange it theatre-style, classroom-style, or however makes the most sense for your group.

We’ve got flexible seating, solid AV equipment, a sound system, wireless mics, and fast Wi-Fi, so presentations and events run smoothly. There’s a small reception area where guests can check in or mingle, plus a pantry that helps with light catering.
