Frequently Asked Questions About Meeting Room Rentals

How can I book a meeting room quickly?

You don’t need a membership with us. Download JustCo App to find, book and unlock meeting rooms near you immediately. Browse locations, choose your preferred room and time, and receive confirmation in just a few taps.

How much does it cost to rent a meeting room in Singapore?

JustCo has meeting rooms for hire across prime CBD locations in Singapore, available from as little as 30 minutes, with rates varying by room size, address and duration. There are no long-term commitments required, so you only pay for the time you need. Browse available locations in Singapore online or contact our team for current pricing at your preferred centre.

Can I book a meeting room by the hour?

Yes. All JustCo meeting rooms in Singapore are bookable from as little as 30 minutes, with instant confirmation online. Whether you need a private meeting room for a quick hourly client meeting or a half-day office space for a team session, flexible rates mean you are never paying for more than you use.

What is included in a meeting room rental?

Every professional meeting room rental at JustCo includes high-speed Wi-Fi, AV equipment, presentation screens, comfortable seating and on-site support. Corporate meeting rooms and larger rooms also include video conferencing tools and full AV setups. Optional catering packages are available through trusted partners for longer sessions.

What size meeting rooms are available?

JustCo offers meeting spaces for rent across a full range of sizes, from small rooms for 1 to 3 people through to mid-sized rooms for 4 to 8 people and larger corporate meeting rooms for groups of up to 20. For bigger gatherings, our event spaces in Singapore accommodate groups of 20 to 80 or more. Whatever your headcount, there is a workspace solution that fits.

Can I book a private meeting room in Singapore?

Yes. Private meeting rooms in Singapore are available across JustCo’s network of centres for professionals who need a confidential, enclosed environment for client meetings, interviews, team discussions or presentations. Availability varies by location, so browse online or speak with the team to find the right room near you.
