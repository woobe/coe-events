  87| Overview
  88| Join us in person for the 3rd International Conference on AI and Data Science to explore the latest tech!
  89| Get ready to dive into the world of artificial intelligence and data science at this awesome in-person event! Connect with experts, share ideas, and explore the latest trends in AI and data. Don't miss out on the chance to learn, network, and have a great time with fellow enthusiasts!
  90| Workshop Details:
  91| Does It Actually Work?
  92| Evaluating, Red-Teaming & Governing AI Agents Before They Reach Production
  93| A high-impact practical workshop on testing and securing AI agents before they transition from impressive prototypes to autonomous real-world systems.
  94| Leave With a Practical AI-Agent Readiness Framework
  95| Agent evaluation criteria
  96| Common failure modes
  97| Red-team methodology
  98| Tool & permission controls
  99| Human-approval strategies
 100| Monitoring & observability principles
 101| Responsible AI & governance considerations
 102| Pre-production readiness framework
 103| When Should Your AI Say No?
 104| Designing AI Agents That Know Where to Stop
 105| A hands-on, non-technical workshop on deciding, in advance, what an AI agent should be trusted to do on its own, and designing the moment it hands control back to a human.
 106| Participants Will Leave With
 107| A practical mental model (Act / Ask / Escalate / Refuse) to apply immediately to any agent project
 108| Direct experience mapping and classifying an agent's decision points
 109| A one-page decision policy, drafted during the workshop, adaptable to your own use case
 110| A clearer sense of where design-time judgment calls end, and technical evaluation begins
