  84| About this space
  85| A carpeted rectangular room in neutral tones with recessed ceiling lights and side windows that supply natural daylight. The seating is arranged around an open central space.
  86| Room features: A ceiling-mounted projector and a wall-length whiteboard are provided. A freestanding flipchart is supplied and the room is air-conditioned.
  87| Best for: Presentations, instructor-led training and facilitated meetings that use a single focal presenter and group discussion.
  88| What sets it apart: An integrated PA system supplies clear amplified audio suitable for speeches and seminars.
