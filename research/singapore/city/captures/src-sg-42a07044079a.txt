  87| About this space
  88| A bright, minimal event venue organised around an open-plan layout that can be rearranged for different setups.
  89| Design: The interior pairs pale timber floors and white walls with low-profile lounge seating and a separate table area under recessed ceiling lights, creating a clean, uncluttered look.
  90| Features: A PA system and wall-mounted TV are provided, and the venue permits own music and external DJs. The room includes a karaoke setup, a pool table, board games, modular sofas and movable tables, plus Wi‑Fi and air conditioning.
  91| Ideal for: Friend groups and small social circles looking for a relaxed, flexible space suitable for mixed seating and standing formats.
  92| What sets it apart: A self-contained hire that includes a mini pantry, two fridges and a private toilet to support on-site food prep and storage.
  93| What guests say: The space is roomy, spotless and bright, and it benefits from attentive, helpful staff and a comfortable layout that supports games and group activities.
