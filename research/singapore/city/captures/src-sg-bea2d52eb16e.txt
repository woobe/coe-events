  86| About this space
  87| A contemporary event venue geared towards social entertainment, combining modern finishes with adaptable, practical space for hosted gatherings.
  88| Design: The interior presents a clean, modern aesthetic with pale tiled floors, white walls and linear recessed lighting. The space pairs dark timber accents and simple wooden furniture to produce a functional, uncluttered layout.
  89| Features: The venue includes a PA system, karaoke setup, projector and screen, Wi‑Fi and air conditioning. The venue provides movable tables and chairs, a small fridge, and a designated buffet/service area and diaper-changing space. The venue also houses games equipment such as a pool table, darts and a Nintendo Switch, and it has a step-free guest entrance.
  90| Ideal for: Families and mixed-age groups seeking a relaxed, social gathering that mixes seated dining with active, in-house entertainment.
  91| What sets it apart: A two-storey layout that separates social seating from dedicated play and entertainment areas.
  92| What guests say: The venue is spacious, clean and well maintained and provides multiple in-house entertainment options that suit different ages. The venue's staff are accommodating and helpful during deliveries and tight turnarounds.

 324| FAQ
 325| Does the venue have a lift for pram access?
 326| No, the venue does not have a lift. The space is double-storey, and the staircase has handrails.
 327| Are decoration services included at the venue?
 328| No, decorations are not included. Guests are welcome to bring items to decorate the space according to their requirements.
 329| Are chairs included with the venue hire?
 330| The venue has eight tables, 30 indoor chairs, one long table and six outdoor chairs, plus over 50 spare chairs at the back that can be used.
 331| Are 15-minute Kompang performances permitted inside the venue?
 332| Yes, 15-minute Kompang performances are permitted. The door must remain closed throughout the event.
 333| Are microphones provided at the venue?
 334| Yes, the venue is equipped with a 120-inch projector screen, sound system and wireless microphones.
