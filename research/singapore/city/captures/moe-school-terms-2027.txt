  87| 1. The school year for 2027 for all MOE primary schools (including MOE Kindergartens) and secondary schools will start on Monday, 4 January 2027 and end on Friday, 19 November 2027
  88| School Calendar 2027
  89| MOE KINDERGARTEN (MK), PRIMARY & SECONDARY
  90| Semester I
  91| Term I
  92| Mon 4 Jan 1 to Fri 12 Mar
  93| Term II
  94| Mon 22 Mar to Fri 28 May
  95| Semester II
  96| Term III
  97| Mon 28 Jun to Fri 3 Sep
  98| Term IV
  99| Mon 13 Sep to Fri 19 Nov 2
 100| 1 Primary 1 and Kindergarten 1 will start school on Monday, 4 January 2027. Primary 2 to 6 and Kindergarten 2 will start school on Tuesday, 5 January 2027.
 101| 2 The last day of the final school term for schools which will be used as venues for the Singapore-Cambridge Secondary Education (SEC) written examinations, will be Friday, 29 October 2027.
