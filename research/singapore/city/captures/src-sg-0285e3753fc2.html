A Destination Designed for Exceptional Events​

Resorts World Sentosa is home to world-class venues, premium stays, curated dining and immersive experiences, all within one integrated resort.

Exclusive Perks for Delegates

MICE delegates and attendees will enjoy discounts on attraction tickets and when dining at selected restaurants across the resort.

An Iconic Destination Minutes from the City

Set on Sentosa Island, just 25 minutes from Singapore Changi Airport and 15 minutes from Singapore’s city centre, Resorts World Sentosa offers the convenience organisers value with destination appeal delegates remember.

Curated Culinary Experience at Scale

Curate event menus from over 50 dining concepts across the resort, including Michelin-starred dishes, celebrity-chef selections, halal-certified options and more.​

Transformative Event Spaces

Our newly renovated ballrooms and event spaces are designed for dynamic formats, from large-scale plenary sessions and exhibitions to immersive brand showcases and gala dinners.​

A Fully Integrated Resort Destination

Our event venues, luxury hotels, restaurants and attractions are walkable within the resort, enabling efficient planning, smoother logistics, and a seamless delegate journeys.​

Sustainable Amenities

Our commitment to sustainable events

We have established robust policies to ensure every event we organise minimises environmental impact, promotes social responsibility, and delivers exceptional experiences.

https://gentingsingapore.com/#!/en/company/governance/corporate-policies

Sustainable Supply Chain

From reusable chinaware to recyclable, biodegradable, and FSC-certified materials, our event offerings are thoughtfully chosen to reduce waste and protect the planet. 
/en/about/sustainability/lower-emissions#ResponsibleSourcing

Supporting Local

We prioritise locally sourced ingredients, with over 80% of our purchasing portfolio sourced from local companies, such as locally grown mushrooms, vegetables, and eggs. 
/en/about/sustainability/lower-emissions#ResponsibleSourcing

Waste Reduction

Understanding the environmental impact of MICE events, we’re working to halve our operational waste-to-landfill by half against a 2015 baseline and currently recycle 10 distinct waste streams.
/en/about/sustainability/lower-emissions#Waste

Centralised Cooling System

Our cooling demand is met by our centralised District Cooling Plant (DCP), creating a balance between guest comfort and energy savings.

/en/about/sustainability/lower-emissions#Energy

Green Buildings

Ten of our buildings are BCA Green Mark certified GoldPlus and above, ensuring that any event you host is in a certified sustainable building.

/en/about/sustainability/lower-emissions#SustainableBuildingsandFacilities
