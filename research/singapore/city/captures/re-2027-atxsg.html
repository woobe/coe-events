About Asia Tech x Singapore

Asia Tech x Singapore (ATxSG) is Asia’s flagship technology event jointly organised by Infocomm Media Development Authority of Singapore (IMDA) and Informa, and supported by the Singapore Tourism Board. The event encompasses three core components: ATxSummit, ATxEnterprise, and the year-round ATxInspire programme.

ATxEnterprise

Organised by Informa at Singapore EXPO, ATxEnterprise brings together global technology leaders, innovators and decision makers across AI, enterprise technology, telecommunications, satellite and broadcast. It is where Asia’s tech ecosystem connects, explores new opportunities and turns innovation into business impact.

Read More

ATxSummit

Hosted by IMDA at Capella Singapore, ATxSummit brings together leaders from government, business and academia to address the most critical issues shaping technology and society. Through the Plenary and The Village, it creates a space for high level dialogue, collaboration and exchange.

Read More

ATxInspire

Organised by IMDA, ATxInspire extends the conversations of ATxSummit throughout the year, bringing together industry leaders, government officials and academics to explore emerging technologies, exchange perspectives and advance the ideas shaping our digital future.
