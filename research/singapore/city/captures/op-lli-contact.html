Getting to Lifelong Learning Institute

By MRT

Lifelong Learning Institute (LLI) is a 3-minute walk from Paya Lebar MRT.

  • Circle Line (CC9), Exit B
  • East-West Line (EW8), Exit A

 

By Car

Lifelong Learning Institute (LLI) is located at 11 Eunos Road 8.

  • From PIE: Exit at Paya Lebar Rd
  • From Sims Ave or Guillemard Rd: Head towards Paya Lebar Rd, make a U-turn at the junction
