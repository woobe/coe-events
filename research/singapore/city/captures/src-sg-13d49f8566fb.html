The Working Capitol provides office space solutions that are flexible, forward-thinking, and embedded in the neighbourhoods we work in.

Since launching at 1 Keong Saik Road in 2015, our network has grown to more than 150 companies and 40 heritage properties across Singapore’s Chinatown and CBD.
1 Keong Saik remains the heart and soul of what we do: providing various spaces for focussed work and meaningful interaction, where members of our community can gather.

If you are looking for flexible workspace and a friendly team to support your business’ needs, please read on or, better yet, book a tour today.
