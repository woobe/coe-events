  99| Glass Room
 100| An extension of Supreme Court Terrace, the Glass Room overlooks the stately Rotunda dome. A private yet inviting space, it is ideal for hosting exclusive client events and corporate evenings.
 101| Suitable for private functions, product and media launches, cocktail receptions, as well as corporate evenings.
 102| Standing capacity: 140 pax
 103| Wheelchair accessible
 104| Where: L5, Supreme Court Wing
 105| Get directions
 106| Things you should know before renting our venue spaces
 107| The venue spaces shall be used solely for the purpose of the scheduled event.
 108| The following activities/events are not allowed in the Gallery: Political, religious, or any events which are socially sensitive in nature
 109| Weddings or ceremonial events
 110| Sales of products or services or roadshows in conjunction with an event at the Gallery
 111| Benefits or events designed for soliciting business
 112| Cooking demonstration events
 113| Exhibition or display of commercial artwork
 114| Display of automobiles in the Gallery’s premises and frontage, except with prior approval from the Gallery.
 115| Animals and pets are not permitted in any part of the Gallery, unless they have prior approval from the Gallery.
 116| The Gallery reserves the right to require the Hirer to limit the noise levels and regulate the volume of any sound, music and performances from audio-visual (AV) or public announcement (PA) equipment or systems at the venue spaces that might affect or cause any disturbance and discomfort to the general public and visitors of the Gallery.
