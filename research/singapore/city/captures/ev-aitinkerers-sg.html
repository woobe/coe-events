Innovate. Share what broke.

Bring side projects, unfinished systems, architecture notes, trade-offs, and failure modes. The room is built for candid technical work.

Local rooms. Global signal.

Each city protects the room locally while contributing to a shared global standard for serious AI builders.

Our thesis

Builders drive the agentic economy.

Builders are establishing the foundations of the next economy. Their choices influence how work gets done, where money flows, and which technologies become infrastructure.

The decisions of a relatively small number of builders will shape how billions of people live and work.

AI Tinkerers connects them through local rooms and a global network, helping good ideas become working practices others can build on.

Be ambitious. Start with an idea. Bring a prototype. Work through the hard parts with builders in your city and around the world.
