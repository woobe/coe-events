Getting Here and Parking

Located in the heart of Kampong Glam, Aliwal Arts Centre (AAC) is a vibrant arts centre in Singapore dedicated to nurturing the city's creative ecosystem.

Established under Arts House Group (AHG) , the Aliwal arts hub serves as a key platform for emerging artists, collectives, and creative practitioners to experiment, collaborate, and engage diverse audiences. It's also the setting of the Aliwal Urban Arts Festival, a celebration of street art, culture and rhythm.

As part of AHG's mission to enrich lives through the transformative power of the arts, AAC offers dynamic spaces that bridge traditional and contemporary practices, encouraging artistic excellence and community participation within Singapore's cultural district.

Historic Milestones

1930s - 1950s

1938: The striking Art-Deco building that houses Aliwal Arts Centre was built in 1938 for two schools: Chong Cheng Boys School and Chong Pun Girls School. Through these early decades, the building became a cornerstone in the local community, nurturing generations of students.

2000s

2009: The historic site was chosen as the second project under the National Arts Council’s Framework for Arts Spaces, embarking on its transformation into a vibrant arts centre.

2011 - Present

2011: The building was officially conserved by the Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA) as part of the culturally rich Kampong Gelam district.

2013: Aliwal Arts Centre was officially opened to the public.

Today, Aliwal Arts Centre stands at the heart of Kampong Gelam as a multidisciplinary hub, spotlighting performing and urban arts, and fostering a vibrant artistic community.
