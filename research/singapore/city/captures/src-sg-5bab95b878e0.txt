  87| About this space
  88| Chrysalis is an Event Space in Lovorth that centres on a built-in raised platform and an integrated display for presentations and panel sessions.
  89| Design: The room has muted grey wall panels, acoustic ceiling tiles and neutral carpeting that give a restrained, professional appearance. The room contains simple lounge chairs, a low coffee table and potted plants that frame the presentation area.
  90| Features: Chrysalis includes a PA system and permits hosts to play their own music or bring a DJ, subject to a noise restriction. The room provides Wi‑Fi, natural light and air conditioning. The room has a step-free guest entrance, wheelchair access and accessible toilets.
  91| Ideal for: Intimate to mid-sized professional groups seeking a seated, presentation-focused layout and a focused, formal tone.
