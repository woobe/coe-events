About this space

The Standard Plus rooms at the RNN Conference Center in the CES CENTER in Singapore offer a comfortable and convenient stay for business travelers and conference attendees.

Featuring modern amenities and stylish furnishings, these rooms provide a relaxing retreat after a day of meetings or exploring the city. With thoughtful touches and a focus on functionality, the Standard Plus rooms offer everything you need for a productive and enjoyable stay in the heart of Singapore.
