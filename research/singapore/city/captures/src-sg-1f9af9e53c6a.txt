  90| About this space
  91| We will offer 30 - 50% discount off original price if your booking is in the next 7 days. FREE unlimited usage of our new receipt photobooth!
  92| Previously known as House of Offline, we moved to a new space called 'The Offline Room'. It is on the GROUND FLOOR (yes, no stairs!) and comes with two complimentary parking lots. While there are plenty of other parking lots just across the street, it is also within 5-10min walking distance from 2 MRT stations (Lavender/Bendemeer).
  93| The most versatile event space in Singapore to cater to all events. It's up to your imagination on how you want to set up the space. Most of our chairs and tables are foldable. Most importantly, the lighting can be dimmed to warmer lights for a cozier vibe or brighter for corporate events. Feel free to just come down for a site visit first before deciding!
  94| We are also giving FREE usage of bouncy castle if you book > 5 hours!

 470| FAQ
 471| Are tables and chairs provided in the room?
 472| Yes, all the tables and chairs are already in the space. They need to be arranged into the preferred layout during the 30-minute complimentary setup time; the furniture is flexible and reconfigurable.
 473| Is a projector available?
 474| Yes, the venue has three microphones, a projector and a sound system.
 475| Is the venue wheelchair accessible?
 476| Yes, the venue is wheelchair accessible, with a ramp leading to the ground-floor unit.
 477| Are 15-minute performances allowed inside the event space?
 478| Yes, performances are allowed inside the space.
 479| What decorations are permitted for birthday parties?
 480| The venue has few restrictions on decorations, but only masking tape and Blu Tack are allowed on the white walls. Taping anything to the red walls or floor is not permitted; most clients use backdrops and balloons.
