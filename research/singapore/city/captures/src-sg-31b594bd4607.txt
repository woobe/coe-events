  91| About this space
  92| Check out our Google reviews: https://maps.app.goo.gl/HweNWN9Mx89pRJmbA
  93| room2f is a multi-use event space that is conveniently located just minutes away from City Hall, Bugis and Bras Basah MRT stations. The cosy, well equipped and versatile cafe-like space can hold 80-100 pax seated for gatherings, 80-100 pax for seminars and 60-80 pax for meetings & workshops.
  94| There are plenty of parking options within and around Bras Basah Complex, as well as escalators and lifts to access our space.
  95| The space comprises a bar and reception with open seating area, an assortment of board, card and tabletop games, retro video game machines, curated books and magazines, as well as a gallery wall for you to display your works, photos or products. Projection equipment and microphones are available upon request. You can showcase, share & engage, get together, create new ideas and hold all important work or life events within this beautiful space.
  96| This makes it the perfect location for a wide variety of events including birthday parties, celebrations, gatherings, workshops, showcases and also corporate events.
  97| We cater to both private events, corporate events, photography and video shoots at affordable rates. Just write to us if you need a space that can work to your needs.
