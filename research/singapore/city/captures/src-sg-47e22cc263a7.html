About this space

A carpeted rectangular room in neutral tones with recessed ceiling lights and side windows that supply natural daylight. The seating is arranged around an open central space.

Room features: A ceiling-mounted projector and a wall-length whiteboard are provided. A freestanding flipchart is supplied and the room is air-conditioned.

Best for: Presentations, instructor-led training and facilitated meetings that use a single focal presenter and group discussion.

What sets it apart: An integrated PA system supplies clear amplified audio suitable for speeches and seminars.
