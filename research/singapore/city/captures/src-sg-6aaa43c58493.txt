  87| About this space
  88| A compact visual arts centre that combines exhibition display and a hands-on teaching studio.
  89| Design: The interior has white walls and ceiling-mounted track lighting that keep artwork clearly lit. Long light-wood communal tables run down the centre, surrounded by stackable white chairs, potted plants and front windows that admit natural daylight.
  90| Features: Modular worktables and stackable chairs allow flexible layouts around a clear central floor. A PA system and Wi‑Fi are provided and the sound policy permits own music or an outside DJ. A hot-and-cold water dispenser is on site; air conditioning, accessible toilets and a step‑free entrance are available.
  91| Ideal for: Families, community groups and small classes seeking relaxed, activity-led sessions and social workshops.
  92| What guests say: The venue provides patient, hands-on instructional support during activities. The space is well organised for group sessions and offers practical prep area for snacks and materials.
