 117| GovWare – Where Cyber Means Business
 118| GovWare is the region’s premier cybersecurity information and connectivity platform, bringing together policymakers, tech innovators, and end-users for over three decades to drive meaningful dialogue, strategic collaboration, and collective action across the cyber ecosystem.
 119| Our success lies in the trust and support from the cybersecurity and broader cyber community that we have had the privilege to serve over the years, as well as organisational partners who share the same value and mission to enrich the cyber ecosystem.
 120| Guided by our commitment to trust, innovation, and community, GovWare continues to strengthen connectivity and collaboration across government, industry, and enterprise – supporting a more secure and resilient digital future.
