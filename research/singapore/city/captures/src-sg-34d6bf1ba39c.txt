  93| Located in the heart of Singapore's Civic District, The Arts House at the Old Parliament is a distinguished arts centre in Singapore. The host of iconic arts and cultural events such as the Golden Point Award (GPA)
  94| , Singapore International Festival of Arts
  95| , and VERSE, it plays a vital role in supporting the nation's creative ecosystem through festivals, performances, exhibitions, and community programmes.
  96| Originally built in 1827 as Singapore's Old Parliament House, Arts House Group (AHG) manages this historic arts venue and national monument. Its neoclassical architecture and storied past provide a unique backdrop for artistic expression, cultural dialogue, and literary appreciation, making it a cornerstone of arts events in Singapore
  97| .
  98| Historic Milestones
  99| 1820s - 1930s
 100| 1826–1827: Designed and constructed by architect G.D. Coleman, this stately building first rose on the banks of the Singapore River.
 101| 1841: The government acquired the property, transforming it into Singapore’s main courthouse - a role held until 1865.
 102| 1875 - 1939: The building began a new chapter as the Supreme Court, serving Singapore’s judicial needs well into the next century.
 103| 1930s - 1980s
 104| Post-1945: After World War II, the stately halls were repurposed to support the city’s recovery, serving as a government storehouse.
 105| 1954: The building was reopened as “Assembly House,” after extensive refurbishment, where the newly formed Legislative Assembly met.
 106| 1965–1999: Witnessing Singapore’s journey from independence to modern nation, the building proudly housed the Parliament of Singapore for over three decades.
 107| 1990s - Present
 108| 1992: Recognised for its extraordinary heritage, the Old Parliament House was declared a National Monument - preserving its legacy for generations to come.
 109| 26 March 2004: The Arts House officially opened after a 4 years restoration, inviting visitors to experience the building’s rich history and vibrant arts scene.
 110| Today: Under the stewardship of the Arts House Group (AHG), the venue continues to flourish as a multidisciplinary arts and heritage space.

 118| Arts in the Civic District
 119| Arts in the Civic District  aggregates arts programmes in the Civic District and presents the precinct as a lively and vibrant arts and culture destination in Singapore. It features programmes ranging from museum visits and live performances to arts and culture activities, curated by the Civic District stakeholders: Asian Civilisations Museum, National Gallery Singapore, The Arts House at the Old Parliament, Victoria Theatre and Victoria Concert Hall.
