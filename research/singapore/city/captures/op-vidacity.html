Vidacity@
one-north

69 Ayer Rajah Cres., Singapore 139961

Situated in the heart of Launchpad@one-north, among 2,400+ startups, 30+ incubators, accelerators, and VCs, this is Vidacity’s first expansion in Singapore.

 

This dynamic base houses Lorong AI, Stage One by ACE.SG, and N9 Offices, alongside VidaStudio, home to LUME Coffee’s pop-up, dedicated co-working space for VidaCircle members, and the venue for regular curated networking sessions across the sustainability industry.

Vidacity@
Pasir Ris

3A Pasir Ris Drive 6, Singapore 519422

House in the adaptive reuse of the former Loyang Primary School, Pasir Ris is Vidacity’s flagship living lab that is purpose-built for testing urban, energy, and agri-tech solutions.

 

At its heart is the Microforest, a living showcase of biophilic and circular economy principles in action, alongside functional testbeds where agri-tech prototypes and circular systems are trialed side by side.
