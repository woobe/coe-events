  86| About this space
  87| Combining Function Rooms 4-2 and 4-3, Function Room 4-5 is a large, versatile space ideal for major events, accommodating up to 100 people in a theatre setup or 78 in a classroom arrangement.
  88| This room is perfect for conferences, large training sessions, or seminars. It boasts a full suite of audio-visual equipment, including a projector, screen, speakers, and wireless microphones, ensuring high-quality presentations. The room also provides access to a contemporary break-out area for networking. Additional features include a glass writing board, flip chart, and numerous power outlets for a tech-enabled environment.
