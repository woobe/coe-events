About this space

A large auditorium ideal for keynote sessions, plenaries, talks, and presentations. Features collapsible seating, a full AV system, and enhanced acoustics.

Includes a long-throw projector, one short-throw projector, and four microphones. Additional flipchart is available upon request.

The room accommodates up to 126 seated participants, with two designated wheelchair spaces.
