  87| Modern & Versatile Corporate Event Space
  88| Uprade your next seminar, workshop, or training session in our bright, contemporary auditorium. Designed with productivity in mind, this professional venue combines high-end technical capabilities with a warm, inviting atmosphere.
  89| Key Features:
  90| Flexible Layouts: The space features high-quality modular furniture that can be easily reconfigured from a classic classroom setup (as pictured) to a theater, U-shape, or workshop cluster arrangement.
  91| Natural Light & Views: Unlike many windowless seminar rooms, our space boasts floor-to-ceiling windows offering expansive green views, ensuring your guests stay energized and engaged during the day.
  92| Fully Equipped Tech: The room is "plug-and-play" ready, featuring a large motorized projector screen, ceiling-mounted projectors, a professional integrated sound system, and a dedicated speaker's stage.
  93| Sleek Design: With charcoal carpeting, wood-paneled accents, and modern recessed lighting, the space provides a high-end backdrop for brand launches or corporate presentations.
  94| Functionality: Includes multiple "EXIT" points for easy flow, climate control (AC), and power points for attendee laptops.
