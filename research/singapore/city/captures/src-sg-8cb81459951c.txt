  87| About this space
  88| Hive Rooftop Lounge is an event space in The Hive Carpenter that centres on an open-air rooftop terrace and city skyline views.
  89| Design: The interior area uses floor-to-ceiling glass doors, neutral tiled flooring and timber-effect tables arranged around a clear circulation route. The terrace area is fitted with low planter boxes and metal-framed seating configured for small groups.
  90| Features: The space includes a PA system and speakers and allows guests to play their own music. The indoor area is air-conditioned and connected to the venue Wi-Fi. The room is wheelchair accessible and supplied with movable tables and chairs for casual dining or standing arrangements.
  91| Ideal for: Creative teams and small work groups seeking an informal, social setting for relaxed standing receptions or casual seated gatherings.
  92| What guests say: The lounge is bright and relaxing and furnished with comfortable, high-quality seating. The space provides stable Wi-Fi and accessible power points, and the on-site staff are consistently welcoming.
