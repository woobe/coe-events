   4| Where will Sibos be held in the coming years?
   5| 2027 Singapore
   6| Singapore, Marina Bay Sands,
   7| 20 - 23 September 2027
   8| Singapore will host Sibos for the third time in 2027. Following successful events in 2003 and 2015, the financial community will come together at the iconic Marina Bay Sands.
   9| Singapore is the heart of the buzzing Asia Pacific financial landscape. Home to almost 120 foreign banks, Singapore has a strong reputation across banking, insurance and capital market services.
  10| Look out for more information in early 2027.
  11| 2028 PARIS
  12| Paris, 2028
  13| Paris will host Sibos for the first time in 2028 – a milestone year marking the 50th anniversary of the event.
  14| The French capital employs approximately 340,000 people in the financial sector (Paris Europlace), making it one of the largest financial hubs in Europe.
  15| Venue and dates to be announced.
  16| 2029 DUBAI
  17| Dubai, 2029
  18| Dubai will follow as host city in 2029. The Middle East is a key strategic region in global finance, bridging Europe, Asia and Africa - facilitating trade and investment flows.
  19| Dubai is home to the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC), a global hub that’s grown significantly since its foundation in 2004. The DIFC is now home to almost 7,000 businesses as well as the majority of the Gulf Cooperation Council’s fintech industry.
