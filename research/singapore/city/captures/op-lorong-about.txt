  31| What is Lorong AI?
  32| Under Singapore’s National AI Strategy 2.0, Lorong AI is a community hub built on a simple belief: breakthroughs happen when the right people connect. We bring together the talent, research, and opportunities shaping a world-class AI ecosystem.
