Etherum BUIDLATHON in Korea

Welcome to ETH Seoul

ETH Seoul is a 3 day hackathon that takes place in Neowiz building in Pangyo, South Korea. Organized by KryptoSeoul, Ludium, and Dystopia Labs, ETH Seoul started in 2022 and this year will be our 3rd hackathon. We urge you to come out, meet our vibrant builder community, and BUIDL cool apps using the latest cutting edge tech in the Ethereum ecosystem!

Short Summary of the Event

  • Place: Neowiz Pangyo Tower
  • Date: Mar 29th - March 31st
  • Programs
    • Opening Ceremony
    • Team Formation / Hacking
    • Conference / Talks
    • Mentoring
    • Workshops
    • Idea Pitch / Judging
    • Award distribution / Closing Ceremony
  • More detailed hacker guide will be updated

Caveat

  • Please register as a hacker on Devfolio to participate. Eligible participants will receive confirmation e-mail
  • At least one member of the team must register on site to participate
  • Meals, late night snacks, drinks will be provided for all participants
  • Venue will be open 24 hours only for hackers. There will not be any separate accommodation other than the designated venue. Please plan accordingly
  • Detailed hacker guide will be updated soon
