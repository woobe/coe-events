We are an award-winning art and community facility with three distinct spaces to choose from for your next rehearsal, class, or video/photo shoot.
Centrally located one-block from Powell Street Station in downtown San Francisco, our intimate space is ready to support your projects and ideas.
All of our spaces are fully ADA accessible. Learn more about accessibility at CounterPulse >>
