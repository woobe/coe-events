Event Rentals
Event Rentals Dara Katrina Del Rosario 2026-05-20T12:54:11-07:00
SOMArts is an industrial building turned cultural oasis and is part of the city’s thriving arts & culture community. Featuring high ceilings, exposed beams, professional lighting, sound systems, stage with rear projection and a lush garden, SOMArts offers event rentals in one of San Francisco’s most memorable venues.

EVENT SPACE
5810 SQ Ft (incl 1470 sq ft stage) / 650 standing Capacity
SOMArts’ venue can be used as a whole or divided into partitions and is both equipped and permitted to host groups as small as 35 or as large as 650. From art auctions, exhibitions , and fundraisers to black box theater performances, this event rental space is flexible to meet your event needs!

Event Rental Packages
Our packages provide our starting price point based on the length of the event and number of days. Even if you’re not sure whether your event will fit neatly into one of these packages – please submit your request for a quote! There are a number of ways we can try to accommodate your group.
Important: these packages do not include discounts given to non-profits, security (which is determined based on anticipated attendance), nor the refundable damage deposit.
Essentials Package
Attendance: 100-150 guests
Event length: 2-3 hours
Pricing starts at $4,549
The Essentials Package is the perfect choice for making your one-day event or pop-up truly memorable! This package includes:
Day-long access to the event space (8 hours)
Full Audio and Lighting Package
Audio technician
Set up and load out labor
Signature Package
Attendance: flexible based on our capacity maximums
Event length: 4 hours
Pricing starts at $6,428
The Signature Package offers enhanced customization and event coordination support to elevate your experience. This package includes:
Day-long access to the event space (8 hours)
Full Audio and Lighting Package
Audio technician
Set up and load out labor
Custom lighting
Video projection capabilities
Stage manager
Janitorial labor and disposal fee
Deluxe Package
Attendance: Flexible based on our capacity maximums
Event length: All-day or two-day event
Pricing starts at $12,382
The Deluxe Package suits most all-day or two-day events, with all the features of the Signature Package:

Frequently Asked Questions
Is insurance required for my event?
Yes–for all public events, we require General Liability Insurance with a minimum coverage of One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) per occurrence. SOMArts and the City and County of San Francisco must be added as additional insured, as SOMArts operates in a city-owned building. Additionally, for organizations renting the space, proof of Workers’ Compensation insurance is required if you have employees.
What is your capacity?
Our Rental Space has a maximum capacity of 650 people. Please note that capacity decreases as tables, chairs, and other items are added to the space. The patio capacity is 300. When the building is rented in its entirety, it can accommodate up to 900 guests.
Where do we park vehicles for event staff, loading, and unloading?
SOMArts has a dedicated driveway off of Brannan St and a limited number of parking spaces near the door of our main entrance which, in most cases, can be used during load-in and load-out. If you are using the patio area for your event, then parking at the end of the driveway won’t be possible, but there is a paid parking lot directly across the street that typically has ample spaces for evening events, and there is usually street parking available (metered until 6pm) on Brannan St and 8th/9th St.
Can I drop off decorations/ food/ beverages in advance of my event?
We will do our best to accommodate pre-event drop-off needs. You can discuss this with the technical director leading up to your event.
When are payments due?
50% of your total rental fee is due upon signing your contract in order to secure your date. The remaining balance is for at least 4 weeks prior to your event.
Do I need to rent audio equipment?
Every rental includes a full-size PA system with subwoofers, microphones, additional speakers, and accessories. Please note, we do not provide DJ gear, amps, or instruments.
Do I need to rent lighting?
Your rental includes professional lighting for your event space. Lighting references can be found linked here.
How big is the stage?
The stage is 35′ wide, 17′ deep, and 3′ high.
Do you have tables and chairs?
Yes, we do have tables and chairs available! You can view our inventory here.
Can we access your kitchen?
We have a “kitchenette” which includes a sink, refrigerator, and freezer that clients can use during their rental. This space is great for light prep, but no cooking is permitted inside the building. Cooking can happen outside with the necessary fire permits secured before your event.
SOMArts reserves the right to decline any space request for any reason, including a determination that the event does not align with SOMArts’ charitable purpose and mission.
