About us
The Blockchain Global Group is focused on bringing together like minded crypto enthusiasts and blockchain experts alike. At BGG you will find blockchain developers, entrepreneurs, community leaders, and investors working together towards a decentralized future. Join our meetup group to stay up to date with our latest events.
