General Jurisdiction Hearing Calendar
Look up hearing dates or other event dates by date, case number, case name, or agency name for the General Jurisdiction Division.
How to Observe a Hearing
Members of the public may observe General Jurisdiction Division virtual, telephonic, or in-person proceedings except where the proceeding is not open to the public pursuant to statute, regulation or order. Any observers may not participate in, record, or disrupt the proceedings. To obtain information on how to observe a particular proceeding, please contact the regional OAH office where the proceeding is scheduled. Contact information is available here: https://www.dgs.ca.gov/OAH/Case-Types/General-Jurisdiction/Contact
Hearing Date Availability for Future Hearings:
Notice: Hearing date availability calendars are provided as a courtesy to assist counsel and parties with conferring about future hearing dates. Dates are subject to availability at the time a Request to Set or other calendar request is processed by OAH. Dates shown as available are not guaranteed to be available. The calendars provided are not a reservation of hearing dates. These calendars are updated regularly.

OAH at this time is not setting 1-day hearings on Fridays. Setting consecutive hearing dates may include a Friday as necessary. If you require a 1-day Friday hearing date, please contact your local OAH office to confirm availability.
