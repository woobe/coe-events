Rentals at Brava
It's easy to reserve space for your next performance or rehearsal at Brava Theater Center. We offer competitive rates and are located in San Francisco's eclectic, restaurant-filled Mission District. Brava is transit-accessible, just a 10-minute walk from BART, and close to the freeway. Parking is available just two blocks away.
Brava has three performance spaces available for rent in one convenient location. Contact us now to reserve the Main Theater, the second stage Studio , or our street level Cabaret .
If you are interested in renting one of Brava’s three spaces, please email our Rentals and Events Manager, Lottie Pacheco, at lottie@brava.org.
Rental Services
Front-of-House
At Brava Theater you can relax knowing that your lobby set-up, ushers, and ticket takers will be managed by our friendly and professional staff. All clients hire a Brava house manager. We provide a short orientation (one half hour) for your ushers and ticket takers before doors open. If you choose, for a small fee, Brava can hire experienced ushers and ticket takers for your event.
Box Office
Take advantage of Brava’s ticketing service, in-house publicity experts, and well-trafficked ticket booth.
MAIN THEATER
Learn more
Seating Capacity: 360
Orchestra: 250 (including 6 ADA accessible)
Mezzanine: 110
Our spacious Main Theater is appropriate for larger-audience shows including films, plays, and live music, and features world-class lighting and sound.
BRAVA LOBBY
Capacity: 80
Our Main Theater Lobby is a wonderful space to hold gatherings such as receptions, dinners, and talks. It can be booked as a stand alone venue or in conjunction with an event on our Main Stage.
BRAVA STUDIO
Learn more
Seating Capacity: 60
Room Layout: 25' x 45', curtain available to cover mirrored wall, sprung floor
Our intimate Studio is an excellent venue for smaller special events, and makes a fine rehearsal space as well.
BRAVA CABARET
Learn more
Seating Capacity: 60
Room Layout:
Downstairs: 30’ wide by 33’ long
Mezzanine: 22’ wide by 13’ long
Our Cabaret is an excellent venue for music, comedy, and live theater, smaller special events and rehearsals, as well as for receptions, workshops and meetings.
