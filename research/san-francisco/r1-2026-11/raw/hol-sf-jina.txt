# City and County of San Francisco holiday schedule

See the City and County of San Francisco holiday calendar. Many City offices are closed on these days.

[Digital Services](https://www.sf.gov/departments--city-administrator--digital-services/)

## 2026 holiday schedule

**New Year's Day**: January 1, 2026

**Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Day**: January 19, 2026

**Presidents' Day**: February 16, 2026

**Memorial Day**: May 25, 2026

**Juneteenth**: June 19, 2026

**Independence Day** (observed): July 3, 2026

**Independence Day**: July 4, 2026

**Labor Day**: September 7, 2026

**Indigenous Peoples Day**: October 12, 2026

**Italian American Heritage Day**: October 12, 2026

**Veterans Day**: November 11, 2026

**Thanksgiving Day**: November 26, 2026

**Day after Thanksgiving**: November 27, 2026

**Christmas**: December 25, 2026

Find out more about [what's going on in San Francisco](https://www.sf.gov/topics--things-to-do-san-francisco/).
