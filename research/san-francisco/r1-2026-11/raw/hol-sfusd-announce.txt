Student Family School Resource Link
SFUSD's Student Family School Resource Link supports students and families in navigating all of the SFUSD resources available to them. Students and families can email requests to sflink@sfusd.edu , call 415-340-1716 (M-F, 9 a.m. to noon and 1 to 3 p.m., closed from 12 to 1 p.m. every day and on school holidays ), or complete an online request form . Staff who need technology support should contact the Technology Help Desk ( help@sfusd.edu , 415-241-6476), while staff who need Human Resources support can find guidance about routing their requests on HR’s homepage .
