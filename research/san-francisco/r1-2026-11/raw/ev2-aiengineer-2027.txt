The World's Fair experience
More of what brought us together
The core format returns in 2027: deep technical content, hands-on learning and a place to meet the people building AI. The detailed schedule, track lineup, speakers and exhibitors will be announced.
Technical tracks & keynotes
Go deep on building and shipping AI alongside engineers and researchers. The 2026 program spanned agents, evals, inference, retrieval, multimodality and more; the 2027 track lineup is still to come.
Hands-on workshops
Work through practical techniques and tooling, with sessions for experienced developers getting into AI and engineers taking production systems further.
AI tools expo & live demos
Meet the teams behind AI tools, models and infrastructure. Explore products, ask technical questions and see what is possible in live demos.
AI leadership
Join CTOs, VPs of AI and technical leaders to discuss building AI teams, choosing architectures and bringing AI into the enterprise.
Hallway conversations & side events
Connect with engineers, founders and researchers between sessions and at community gatherings around San Francisco. The 2027 side-event calendar will follow.
