Our Venues
The Marsh San Francisco
Mainstage
The San Francisco Mainstage is a 105 seat theater on the ground floor of the building. Concessions, including non-alcoholic drinks, are available for purchase inside the theater.
Studio Theater
The San Francisco Studio Theater is a 60-90 seat black box theater on the second floor of the building, accessible by stairs and elevator. Will call and concessions are located at The Marsh Cafe one door down from the Studio entrance on the ground floor.
The Marsh Berkeley
Theater
The Berkeley Theater is a 75-120 seat theater on the ground floor of the building. Additional seating is available on the Mezzanine level, accessible by stairs and elevator. Snacks and drinks from the Cabaret Bar are welcome inside the theater.
Cabaret
The Berkeley Cabaret is a 60 seat performance space with cabaret-style seating on the ground floor of the building. The Cabaret Bar is open both for Cabaret events and as the bar for shows in the theater.
