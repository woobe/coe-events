Performing Arts Venue — Theater
Alliance Française de San Francisco
Theater Rental Policy & Fee Schedule
1345 Bush Street, San Francisco, CA 94109
1. Venue Overview
The Alliance Française de San Francisco (AFSF) performing arts venue seats 74 guests and is available for evening rentals by independent
artists, nonprofit organizations, and private event organizers. All rentals are subject to the terms outlined in this policy.
The venue is configured for seated events and is suitable for the following event types: presentations, conferences (with or without
slideshow), and movie screenings. Concerts and theater productions may also be hosted at the venue; however, these require a separate
agreement and are subject to additional terms and conditions. Please contact AFSF to discuss availability and applicable fees for these
formats.

Catering & Beverages surcharge (renter-provided): a flat surcharge applies when the renter plans to serve food and/or
beverages on the premises, to cover additional cleaning and post-event reset.
Alcohol Service: Under California law (Business and Professions Code §25680 et seq.), all persons serving alcohol at any
event must hold a valid California Responsible Beverage Service (RBS) certification. This requirement applies to all renters without
exception, including nonprofit organizations. Proof of valid RBS certification for each server must be submitted to AFSF prior to the event
date. No alcohol may be served at the venue without prior verification of certification. Renters who do not have a certified server may
engage the AFSF event host to serve alcohol for a flat additional fee of $125. Please note that the event host will act solely as the
designated alcohol server and is not available to provide general catering or food service. This arrangement must be requested and confirmed
at the time of booking.
Nonprofits must provide proof of nonprofit status (IRS determination letter or equivalent documentation) at the time of
booking to qualify for the nonprofit rate. Rentals booked without valid proof of status will be invoiced at the private event rate.
A refundable security deposit of $300 is required for all rentals, payable at the time of booking confirmation.
4. Staffing
A mandatory staffing fee of $290 applies to all evening rentals. This fee covers the overtime compensation for two AFSF staff members
required for every event, including a 22% payroll overhead to account for employer taxes, California UI/ETT/SDI, and workers' compensation.
Front Desk Person: stationed at the building entrance to welcome and check in guests from 5:00 pm through 7:30 pm, after
which the entrance is no longer staffed.
Event Host: an AFSF representative present throughout the event to oversee venue operations, ensure compliance with this
policy, and remain on site until the building is fully secured. The event host is responsible for closing the building at 9:15 pm.
5. Movie Screenings
Renters wishing to host a movie screening must comply with the following requirements:
The film file must be provided in MP4 format.
The file must be submitted to AFSF staff at least 5 business days prior to the event date for technical testing and
compatibility verification.
AFSF reserves the right to decline screening of files that fail technical testing or that do not meet format requirements.
The renter is solely responsible for securing all necessary public screening licenses and rights clearances prior to the event.
6. General Conditions
Full payment of all applicable fees is due no later than 10 days prior to the event date . Dates will not be held and the
rental will not be considered confirmed until payment is received in full.
All payments are non-refundable. Cancellations for any reason, including by the renter, will not give rise to any refund of
fees paid.
The security deposit will be returned within 10 business days following the event, provided no damage or policy violations are identified.
AFSF staff reserve the right to end an event early if terms of this policy are not observed.
Rentals must be confirmed in writing and a signed rental agreement returned to AFSF prior to the event date. The rental is not confirmed
until full payment has been received.
Renters are responsible for the conduct of their guests throughout the rental period.
7. Daytime Rentals
The theater is also available for daytime rental on weekdays (Monday to Friday) and Saturdays, subject to AFSF operational schedules. No
additional staffing fee applies to daytime rentals as AFSF staff are already on site during opening hours.
