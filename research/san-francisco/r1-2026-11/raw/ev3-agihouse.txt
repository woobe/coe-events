04 History
A Short
History
In February 2022, Jeremy Nixon put his own name on the lease for a house in Hillsborough and started inviting researchers to live and build there. That fall he and Andrej Karpathy ran the first hackathons out of the living room. There was no brand and no plan, just a full house of people who wanted to work on intelligence together. It became the first AGI House.
Perplexity was started there. Martian was invented and won a hackathon there. The founders who came through, the group that got called the PayPal Mafia of AI, went on to build companies now worth ~$1 trillion . Across 2022 and 2023 the house ran dozens of hackathons, from Whisper and the first ChatGPT weekend to LangChain, GPT-4, and the CRV agents hackathon where MultiOn and AutoGPT first showed up.
The mission never changed: give the people building the future a place to build it together, then get out of their way.
AGI House
Hillsborough · 2022
The first house. Jeremy Nixon and Andrej Karpathy's hackathons, and the estate where Perplexity and Martian were built.
AGI House SF
San Francisco · 2024
The second house. Jeremy opened it in the city to run the same thing at a larger scale, now hosting up to five events a week.
2022
Jeremy Nixon takes the lease on a house in Hillsborough . He and Andrej Karpathy run the earliest generative-AI hackathons out of it, and the first AGI House is born.
2023
Dozens of hackathons in a single year. The house becomes the default place new agents, image models, and LLM tools get built and demoed first.
2024
Jeremy opens AGI House SF , a second house in San Francisco, and moves the work into the city.
Jan 2026
Julius Ritter joins as president to lead AGI House SF. The house runs hackathons with OpenAI on autoresearch, self-improving agent frameworks, and harness optimization, at a cadence of up to five events a week .
