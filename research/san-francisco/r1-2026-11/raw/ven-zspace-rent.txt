Z SPACE: NOT YOUR AVERAGE
REPURPOSED CAN FACTORY
Z Space is now accepting bookings for 2027. Please contact Curatorial Director Nikki Meñez at nmenez@zspace.org to inquire further or fill out our rental request form below.
Located in the heart of San Francisco’s vibrant and historic Mission District, Z Space is an unconventional and unique 13,000 square foot venue with the flexibility to suit a variety of event needs. In addition to theater and dance productions, we host community events, staff retreats, holiday parties, ping-pong tournaments, and more.
Originally constructed as a factory for the American Can Company, Z Space has been converted into one of the most dynamic arts centers in San Francisco, featuring a 236-seat flexible performance space (The Steindler Stage), a lobby equipped with a full bar, and a separate downstairs 85-seat intimate theater (Z Below).
Take a virtual, three-dimension tour of our main stage courtesy of Google Maps .
Z Space is easily accessible by BART, MUNI, car, bike, and foot. The neighborhood around Z Space teems with artists studios, galleries, bakeries, chocolate factories, and some of SF's hottest bars and restaurants.
For all space use requests, please submit an inquiry via our online form . Note that it may take up to 2 weeks to respond to your rental request and that most rentals need 4-6 months advance notice for consideration. Thank you!

Formalized in 2018, our Subspace program provides heavily subsidized or free rent and support to artists who cannot afford our below-market non-profit rental rate. Since its inception, Z has given away over $350,000 in subsidies to our community. To learn more and apply for Subspace funds, please visit our Subspace page here.
