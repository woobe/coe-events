02 — What is PTW
Not a conference.
A whole country's week.
Portugal Tech Week is a ten-day festival every November that brings startups, investors, tech talent and companies from around the world to Portugal — and connects them with the people building here. Anyone can host an event; anyone can come. 351 — the Portuguese Startup Association — runs the map, the Palace and the signature moments.
European Enterprise Promotion Awards 2024 — 1st place, Promotion of Entrepreneurial Spirit.
■The PTW Palace
Our home base in Lisboa: talks, investor rooms, coworking and the bar.100+ events in 2026
■Elétrico Pitch
Startups pitch investors on a moving Lisbon tram. No slides.Streamed live
■Tech Days
One day per city, run by local ambassadors — Porto to the Algarve.20+ cities
■Tech Run and Hackathon
The things that make it a festival, not a trade show.Signature
□Everything else
Breakfasts, panels, demo nights, parties — hosted by the community, all on one map.468 in 2026

Questions
When and where is Portugal Tech Week 2027?+
5–14 November 2027, in Lisboa — with the PTW Palace as home base — and in 20+ cities across Portugal.
Is Portugal Tech Week free?+
Yes. The Pass is free and most events are free. A few hosts charge for their own events; the programme says which.
Who comes to Portugal Tech Week?+
Founders, investors, tech talent, corporates and international delegations — from Portugal and from 100+ countries. In 2026 the programme had 564 events from 221 hosts, and 50,000+ people use the PTW platform.
Can I bring a delegation?+
Yes. We build programmes for country and regional delegations: investor and client meetings, a stage at the PTW Palace and introductions across the ecosystem. See the delegations page or write to team@351startups.com.
Where does it happen?+
Lisboa, with the PTW Palace as home base, plus Tech Days in 20+ cities from Viana do Castelo to the Algarve and the Azores.
When does the programme open?+
Hosts can submit events any time; PTW Palace applications close in September 2027. The full schedule and matchmaking go live in October 2027, and Pass holders hear first.
How do I host an event?+
Submit it any time: it takes a few minutes and you keep your own registration page. Want a room at the PTW Palace? Apply before September 2027.
How do I sponsor?+
See the 2027 packages or write to team@351startups.com. Early partners get first pick of moments and rooms.
