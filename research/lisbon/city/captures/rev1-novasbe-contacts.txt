Quality and Accreditations
The Quality and Accreditations Department supports the School in maintaining and enhancing its standards, with a particular focus on teaching and learning. It coordinates national accreditation processes through A3ES and leads the School’s efforts in securing and renewing international accreditations from EQUIS, AACSB, and AMBA. The Department implements and oversees the School’s quality assurance systems (Nova SIMAQ and Assurance of Learning) and is responsible for monitoring and ensuring compliance with degree program structures, academic regulations, and institutional policies. It also produces official statistics on students and faculty, as well as key performance indicators to support planning, reporting, and continuous improvement.
Email: qualidade@novasbe.pt
More information here.

The Research Office supports the management of scientific projects throughout their complete cycle, from applications for competitive funding to planning, development, and scientific outreach. It also manages the PhD Programs in Economics, Finance, and Management.

The Lisbon MBA
The Lisbon MBA Católica | Nova is an internationally recognized joint-venture between Católica Lisbon School of Business and Economics and Nova SBE, two leading business schools in Europe, in collaboration with the MIT Sloan School of Management. Católica-Lisbon and Nova SBE are among the exclusive business schools in the world with the Triple Crown Accreditation (EQUIS, AMBA, and AACSB).
The Lisbon MBA Católica | Nova is considered one of the best MBAs in the world and is the only Portuguese MBA in the Financial Times. It comprises two MBA programs: The International MBA (a 12-month full-time program) and The Executive MBA (a 22-month part-time program).
