About the Conference
Bridging Digital Innovation and Sustainable Development
Technology for a more sustainable, inclusive and resilient future.
The 8th International Conference on Digital Age &
Technological Advances for Sustainable Development
(DATA'27) is an international multidisciplinary forum
organized by the Mediterranean Association of Sciences
and Sustainable Development (Medi-ASD).
Held in Lisbon, Portugal, on March 23-24, 2027,
at the Hotel Meliá Lisboa Oriente, DATA'27 brings
together researchers, academics, industry professionals and
policymakers to explore how emerging digital technologies,
artificial intelligence, intelligent systems and engineering
innovations can address global sustainability challenges.
DATA'27 goes beyond the technological dimension of digital
innovation. It emphasizes the contribution of scientific and
technological advances to the three interconnected pillars
of sustainable development:
environmental protection, social progress and
economic prosperity.
The conference also encourages research aligned with the
United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs),
promoting responsible innovation, digital inclusion, resource
efficiency and resilient societies.
Organized in partnership with UAE – FST Tanger (Morocco),
Al-Mustaqbal University (Iraq) and
Universidade Politécnica de Castelo Branco (Portugal),
DATA'27 offers a hybrid format for both in-person and virtual
participation.

Call for Papers
DATA’27 invites high-quality original research papers that advance digital technologies for sustainable development.
Submission Guidelines
Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished manuscripts of up to
10 pages following the official conference template.
Papers must not be under review or accepted elsewhere.
Similarity check using iThenticate (max 15% overall, 5% from a single source)
Double-blind peer review by 2–3 program committee members
Evaluation based on originality, technical quality, clarity and impact
Accepted papers will be included in the conference proceedings

Suggested Paper Structure
Anatomy of a Paper · The Fish Model
Abstract (Head)
• Problem statement
• Methodology used
• Summary of findings
• Conclusion
Introduction
• Literature review
• What is the problem / burden?
• What is known and what is unknown
• What is your aim and hypothesis
Methodology
• Study design
• Study population
• What data source did you use?
• Risk factors (exposures) & outcomes
• Statistical analysis
Results
• What did you find out?
• What are your primary and secondary findings?
• Data representation in tables and figures
Discussion
• Interpretation and contextualization of the study’s findings in relation to existing knowledge
• Significance, implications, and limitations
Conclusion
• The take-home message
References (Tail)
• Existing knowledge you built this work upon
