About the space
Choose from thoughtfully curated rooms for 8–12 people, each featuring high-speed Wi‑Fi, tele‑ and video-conferencing, and a TV screen (Mac + PC ready).
Ideal for strategy sessions, client presentations, and on‑site team days. Located in Cais do Sodré with easy access to bars, cafés, and Lisbon’s creative pulse.
Book by the hour or day – open to everyone, no membership required.
Amenities:
Fast Wi‑Fi & HD screen with Mac/PC inputs
Tele- & video-conferencing systems
Thoughtful design – mid-century chairs & natural light
Tea, coffee & catering options
Flexible layouts for workshops or meetings
