Overview
Iconic and refined
This 5-star hotel in Lisbon is close to the city's main monuments, such as the Tower of Belém, the Belém Cultural Centre, the Jerónimos Monastery, the MAAT –Museum of Art, Architecture and Technology and the Lisbon Convention Centre.
With two swimming pools, indoor (82º F) and outdoor, a spa and lush gardens complete with plants designated as "National Monuments", this is the perfect hotel for your stay.
The four royal suites located in the palace were once used by the marquesses. A breathtaking view of the river or palatial gardens is part of a unique experience.
This urban resort is a great starting point for touring the amazing city of Lisbon, as well as the renowned town of Cascais or the romantic town of Sintra.

Heritage
Our History
1855-1890
The palace was commissioned by José Luís Constantino Dias, Marquess of Valle‐Flôr. Born in 1855 into a farming family in Trás os Montes, he left for São Tomé at the age of 16, where he established himself in business during a period when the island was becoming a major cocoa producer. He amassed a fortune and gained prestige at the Portuguese court, first as a viscount, then as a count and finally as a marquess.
1890
In 1890, at the height of his success, José Luís Constantino Dias commissioned the construction of the Palácio de Valle Flôr as a family residence in Lisbon, on land granted by the Crown. The project, begun by the Italian architect Nicola Bigaglia and completed by José Ferreira da Costa, brought together architectural and decorative work from Parisian ateliers, establishing the palace as a centrepiece of Lisbon’s social life.
1991
After decades of uncertainty and some neglect, the Palácio de Valle Flôr was acquired by the Pestana Hotel Group in 1991. Over the course of a decade, an extensive programme of intervention, conservation and restoration returned the original beauty of the palace’s façades, interiors and gardens. Today, the Palácio de Valle Flôr is classified as a National Monument.
2001
On 18 May 2001, the former private palace reopened as the Pestana Palace Lisboa, following a decade of restoration. The building was reborn as a refined hotel and living museum, where guests from around the world discover one of Lisbon’s most remarkable stories.
The hotel
Set within the historic Palácio de Valle Flôr and the contemporary wings that surround it, the Pestana Palace Lisboa blends heritage with modern comfort. The stained-glass entrance hall, domed ceiling, monumental staircase, 194 rooms and suites and French-style gardens – also classified as a National Monument – create a distinctive setting, complemented by the swimming pool, Casa do Lago and the Magic Spa.
The suites
The suites at Pestana Palace Lisboa elevate the palatial experience, particularly the four Royal Suites on the palace’s second floor. Once the family’s private quarters, they now feature exclusive terraces, views over the Tagus River and classical interiors in which every detail combines the palace’s history with the comfort expected of a contemporary hotel.
The rooms
The rooms are distributed across the east and west wings and have been renovated in a classic and understated style, designed for comfort. The rooms range from Deluxe Rooms to Junior Suites and offer views of the city or gardens, extending the palatial atmosphere of the main building in a more intimate setting.

Places to see
Rossio
The Praça do Rossio (Rossio Square) is one of the city’s busiest squares. Considered to be the heart of Lisbon, the Praça do Rossio is composed of old Pombaline-style buildings, shops, cafés and the famous Estação do Rossio (Rossio Station), one of the world’s most beautiful train stations. Here you will feel like a native of Lisbon.
See more about Rossio
Belém Tower
Classified as a UNESCO World Heritage Site and voted one of the Seven Wonders of Portugal, the Belém Tower is an icon of Manueline architecture and one of Lisbon’s greatest landmarks.
See more about Belém Tower
Castelo de São Jorge (Saint George’s Castle)
Located in the parish of Santa Maria Maior (Castelo), the Castelo de São Jorge stands on the highest hill in the Historic Centre, providing its visitors with one of the most beautiful views over the city. Here you can explore the archaeological remains, stroll through the gardens and take part in guided tours.
See more about Castelo de São Jorge (Saint George’s Castle)
Oceanário
Considered the “best aquarium in the world” by TripAdvisor, the Oceanário features a large central aquarium and four marine habitats. Discover birds, invertebrates, fish, amphibians, mammals and plants.
See more about Oceanário
Cuisine
Traditional cuisine lovers must try grilled sardines, cod à la Brás, charcoal-grilled dishes and convent sweets, of which the pastel de Belém (custard tart) is the best example. The city features renowned signature cuisine, with some of the top restaurants in the world.

FAQs
What is the price per night at the Pestana Palace?
Rates start from approximately €200 per night for a Classic Room booked directly on pestana.com. Pestana Guest Club members benefit from additional discounts of 10% to 25%. Royal Suites have a premium rate—please contact the hotel.(Note: Prices vary daily. For current rates, please check our website for your selected dates.)
What is the breakfast like at the Pestana Palace?
A buffet breakfast is served in a palatial room and is considered one of the best breakfast experiences in Lisbon. It includes a wide selection of fresh fruits, Portuguese cheeses, cured meats, artisan bread, pastries, made-to-order eggs, fresh juices and Portuguese specialities.
Does the Pestana Palace have a restaurant?
Yes. The Valle Flôr Restaurant offers contemporary Portuguese cuisine in a palatial setting overlooking the gardens. Casa do Lago by SOI, by the garden lake, serves Asian-inspired cuisine. Both are open to guests and the public, with award-winning service recognised internationally.
Does the Pestana Palace have a swimming pool?
Yes, two: an indoor pool heated to 28°C (open year-round) and an outdoor pool surrounded by gardens designated a National Monument (open in the warmer months).
Can I bring children to the Pestana Palace?
All ages are welcome. Cots are free for children up to three years old. Extra beds for children up to 12 years old are available for €60 per night, subject to availability.
Is the hotel in the centre of Lisbon?
The Pestana Palace is located in the Alto de Santo Amaro neighbourhood, near Belém, about 20 minutes from the historic centre. The hotel offers a free shuttle to the city centre. It is a 10-minute walk from the Lisbon Congress Centre and 2 km from the UNESCO-listed monuments of Belém.
What food & drink options are available at Pestana Palace?
The Pestana Palace has 2 restaurants: Valle Flôr and Casa do Lago (open seasonally). The hotel also has one bar: Allegro Bar. Seasonally, on Saturdays, a picnic can be served in the gardens - the "Chic-nic".
Is the Pestana Palace good for weddings?
Yes, it is considered one of Lisbon’s most prestigious wedding venues. It offers historic gardens designated a National Monument, palatial halls and views over the River Tagus. It has 32 event spaces, with capacity for up to 500 guests.
Does the hotel offer an airport transfer?
Yes. Transfers are available on request from the hotel, at an additional charge. Lisbon Airport is 11 km away, about 20 minutes by car.
What are the check-in and check-out times at Pestana Palace?
Check-in at Pestana Palace is from 3:00 p.m., and check-out is until 12:00 p.m..
What bar options are available at the Pestana Palace?
The Pestana Palace has one bar: Allegro Bar.
What is there to do at Pestana Palace?
Pestana Palace offers the following activities / services (charges may apply):
- Outdoor Pool
- Indoor Pool
- Sauna
- Turkish Bath
- Wellness Center
- Massage
- Gym
- Boat Trips
- Guided Cultural Tours
- Tram Ride
- Nightclub/DJ
- Shops
Are there opportunities to exercise at Pestana Palace?
Yes, guests have access to a gym during their stay.
Which popular attractions are close to Pestana Palace?
Nearby attractions include Jerónimos Monastery, Palace of Ajuda, and Belém Tower.
Can I park a car at Pestana Palace?
Yes, parking is available to guests (payable).
