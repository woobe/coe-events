FAQs
Find quick answers about our coworking spaces, private offices, meeting rooms, event spaces and other services, whether you’re a freelancer looking for a vibrant place to work, a start-up in need of an office, or a company planning an event.
What is coworking?Coworking means flexible, membership-based workspaces where freelancers, remote workers, start-ups and companies work side by side in a shared setting, with the services and community to help them grow.Where are you located?Here are our addresses:Largo do Duque de Cadaval 17, Fração I, 1200-160 LisboaHeden Santa Apolónia – Terminal de Cruzeiros de Lisboa – Edifício NE, Doca, R. Jardim do Tabaco 1st floor, 1100-651 LisboaLargo Rafael Bordalo Pinheiro 29, 2nd floor, 1200-369 LisboaHeden Alvalade – R. do Centro Cultural 45, 1700-111 Lisboa Heden Intendente – R. Maria 10, 1170-014 LisboaHeden Trindade – R. Nova da Trindade 3, 1200-301 LisboaWhat does a Heden membership include?Fast, reliable internetCoffee, espresso, tea and infused waterA fully equipped kitchenBookable meeting-room timeA member network to connect with the communityMembers-only eventsAre there any additional expenses that aren’t included in the fees?There are no hidden fees. Anything beyond your plan, such as extra meeting-room hours or printing well beyond fair use, is charged only as agreed.Do you have a Hot Desk membership?Yes. At Heden we call it a Flex Desk membership, as it gives you access to our flexible shared space.What’s the difference between a Flex Desk and a Fixed Desk?With a Flex Desk, you can work at any free desk in our shared space, first come, first served. A Fixed Desk is yours alone: you can set it up with your own monitor and printer. Learn more about our plans.Do I have to sign a lease?No. Instead of a traditional lease, members sign a space use agreement.Is there a minimum commitment?Private offices and dedicated areas require a minimum six-month commitment. Other plans run month to month, except the Annual Pass.Is a deposit required?Only for private offices and dedicated areas, where the final month of the agreement is paid upfront.Is parking included?No, our plans don’t include car parking.What if I want to sign up, but won’t be able to use my membership for a few months?We only charge from the day your membership starts. When you’re ready, come and see us and we’ll get you set up.Can I put my membership on hold?Flex Desk members can pause their membership. Fixed Desk and private office plans can’t be paused, as we’d have to keep the desk or office reserved for you.Can I share an office with another business owner?Yes, but we don’t split the cost. One member pays and is responsible for the office agreement for its full term, even if the other party leaves.Do I need insurance?It isn’t required, but we recommend insuring your equipment if you have a private office or Fixed Desk.Is there a waiting list for private offices or Fixed Desks?Please contact us for availability.Why don’t all Heden offices cost the same?Office prices depend on the size (in m²) and features of each office.What are Heden’s opening hours?Our reception is open Monday to Friday, 9:00–18:00.Can I only work during opening hours?If you need 24/7 access, choose a Fixed Desk or private office plan.Do I need a certain type of Heden membership to book a meeting space?No. Any member can book a meeting room if the space is available.Can non-members book Heden for meetings or events?Yes. Check availability by filling in our booking request form.How can I book a meeting room if I am a member?Once your membership is set up, you book meeting rooms through Google Calendar: each room has its own calendar, and our team will share them with you.Can I bring outside clients in for meetings? Do I need to book a meeting room for this?Of course. We just ask you to book a meeting room for meetings longer than 30 minutes.Do I need to book a tour, or can I just drop in?Tours must be booked in advance so that we can make sure someone is available to show you around and answer any questions you may have. Contact us to book a tour.Do you have printing services?Yes, there are printers at every Heden coworking space. Printing is included in your plan under a fair-use policy.Do you offer a mail service?Yes, for private office members, for whom it’s included. We don’t offer a mail service for other plans.Can I bring my children?We’re not able to accommodate children, although in an emergency we can make an exception for short periods.Can I bring a friend in to work with me?Yes. Guests who work in the space need a day pass, which you can buy through your member account.What if my business outgrows my Heden space?We always try to accommodate our members’ growth. If your team outgrows our spaces, talk to us: we can design and build a custom space for you.Are there any types of businesses that aren’t a good fit for coworking?Businesses with frequent walk-in traffic or appointment-based services, such as retail or therapy practices, aren’t a good fit for Heden. If that’s you, we’re happy to point you towards spaces that suit you better.Not sure coworking, or Heden, is right for you?Book a tour: we’ll show you around, introduce you to some of our members and answer your questions over a coffee. There’s no long-term commitment: plans run month to month, so if it’s not for you, you don’t have to stay.What have you done differently since the 2020 Pandemic began?At Heden we have accommodated all the practices and rules required by DGS.
