AIhub is Unicorn Factory Lisboa's community for startups, companies and researchers building solutions in artificial intelligence, machine learning and data science. With offices in Alvalade, it connects members with corporates, investors, universities and expert mentors through AI-focused workshops, networking sessions and events.
