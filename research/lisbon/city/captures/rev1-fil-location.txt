How to get there
AirportThe Humberto Delgado Airport is located just 4 km ,( approximately 7 minutes) from FIL, providing flights to more than 125 national and international destinations.
View mapPublic TransportationIts proximity to Orient Transit Station allows for quick access to FIL via Train, City Bus, Express bus, Subway (the Red Line), and Taxi.
By carFIL is accessible via Portugal’s main national highways, the A5, and the Ponte Vasco da Gama (The Vasco da Gama Bridge). For your convenience, FIL has an underground parking garage with 830 spaces.
On foot or by bicycleFIL is close to several hotels, making it accessible on foot or by bicycle.
