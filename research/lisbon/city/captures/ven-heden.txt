Venues that make your events stand out
Your next event in Lisbon doesn’t have to feel corporate. Choose the right Heden venue and turn it into an inspiring, engaging experience. We know it has to run flawlessly, and we make sure it does, but why not add a little spice? Our spaces are home to brilliant entrepreneurs, tech companies and exciting start-ups, in iconic places such as the historic Rossio Station. As a certified B Corp, we’re known for our sustainable practices, and we see events as a powerful way to bring teams and communities together.

Where we are
Location is a big part of what we offer. Our spaces sit in some of Lisbon’s best neighbourhoods, and we’re firm believers in the 15-minute city: right outside every Heden you’ll find restaurants, public transport, everyday services and gyms, everything you need for a good balance between work and life.
