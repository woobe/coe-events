FAQSFind the answer you need
What types of event spaces are available at Avila Spaces in Lisbon?
Avila Spaces offers four distinct venue options in central Lisbon: the Blue Ocean Auditorium at Parque das Nações (up to 50 guests, 73 m²), the Atrium Saldanha Auditorium (up to 35 guests, 30 m²), Meeting Rooms across locations (up to 20 guests, 10–40 m²), and the Avila Café (up to 60 guests, 60 m²) — each with natural daylight, modern design, and flexible layouts.
What kinds of events can I host at Avila Spaces?
The spaces are designed for a wide range of corporate events, including conferences, workshops, training sessions, talks, presentations, team-building sessions, networking events, client meetings, breakfasts, cocktails, and happy hours. Each venue can be configured to fit the format of your event.
What AV and technology equipment is included in the event spaces?
Both auditoriums include a complete audiovisual system. The Parque das Nações Auditorium also features a digital LED wall and stage use, with optional video-conferencing setup. Meeting rooms come equipped with a video-conferencing system as standard. Catering and café support are available across all spaces.
Where are the Avila Spaces event venues located in Lisbon?
The event spaces are located across two central Lisbon locations: Parque das Nações (near Oriente Station, 10 minutes from Lisbon Airport) and Atrium Saldanha (next to Saldanha Metro on the Yellow and Red lines) — both easily accessible by public transport.
Is catering available for events hosted at Avila Spaces?
Yes. Café support and catering services are available across all event spaces. The Avila Café in particular offers bar service, cocktails, and full meal options – ideal for team gatherings, client events, and after-event networking moments.
Can I get a turnkey event management service?
Yes. Avila Spaces offers a full turnkey service to help you plan and run your event, so you can focus on the content while the team takes care of the setup and support. Contact us to discuss your event needs and get a tailored proposal.
