Lisbon Campus
The headquarters of the Universidade Católica Portuguesa is located on the Palma de Cima campus, in the center of Lisbon.
It is home to 8 of the 19 faculties of Católica.
In the city of Sintra, the Universidade Católica also has the Sintra campus, where the Católica Medical School is based.
The Católica campi are open to the community. Get to know everything they have to offer on the website.
