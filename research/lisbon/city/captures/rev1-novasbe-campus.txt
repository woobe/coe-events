What we stand for
We understood our role from the very beginning of our journey. And for the past 40 years, we have committed to participating in the positive transformation of our world.
As the world continues to change, we will continue to adapt to its multiple challenges. As the challenges continue - we will stay true to who we are.
We believe we have a commitment to every single person who continues to wonder, discover, create. To all those who keep trying to improve themselves and make meaningful impact.
We believe we should support those who strive to be better: to do better as people, professionals, leaders and as citizens.
Tomorrow as with yesterday, we are a school with a role to play. A role we cannot avoid or delay. We will offer a space to all those who believe in our ability to do better - curious minds and resilient souls. A Space where ideas and ideals can flourish safely, freely.
We are a community of world citizens who want to improve peoples' lives and in doing so, prove our collective potential to keep growing. This is our test and our ultimate driving force.
Dean's Message
At Nova SBE, we leave no student behind and firmly believe that each individual has the potential to make a difference and contribute positively to society through their purpose.
Our commitment is to be a community dedicated to fostering talent and knowledge with a global impact.
Beyond our position in international rankings, we are a school that invests in rigorous academic research with social, economic, and political relevance.
With an outstanding faculty, strategic partnerships with international schools, and renowned companies, we continue to prepare leaders capable of turning challenges into opportunities and contributing to a fairer and more prosperous world, within an inclusive environment that celebrates diverse perspectives.

Knowledge Centers, Institutes & Labs
Digital Data Design Institute at Nova SBE and NOVA Medical School
The Digital Data Design Institute at Nova SBE and NOVA Medical School focuses on integrating data science and artificial intelligence into healthcare and management practices, driving forward the digital transformation of these fields. With a mission to deliver impactful, data-driven solutions, the Institute fosters interdisciplinary collaboration and cutting-edge research, creating new paradigms in education and professional practice. Nova SBE reinforces its commitment to global excellence and societal progress through this initiative by bridging the gap between technology, academia, and practical applications.
Learn more
Nova SBE Behavioral Lab
At Nova SBE Behavioral Lab, teachers, students, and researchers work together to improve the scientific understanding of human behavior. Here, experience meets science to unlock human potential and generate better interactions and approaches between people, companies, and society. Through a holistic perspective, this Lab wants to be a lever for more human-centered business models and decisions.
Learn more
Nova SBE Co.Innovation Lab
Nova SBE Co.Innovation Labis a space for co-creating agile, action-oriented solutions. By facilitating communication between people, companies, industries, and society, the Nova SBE Co.Innovation Lab aims to change the paradigm of "business as usual" and create a lasting impact on the entrepreneurial culture of Portuguese companies.
Learn more
Nova SBE Data, Operations and Technology Knowledge Center

Nova SBE Finance Knowledge Center
Meet the dynamic world of finance with Nova SBE's Finance Knowledge Center – a powerhouse of insights and expertise at the crossroads of academia and industry. Nova SBE Haddad Entrepreneurship Institute

Nova SBE Health Economics & Management Knowledge Center
The Nova SBE Health Economics & Management Knowledge Center is a nexus of innovation where healthcare meets economic insight. In a world where the health sector is undergoing rapid transformation, the center aims to unravel the intricate relationship between economics, management, and the delivery of healthcare services. This knowledge center offers a unique space where theory meets practice and where the business of healthcare is redefined for a healthier tomorrow.

Nova SBE Leadership for Impact Knowledge Center
Step into the realm of transformative leadership with Nova SBE's Leadership for Impact Knowledge Center – a dynamic space where leadership transcends traditional boundaries. Committed to cultivating leaders who drive positive change, this center merges rigorous academic research with practical insights, preparing individuals to make a meaningful impact in their organizations and communities.

Nova SBE NOVAFRICA Knowledge Center
Welcome to the Nova SBE NOVAFRICA Knowledge Center – an intellectual powerhouse dedicated to unlocking Africa's economic and social potential. At the intersection of rigorous research and actionable insights, the Nova SBE NOVAFRICA Knowledge Center stands as a beacon for understanding and addressing the unique challenges and opportunities across the African continent, thriving on a multidisciplinary approach, bringing together scholars, policymakers, and practitioners to foster innovative solutions for sustainable development.

Nova SBE Open & User Innovation Knowledge Center
Meet the Nova SBE Open & User Innovation Knowledge Center – an incubator of ideas where collaboration and creativity converge. At the heart of the evolving innovation landscape, the center is dedicated to exploring the transformative power of open and user-driven approaches. Through cutting-edge research, collaborative projects, and interactive workshops, they foster an environment where ideas are generated and thrive.
Learn more
Nova SBE Public Policy Institute
The Nova SBE Public Policy Institute is an innovative platform to reshape how we tackle modern public policy challenges. With a focus on critical areas such as public finance, housing, productivity, and leveraging artificial intelligence in public administration, the Institute employs a multidisciplinary approach to craft impactful, evidence-based solutions. Positioned as a neutral and collaborative space, it bridges academia, government, and civil society through four pillars: high-quality research, educational programs, practical initiatives, and strategic communication.

Nova SBE Westmont Institute of Tourism & Hospitality
As a beacon in Tourism & Hospitality, this institute is dedicated to advancing knowledge, fostering innovation, and shaping future leaders in tourism and hospitality. Through a comprehensive blend of research, industry collaborations, and hands-on learning experiences, the Nova SBE Westmont Institute of Tourism & Hospitality equips individuals with the skills and insights needed to navigate the evolving landscape of global tourism.

Strategic Iniciatives
Estoril Conferences
Yearly and with a two-day agenda focused on global challenges, the Estoril Conferences aim to promote an intergenerational dialogue between international leaders and younger generations to inspire the co-creation of effective, solution-focused partnerships for a more inclusive and sustainable future.
Learn more
Finance For All
The Finanças Para Todos (Finance For All) program was born out of the deep conviction that universities play a fundamental role in providing services to civil society, especially concerning knowledge and information gaps in relevant areas, such as finance. The creation of this project also comes as a contribution from the Nova SBE Finance Knowledge Center to the achievement of the 2030 Agenda on the Sustainable Development Goals, namely goals 4 - Quality Education, 8 - Decent Work and Economic Growth, and 10 - Reducing Inequalities.
