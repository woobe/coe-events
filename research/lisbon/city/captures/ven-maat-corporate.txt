FAQ
Accordion block item
Title
1. Does MAAT provide catering services or recommend suppliers?
Yes, MAAT can recommend certified suppliers for catering, decoration, technical production, and other services. There is no exclusivity, but we prefer to work with partners who comply with the museum’s technical and heritage requirements.
Accordion block item
Title
2. Are the venues available outside the museum’s regular opening hours?
Yes. Many events take place outside public visiting hours, subject to prior approval and a pre-agreed logistics plan.
Accordion block item
Title
3. Can I visit the spaces before booking?
Yes, our team can arrange a technical visit to view the available spaces and discuss the specific requirements of your event.
Accordion block item
Title
4. Does the venue hire include technical equipment or furniture?
The hire may include certain basic equipment, depending on the space booked. Additional equipment can be hired separately.
Accordion block item
Title
5. What types of events can be held at MAAT?
MAAT’s spaces are highly versatile and can host:
- Brand activations and product launches
- Conferences, seminars, and corporate breakfasts
- Cultural events such as concerts (with sound limitations), exhibitions, and performances
- Social corporate events such as dinners, cocktail receptions, and gatherings
- Outdoor events such as markets and wellness activities
Accordion block item
Title
6. Are there any technical or logistical restrictions?
Due to the historic and architectural nature of MAAT, especially MAAT Central, the following must be considered:
- Sound levels must be limited to 80 dB to protect the century-old structure
- Proper oversight is required for installation and decoration to preserve the site
- Any major alterations to the spaces must receive prior approval
The MAAT team is available to assist with all technical and logistical aspects throughout the planning and execution of your event, ensuring its success.
Venue regulations
To safeguard MAAT's industrial, architectural and artistic heritage, all events held on its premises are subject to specific regulations and procedures.
Please consult the regulations concerning the protection of exhibitions, artworks and heritage assets before planning your event.
