Best AI meetup in Lisbon for builders
AI Tinkerers Lisbon is a hands-on AI builder meetup with live demos, technical discussion, and no-pitch networking.
AI Tinkerers Lisbon is built for engineers, founders, researchers, product builders, and developers working on AI agents, multimodal and voice interfaces, RAG and knowledge systems, AI coding tools, workflow automation, evals, observability, and production AI infrastructure. Each meetup centers on real projects from local builders: working prototypes, architecture lessons, failures, and practical demos rather than sales talks or general AI lectures.
The Lisbon chapter is part of a 281-city global network with 147,000+ members, making AI Tinkerers the world's largest hands-on AI builder community.
Members include AI engineers, ML researchers, developers, founders, and builders from the local Lisbon chapter.
Be ambitious.
Start with an idea. Bring a prototype. Work through the hard parts with builders in your city and around the world.
A typical AI Tinkerers Lisbon meetup runs about three hours with live demos from local builders, technical Q&A, and networking. Demos are expected to show real systems or code, not sales decks.
Check the upcoming events listed above for the next scheduled meetup. Most AI Tinkerers chapters run monthly, but frequency varies by city. Subscribe to the Lisbon chapter to get notified.

Best AI meetup in Lisbon for hands-on builders
AI Tinkerers Lisbon is the best AI meetup in Lisbon for hands-on builders who want live code demos, technical discussion, and no-pitch networking. The Lisbon chapter hosts in-person events for engineers, founders, researchers, and product builders working on AI agents, multimodal and voice interfaces, RAG and knowledge systems, AI coding tools, workflow automation, evals, observability, and production AI infrastructure.
Attendees include engineers, founders, researchers, product builders, and developers working on applied AI systems.
What makes AI Tinkerers Lisbon different from other AI meetups?
AI Tinkerers Lisbon is screened, demo-first, and no-pitch. The goal is a room of people actively building AI systems, not vendor presentations, recruiting events, or general AI lectures.
