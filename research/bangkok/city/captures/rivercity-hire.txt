Lease or Hire a Venue
Leasable spaces range from 850 sqm to 120 sqm, with flexible opportunities to combine galleries. In addition to medium to long-term leasing, there are a number of venues for short-term hire including 2nd floor RCB Gallerias and RCB Forum, ground floor RCB Artery and RCB Rooftop Helipad. The centre is serviced by full-time security, cleaning and a concierge desk at the ground floor entrance. There is parking for 500 cars.
The in-house marketing team promotes galleries through River City Bangkok’s digital platforms and with media and their journalists. Promotions and events are staged each month to attract collectors and art lovers to the centre.
The auction house, RCB Auctions, holds live auctions each year in the 4th floor auction room, and online to bidders around the world, powered by Invaluable international auction platform.

208 sqm | 1st Floor
A spectacular black and white tiled four-story space at the center of River City Bangkok is a venue for live performance, pop-up events, press conferences and official celebrations, with direct access from the front entrance, riverfront piers and the car park.

RCB Rooftop
Multi-purpose Venue
400 sqm 5th Floor
RCB Rooftop is a favourite venue for spectacular events. From the roof of River City Bangkok you get the most beautiful panoramic view. This is a truly unique venue for rooftop screenings, concerts, performance, pop-up fairs or that special product launch. With access from the car park, the rooftop is an idea location for a car-boot market. What better place for partygoers and night crawlers to enjoy mellow yellow sky at sunset, sip some crafted beers, watch dinner cruises floating by or take some shots of New Year’s breathtaking fireworks. Under the boundless sky, this multi-functional space is open for any creativity against the backdrop of urban living.
