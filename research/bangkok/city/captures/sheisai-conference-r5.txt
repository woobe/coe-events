Questions, answered.
What is AMPLIFY '27?
AMPLIFY '27 is the global summit of SHE IS AI, the women-led ethical AI education platform. It brings together an all-women line-up of AI experts, an AI art and film exhibition, and an expo of 40 ethical business partners in Bangkok, Thailand, in September 2027.
When and where is it?
Bangkok, Thailand, from 23 to 25 September 2027, with the main summit day on Friday 24 September. Dates and venue are being confirmed, and the waitlist hears first.
Who is it for?
Women who want to use and lead AI ethically: founders, leaders, educators, creatives and corporate teams, along with the organisations that back them.
What does human-led AI mean?
The person leads and the tool assists. Ethics, governance and personal judgement are built in from the start, and AI is shaped around the way each person works.
Is the speaker line-up all women?
Yes. Every speaker on the main stage is a woman working in AI, including researchers, founders, educators, ethicists, artists and builders.
How do I apply to speak?
Use the speaker application on this page. Keynotes, fireside chats, hands-on workshops, panels and AI art or film submissions are all welcome.
How can my organisation sponsor or exhibit?
Request the partner pack on this page. Options include a Presenting Partner, Gold and Sage partnerships, expo booths with startup rates for women-led ventures, and funding or community partnerships.
Can I attend online?
Yes. The main stage is planned to be livestreamed globally, with recordings available inside the SHE IS AI Inside Out community.
