ABOUT TRUE DIGITAL PARK
Integrating Work and Lifestyle Under One Roof
True Digital Park is Southeast Asia’s largest tech and startup hub, spanning over 230,000 sqm and located in the South Sukhumvit district of Bangkok, next to BTS Punnawithi. This vibrant community is a key driver in developing Thailand’s startup and innovation ecosystem, seamlessly connecting business and lifestyle. With workplaces, event spaces, and shops all under one roof, you can work, shop, eat, play and learn in one place.
