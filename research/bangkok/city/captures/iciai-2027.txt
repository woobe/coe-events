Conference overview
★ Sponsored by National Institute of Development Administration, Thailand, 2027 the 11th International Conference on Innovation in Artificial Intelligence (ICIAI) will be will take place in Bangkok, Thailand, from April 26 to 29, 2027. The conference will bring together an international community of experts to exchange cutting-edge research findings, discuss emerging trends, and explore innovative applications in artificial intelligence, mechatronics, robotics, control, and automation.
The Conference Co-Chairs are Prof. Steven Guan (Sichuan University - Auckland Institute, China) and Prof. Chutisant Kerdvibulvech (National Institute of Development Administration, Thailand).
★ Publication:
After a careful reviewing process, all accepted papers after proper registration and presentation, will be published in Lecture Notes in Electrical Engineering as a proceedings book volume. Springer will conduct quality checks on the accepted papers and only papers that pass these checks will be published. Indexed by SCOPUS and EI Compendex. All books published in the series are submitted for consideration in Web of Science.

★Call for papers★
Topics of particular interest for submissions include, but are not limited to the following areas:
**Core AI & Technology
-Artificial Intelligence and Technology
-Supervised and Unsupervised Learning
**Machine Learning & Data Science
-Natural Language Processing (NLP)
-Computer Vision
**Intelligent Systems & Optimization
-Robotics and Related Fields
-Multi-agent Systems
**Computational Intelligence & Fuzzy Systems
-Fuzzy Logic
-Fuzzy Control
**Parallel Computing & Hardware
-Concurrent and Parallel Processing
-Hardware Implementations (e.g., AI accelerators)
**Applied AI Domains
-Bioinformatics
-Artificial Intelligence in Modeling and Simulation
For more information, please visit the webpage: https://iciai.org/html/cfp.html
