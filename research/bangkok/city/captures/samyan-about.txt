ABOUT SAMYAN MITRTOWN
Spanning over 300,000 square meters, Samyan Mitrtown is the first mixed-use development on Rama 4 Road, situated in the legendary Samyan district of Bangkok. Samyan is a renowned historical community famed for its culinary offerings, seamlessly connected to the bustling 'Street Food' scene of Banthat Thong, situated less than one kilometer away. This is the long-time popular check-in destination for tourists and savvy locals eager to savor the delectable offerings from renowned eateries lining both sides of the thoroughfare.
Samyan Mitrtown is conveniently located near Chulalongkorn University, the first institute of higher learning in Thailand, along with prominent office buildings. Hence, it's no surprise that Samyan Mitrtown is set to become a trendy community hub, capturing the hearts of urbanites and city dwellers, catering to diverse lifestyles in every aspect. From convenience in transportation to living the urban life, Samyan Mitrtown seamlessly connects to business districts like Silom, Sathorn, and various key locations or popular tourist spots, easily accessible by the MRT subway or via Rama 4 Road and Phaya Thai, two of the main arteries of Bangkok.
To reach Samyan Mitrtown via the MRT subway system, passengers get off at Samyan Station and take exit number 2 where they are greeted by a trendy underground walkway that directly connects to the shopping center, enhancing convenience for Shopper. One can walk comfortably in a safe atmosphere at all times no matter the weather from Samyan Station. Only two stations away from Samyan Station is Yaowarat Station, and Silom Station is only one station away. If traveling by road, the Si Rat Expressway entrance is only 1.6 km away, while Chalerm Maha Nakhon Expressway is 3 km away. Coming down Phaya Thai Road from Siam, Samyan Mitrtown is on your right at the intersection with Rama 4 Road.
SAMYAN MITRTOWN CONCEPT
Samyan Mitrtown perfectly caters to the lifestyle of the 5.0 generation, embodying the concept of the Urban Life Library, or the 'Hub of Food and Learning.' Encompassing the entire spectrum of work, eat, rest, and learning, it is not only a shopping center; with its close proximity to educational institutions, Samyan Mitrtown serves as a destination hub for students to create and share new experiences together."
NEVER SLEEP MITR –24hr ALIVE ATRIUM
A special zone that operates 24 hours a day, catering to a variety of lifestyles, including night owls and digital nomads. From co-learning spaces for those seeking a quiet place to read, to spaces to get work done, to contemporary meeting rooms, supermarkets, pharmacies, restaurants, and over 18 eateries. Additionally, for those with busy schedules who want to stay fit, there is a gym to work out at anytime 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
C asean Samyan CO-OP
This 'Co-Learning Space' is open 24/7, catering to urbanites who seek a 'learning space'. It's a place to gather new knowledge, share ideas, move businesses forward, and develop various life skills throughout the day and night. The space is divided into 4 zones based on usage:
Focus Zone: Glass-enclosed rooms located at the center of the area, quiet and suitable for concentration.
Collaborate Zone: Designed for group work or reading together.
Socialize Zone: A place for social interaction.
Learning Zone: Accommodating various events and workshops.
The Sky Reading Zone is one of the highlighted spaces, featuring counters with a panoramic view of Bangkok, perfect for those looking for a moment to unwind as they work. The Amphitheatre provides a crescent-shaped seating for users to comfortably work as they wish, creating a vibrant atmosphere. Private rooms of various sizes are available for booking at no cost.
With meeting rooms, a cafe, and monthly workshops and activities, this space can accommodate more than 500 visitors. Located on the 2nd floor, in the 24-hour zone of Samyan Mitrtown, and open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Medium and More
A hub that gathers ART&CRAFT and KITCHEN ART products like no other in the heart of the city. Dive into the fun of creating 'Art & Craft' blended with products under the 'Kitchen Art' concept that stands out from the rest. Offering a comprehensive range of art supplies, learning materials, and crafts, this is the ultimate creative destination in Bangkok, spanning 1,525 square meters.
Located on the 3rd floor of Samyan Mitrtown, operating hours are from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily.

Samyan Food Legends
A space that brings together renowned eateries carefully curated from the best in Samyan, the legends of flavor in this area are gathered in one place so you don't have to spend a lot of time searching. Featuring over 17 establishments along with an Outdoor zone for dining with a stunning city view.
This dining area accommodates over 2,500 visitors daily within an area of more than 1,275 square meters. Situated on the 4th floor of Samyan Mitrtown, operating hours are from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily.
Samyan Mitrtown Hall
A new choice for organizing events, concerts, and various meetings, complete with facilities to fulfill every function. This comprehensive event space on the 5th floor of Samyan Mitrtown spans over 5,000 square meters. Samyan Mitrtown Hall can accommodate an audience of more than 3,000, featuring a main hall that can be divided into two, accommodating up to 1,500 pax each, along with one function room accommodating 200 pax and 5 smaller meeting rooms for 30-60 pax.
The Hall can cater to a variety of event types, from small meetings to exhibitions, with flexible spaces tailored to suit individual needs. Strategically located in the heart of the city, with direct mass transit connection from the MRT Samyan Station to the shopping center fully equipped with amenities, restaurants, and services, some of which are open 24 hours, seven days a week
House Samyan
This offbeat movie theater located in the heart of the city follows the concept of 'a space within a cinephile friend's home' for watching movies. It is a place that delights cinema enthusiasts, showing alternative films for those seeking a different movie experience. House Samyan has a total of three theaters, with a beautiful corner café to enjoy.
Located on the 5th floor of Samyan Mitrtown.
Sky Garden
On the 5th floor of Samyan Mitrtown is floating beauty with a panoramic view in the heart of the city, reminiscent of a dream. The Sky Garden is a tranquil spot perfect for relaxation and recharging for all ages. Featuring many beautiful corners offering various seating options for work, relaxation, or engaging in creative activities to stimulate learning or living. Amidst the beautiful atmosphere of Bangkok, it offers a welcome alternative to the bustle of shopping.
The Sky Garden can accommodate up to 200 people within an area of 4,500 square meters, and is offered as a public space that aims to give back to the community."
