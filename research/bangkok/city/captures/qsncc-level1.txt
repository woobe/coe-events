Layout and Capacities
The First Floor has four ballrooms and four plenary halls. The ballroom is inspired by Chut Chao Khao Thai or Thai hill tribe costume, which portrays nature through stripes and geometric shapes. The silvery decorative items represent silver accessories, a popular ornament worn by hill tribe people. The Chut Thai Chakri, one of Thai Phra Rajaniyom or Thai outfits of royal favor, inspired the design of the Plenary Hall. The designer enlarged the simplified "Pra Jam Yam," a basic Thai traditional decorative pattern, and added the exquisite pattern from the Chut Thai Chakri dress's brocade to enhance the contemporary and timeless feel. The small meeting rooms overlook the lake and the lush greenery of Benjakitti Park.
