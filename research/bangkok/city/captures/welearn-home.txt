HOW WE LEARN
Two engines, one goal
Project-Based Learning with Coaches
12-week projects such as Formula 1 in Schools, robotics, designing an AI learning buddy and a start-up studio, guided by coaches who facilitate rather than lecture. Learners present their work to an audience at the end.
Explore PBL programmes
Academic Tutoring & Mentoring
Structured support for IGCSE and US Diploma pathways, plus a Mastery Transcript Consortium learning record of the skills they earn. Daily Focus Hub sessions keep academics on track while projects bring them to life.

YOUR CHILD’S JOURNEY
How personalized learning progresses from age 11 to 18
Whether your child joins at 11 to rebuild confidence and love for learning, or at 15 with university credentials in sight, their pathway is mapped 1-on-1 with their mentor.
Starting at 11Starting at 15
Years 1–2 · Ages 11–13
Learning how to learn
Self-management, research skills, and presenting come first. Your child discovers genuine interests through hands-on projects — robotics, design, storytelling — and rebuilds confidence, especially if traditional school knocked it down.
Years 3–5 · Ages 13–16
The accredited pathway begins
IGCSE subjects via CambriLearn or US Diploma credits via Edmentum, at their own pace. The Mastery Portfolio grows with real, evidenced work.
Years 6–7 · Ages 16–18
Credential + portfolio
Qualifications completed, capstone projects led. Your child graduates with an accredited credential plus a portfolio that shows who they are — ready for university applications.
First term
Pathway assessment
Where are they academically? What do they love? Which credential fits — IGCSE exam prep or US Diploma credit accumulation? A personal learning plan is set with their mentor.
Years 1–2
Focused progress
Daily coaching drives academic momentum, alongside 2–3 substantial portfolio projects tied to their intended field.
Year 3 · Ages 17–18
Graduation, their way
Final credentials, capstone project, and university or career applications with mentor support — graduating on the same timeline as their traditional-school peers, with a portfolio they can't match.
