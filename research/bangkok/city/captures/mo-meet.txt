Expert Planning
An Iconic Riverside Setting
Overlooking the Chao Phraya River, our venues provide a captivating setting for special occasions. From elegant banquets to milestone celebrations, the glittering riverfront creates an unforgettable backdrop.
Bespoke Culinary Experiences
Treat your guests to exceptional cuisine prepared by our award-winning chefs. Bespoke menus, international buffets and elegant set courses are created with carefully sourced ingredients and presented with artistry.
Your Dedicated Event Team
Our dedicated Meetings & Events Specialists are with you at every stage, from initial concept to final detail. Combining expertise with creativity, they ensure each event is personal, seamless and memorable.
Sustainability
Our approach to sustainability is guided by long-term stewardship. We have eliminated 99% of single-use plastics, prioritise responsibly sourced ingredients and invest in energy efficiency and waste reduction to help preserve the natural environment.
Frequently Asked Questions
What types of meetings can be hosted at Mandarin Oriental, Bangkok?
Mandarin Oriental, Bangkok offers a selection of venues suitable for a wide range of events, from intimate board meetings and seminars to larger conferences and networking gatherings.
Are audiovisual and technical facilities available?
Yes. All meeting and event spaces are equipped with modern audiovisual technology, including presentation screens, sound systems, and high-speed connectivity.
Can catering be arranged for meetings and corporate events?
Yes. Mandarin Oriental, Bangkok offers bespoke catering for meetings and corporate events.
Can multi-day meetings or conferences be supported?
Yes. Mandarin Oriental, Bangkok is fully equipped to support multi-day meetings and conferences.
Can accommodation be arranged for meeting attendees?
Yes. Mandarin Oriental, Bangkok is pleased to arrange accommodation for meeting and conference attendees.
