From the elaborate to the intimate, the traditional to the unconventional, a ceremony in one of our beautiful Sukhumvit, Bangkok wedding venues exceeds your expectations. With multiple event spaces, catering menus, Indian wedding planners and Thai packages, our hotel ensures the small details are covered.
