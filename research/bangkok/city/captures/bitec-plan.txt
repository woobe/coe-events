Customer Experience Services
Seamless Business & Visitor Support
BITEC is conveniently accessible by public bus services operating from key districts across Bangkok. With multiple routes running along Sukhumvit Road and Bangna–Trad Road, visitors can travel to the venue efficiently and reliably throughout the day.
Bus services provide a practical option for visitors travelling from nearby business areas, residential zones, and transport interchanges. Clear access points and designated entrances ensure smooth arrival and easy movement into the exhibition and event areas.
From Sukhumvit Road, bus routes 102, 116, 129, 142, 184, 2, 23, 25, 45, 507, 508, 511, 536, 544, and 545 provide access to BITEC via Entrance 3.
From Bangna–Trad Road, bus routes 132, 139, 180, 38, 46, 48, 552, and 558 serve the venue, with access via Entrances 1 and 2.
