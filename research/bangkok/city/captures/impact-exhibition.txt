IMPACT Exhibition Center
IMPACT Exhibition and Convention Center
IMPACT Muang Thong Thani is composed of IMPACT Arena, IMPACT Exhibition and Convention Center, IMPACT Challenger, IMPACT Forum Hall 4, and The Portal Lifestyle Complex.
IMPACT Exhibition and Convention Center contains 8 multi-purpose halls with a combined space of 47,000 square meters and can be converted or partitioned into bigger or smaller spaces.
The pillar-free Halls 5 - 8 offer 20,000 square meters of usable space (5,000 square meters/hall) and can handle large-scale exhibitions while Halls 9 - 12 encompass 27,000 square meters and can be converted into a 13,500-square-meter space for smaller events.
