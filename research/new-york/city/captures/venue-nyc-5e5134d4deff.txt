About this space
A long, rectangular glass-fronted meeting room featuring carpeted floors, patterned ceiling panels and contemporary finishes. The layout provides a clear focal wall and abundant natural light from the exterior glazing.
Room features: The room provides a ceiling-mounted projector, wall-mounted flatscreen, PA speakers and conference-call facilities; a whiteboard and flipchart are available for facilitated sessions.
Best for: Presentations, instructor-led training and team briefings.
