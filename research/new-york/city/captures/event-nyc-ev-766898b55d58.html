Questions & Answers

01

Where is the venue?

The Glasshouse, 660 12th Ave, New York, NY 10019. A stunning glass-walled venue in Manhattan with panoramic views of the Hudson River.

02

Who should attend TokenizeThis?

Finance professionals, asset managers, tokenization platform builders, legal and compliance experts, and investors interested in the intersection of traditional finance and blockchain technology.

03

How do I request refunds or contact organizers?

Email TokenizeThis@RedStone.Finance for any inquiries. Please note that all ticket sales are final and non-refundable.

TokenizeThis features VIP parties, after-party events, and curated networking sessions. Attendees connect with executives from leading financial institutions, crypto protocols, and legal firms.
