About this space
The room is a modern, carpeted meeting space arranged with movable desks in clusters and red swivel chairs for small-group working. A full-height cityscape mural and a slatted wood front wall give the space a contemporary, professional appearance.
Room features: Audio-visual systems support presentations and remote participation, including a projector, PA speakers and conference-call facilities. Climate control is provided by air conditioning and the room is designated as a quiet space for focused work.
Best for: Instructor-led training, collaborative workshops and meetings that require remote dial-in and presentation-capable facilities.
What sets it apart: A sculpted, contoured recessed ceiling light runs across the length of the room and provides even, glare-reduced illumination for long sessions.
