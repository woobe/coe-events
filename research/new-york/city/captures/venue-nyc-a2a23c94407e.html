About this space

Secret Garden is a private outdoor terrace in SAINT characterised by an enclosed, courtyard-like layout that creates an intimate outdoor room.

Design: The space pairs patterned tiled flooring with wrought-iron bistro tables, low wooden benches and exposed brick walls punctuated by mirrors and planted greenery, producing a compact, urbane courtyard look.

Features: The terrace provides clustered round tables and bench seating on a tiled floor, built-in PA speakers and venue Wi‑Fi, and allows own music or a hired DJ. The area is wheelchair accessible with a step-free entrance and accessible toilets nearby.

Ideal for: Small groups seeking a relaxed, social setting suitable for standing receptions or seated small‑plate gatherings.

What sets it apart: A covered, ivy-draped trellis strung with festoon lights transforms the terrace into a sheltered, atmospheric outdoor room.

What guests say: The space is intimate and welcoming, and the staff are attentive and adaptable when plans change.
