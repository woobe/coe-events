About this space

The Speakeasy Loft is an inviting, 2,500 square-foot private event space situated in the heart of Midtown Manhattan's Garment District. This highly sought-after venue is characterized by its warm, intimate speakeasy vibes with stylish touches of European and industrial influences. The space features classic exposed brick walls, polished hardwood floors, and plush furniture, lending an inviting ambiance perfectly suited for a wide variety of gatherings. We can comfortably seat up to 75 guests or host up to 125 standing guests, making it ideal for cocktail parties, networking events, after parties, holiday parties, birthday celebrations, office parties, bar/bat mitzvahs, or even a bachelor/bachelorette party. Our comprehensive amenities ensure that you have everything you need to host an unforgettable event!

Host rules

Alcohol Consumption: A bar package or bar buyout is required if you are serving drinks of any kind. Your options are:
1) Purchase a LOFT39 bar package
2) Provide your own "DIY open bar" for a buyout fee of $8 per person (no sale of alcohol allowed, all alcohol must be purchased from a licensed retailer) Contracting a third-party bar vendor or selling alcohol is strictly prohibited.
3) Host an event without a bar. No bar buyout fee applies in this case.

Crucial Regulations:
Contracting a third-party bar vendor or selling alcohol (directly or indirectly) is strictly prohibited.
Regardless of your bar choice, if alcohol is present, all guests must be 21 years or older unless otherwise approved by LOFT39 in writing.
The client is responsible for ensuring compliance with all local and state alcohol laws. LOFT39 reserves the right to deny service or remove guests in violation of these terms.

Smoking Prohibited: Smoking inside is strictly prohibited, including cigarettes, cigars, vaping, and other devices.

Decorations: No glitter, sparklers, or confetti. Live candles must be covered. Nothing may be fixed to the walls, ceilings, or fixtures.

Vendor and Delivery Access: Vendors must adhere to the agreed setup and breakdown times. Deliveries must be coordinated in advance and comply with building policies.
LOFT39 offers a curated list of preferred vendors for entertainment, party rentals, A/V & lighting, and planning services. Please note that all vendors, whether preferred or outside, must be licensed and insured to work within the venue.
You are welcome to bring in an outside caterer. However, a catering buyout fee of $2 per person will be applied. This fee helps cover the additional coordination and resources required when working with external food arrangements. Any outside caterer must be a full-service, licensed, and insured company.

All events require event insurance and a credit card on file.

Cancellation policy: Standard 30 day with Grace Period
