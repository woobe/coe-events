Home Attend Register Venue Speakers Agenda Workshop Short Papers Innovation Challenge Embodied AI and Remote Sensing for Sustainable, Safe Connected Cities (EARS-CONN) Conference Agenda Two plenary days at the Grove School of Engineering, The City College of New York. Day 1 builds the sensing and embodied-AI foundations of Digital-Earth. Day 2 turns to urban applications and closes with the Innovation Stage, where selected startup and innovation applicants pitch for a prize. The hands-on workshop is a separate program on January 21–22. Conference days: January 19–20, 2027 Venue: Grove School of Engineering, CCNY Format: One plenary track Day 1 Day 2 and Innovation Stage Times are provisional. A downloadable PDF will be posted on this page later. Names marked “to be announced” are open slots.
