Marquee Events Center
Elevated Symposium Spaces

The Craig Newmark Auditorium is the newest and most exciting event and conferencing center in New York City. The cutting-edge venue accommodates a wide range of configurations – from a 250-person gala dinner or 300+ keynote address, to a 500+ standing cocktail party. From stately architecture to state-of-the-art streaming cameras and video wall, the Craig Newmark Auditorium provides flexibility to clients of all types. wide range of meetings and events.

Special rates for nonprofits, community organizations, Civic Hall members and Zero Irving tenants are available.

This centerpiece event space is made possible through an extremely generous donation by Craig Newmark, the Founder of Craigslist and Craig Newmark Philanthropies.
