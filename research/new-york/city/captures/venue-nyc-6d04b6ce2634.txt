About this space
The hall features an open layout, a fixed stage, DJ booth and space for dining, dancing and decorations.
The venue is suitable for birthdays, baby showers, wedding receptions, Sweet 16s, quinceañeras, graduations, family celebrations and corporate events. Facilities include tables and chairs, event lighting, a bar area, refrigeration, a food preparation area with a sink, Wi-Fi, heating and air conditioning.
Guests can bring their own catering, decorations and entertainment, subject to venue policies. Standard bookings include six hours of event time plus two hours for setup, with optional linens, tableware, buffet arrangements and staffing available.

RIDGEWOOD HALL PRICING
Private venue rental for up to 120 guests:
• Monday–Thursday: $995
• Friday or Sunday: $1,595
• Saturday: $1,995
• Saturday daytime special: $1,495 when your event ends by 5 PM—a $500 discount on the Saturday venue rate.
ADDITIONAL FEES
• Cleaning fee: $95 per event.
• Sales tax: 8.875%, applied to the rental and applicable fees.
• Bartender: $175 flat fee when alcohol is served. Our venue-provided bartender is required; outside bartenders and guest self-service are not permitted.
• Optional services and upgrades are priced separately. Please request pricing for sound equipment, linens, tableware, buffet setup, and additional staffing.
BOOKING DEPOSIT
A $1,000 booking deposit is required and is applied toward your total.
Outside food and caterers are welcome. There is no required per-person catering package.
Security is mandatory for every event. Your itemized quote will confirm security arrangements and all applicable charges.
Contact us through Tagvenue to confirm availability and receive a complete quote for your date and event requirements.

Host rules
• Maximum capacity is 120 guests.
• Outside food and caterers are welcome. The venue does not provide food.
• Alcohol must be served by our venue-provided bartender. Outside bartenders and guest self-service are not permitted.
• Security is required for every event and must be coordinated with venue management.
• Confetti, glitter, and fog machines are not permitted.
• Obtain management approval before hanging decorations from the ceiling.
• The preparation area is for food preparation only. It is not a commercial kitchen and has no stove, gas line, or food warmers.
• All decorations, personal belongings, and vendor equipment must be removed by the end of your booked time.
• Saturday daytime bookings using the brunch discount must end by 5 PM.
