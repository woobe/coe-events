About this space

As you step through the low-key entrance on the ground floor, you'll be instantly transported to a breathtaking paradise, complete with 30-foot high ceilings and lush greenery all around.

The space is adorned with warm and vibrant colors, giving it a magical feel that is complemented by the beautiful and welcoming staff. The elaborate custom high-tech lighting and sound system adds to the ambiance, ensuring that you feel the positive vibe the moment you arrive.

Plus, with ample custom bathrooms and dedicated attendants, large crowds can move freely while experiencing top-notch service in every corner of the room.

As a versatile venue, La Victoria offers a range of options for buyouts or semi-private parties. The flexible furniture layout allows you to create the perfect atmosphere for your event, whether you're looking for an intimate gathering or a larger celebration.

VIP tables can accommodate up to 11 guests, and there's even an elevated super VIP section that overlooks the dance floor. With such an exclusive setup, you're sure to feel like a VIP all night long.

So, whether you're planning a corporate event, a birthday celebration, or a social gathering, La Victoria is the perfect venue for your next event.

Very poor communication

My experience with La Victoria NYC was absolutely terrible from start to finish. I booked this venue 2 weeks in advance for my 21st birthday, paid my deposit, confirmed every detail, and had guests flying in from out of state. Just 3 days before the event, the venue suddenly tried to add new fees and conditions that were never included in the original agreement which is 100% ILLEGAL. When I raised valid concerns, the communication became dismissive and unprofessional. They showed no empathy, no accountability, and no respect for my time or investment. To make matters worse, some of the comments made by Robert during our conversations were extremely inappropriate and made me feel profiled and unwelcome. No professional venue should operate this way. Their lack of transparency, poor communication, and disrespectful behavior are completely unacceptable. I strongly recommend that anyone planning an event stay far away from La Victoria NYC. There are many venues in New York that treat their clients with integrity, respect, and professionalism — this is not one of them.
