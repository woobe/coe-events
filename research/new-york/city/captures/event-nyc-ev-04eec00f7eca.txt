Sandra Cai Founder Session: TBA Sandra Cai is building Plurall AI at the intersection of urgency and inevitability. While most people were still treating deepfakes as a novelty, Cai was already tracking a very different trajectory. The rise of synthetic media was not just a technical evolution, it was a structural threat to trust itself. As fraud attempts surged and detection became increasingly unreliable to human judgment, she saw a gap forming that would soon become one of the defining cybersecurity challenges of the decade. That realization became Plurall AI. From the beginning, Cai chose to build the core detection infrastructure from first principles rather than relying on existing models. The result was a proprietary multimodal system capable of identifying deepfakes in under two seconds with high accuracy, designed specifically for enterprise and real-world deployment environments where speed and reliability are critical What distinguishes her approach is not only the technical depth, but the conviction behind it. Early in the company’s journey, Plurall AI received a seven-figure acquisition offer. She declined it. For Cai, the problem was never about a fast exit or incremental validation. It was about building the foundational standard for digital trust before the scale of the problem outpaced the tools designed to solve it. The market response has begun to validate that conviction. Plurall AI has secured its first enterprise customer, signed a letter of intent with another, and built a pipeline of more than 100 B2B leads, alongside strong organic consumer interest. The company was also recognized by TechCrunch as one of the Top 32 Enterprise Tech Startups at Startup Battlefield, signaling early credibility on a global stage. Backed by partners across NVIDIA, IBM, AWS, and NYU’s entrepreneurial ecosystem, Plurall AI sits within a rapidly expanding need state: organizations and individuals alike searching for reliable verification in an increasingly synthetic world. For Sandra Cai, the mission is not abstract. It is immediate. She is not building a feature inside the AI ecosystem.
