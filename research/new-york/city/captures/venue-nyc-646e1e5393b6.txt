About this space
This raw open space on our main level has endless potential and can be transformed into the space of your dreams for larger events.
DCTV’s French Renaissance landmark former firehouse circa 1895 is conveniently located in Tribeca/Chinatown and offers unique and versatile multi-use space rentals for: community events, film/photo/green screen shoots, production holding/catering, art exhibits, concerts, parties, dance/theater performances, product launches, lectures, rehearsals, screenings, and fundraisers
It features a 4,500 square foot flexible space with discreet lobby access, street-side load-in and ramp, customizable stage decks/risers, blackout capability, wi-fi, additional side rooms, 4K projector and screen, pro-audio sound system and color-adjustable LED light fixtures.
Engine Bay adds character to your event and is adjacent to the event space with garage-level access that can be transformed into a bar/lounge. The engine bay is perfect for holding and catering, and the outdoor alleyway is included with the rental.
Make your event unforgettable at DCTV!
