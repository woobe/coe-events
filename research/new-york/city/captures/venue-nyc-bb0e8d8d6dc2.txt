About this space
The Sonnet is a 51-seat theater and private screening room at Producers' Club Theaters, 358 West 44th Street, steps from Times Square, with about 680 sq ft of space.
For screenings, premieres and presentations: a new 200-inch screen with a true 4K projector, 7.1 surround sound and a Mac mini. Bring your film on a hard drive or USB drive, or play it from Dropbox, Google Drive or Vimeo.
Great for plays, comedy, readings, film premieres, festival screenings, product launches, company events and private parties.
