About this space
Room I is situated on the 6th floor of Jay Suites Times Square. It is a large, modern meeting room with additional seating on the benches.
It has sustainable wood laminate conference tables & matching serving credenzas. Ergonomic black leather executive designer chairs provide your employees or business partners convenience even during long meetings.

As you may need some time to get settled in and prepare for your meeting, please do allow for preparation time in your reservation.
When arriving at our center, you will see your booking on TV screens outside the meeting room. You will also see whether the room is booked immediately following your meeting. If it is, we ask that you be respectful to your peers and courteous in vacating the room in a timely manner. However, if the room is available, you may continue your meeting by advising the front desk of how much more time you need it. Please note that in this case, we will charge your credit card for the overage following your meeting.
