About this space
A long, rectangular carpeted meeting room centered on a single boardroom table and high-backed chairs, finished with recessed ceiling lighting and a photographic feature wall. A glass front wall separates the room from the corridor.
Room features: The room supports presentations and remote participation through a projector, PA speakers and integrated conference-call facilities. Air conditioning provides climate control for all-day sessions.
Best for: Board-level discussions and presentation-led meetings are best suited to this space.
