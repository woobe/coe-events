AI, Quant & Finance NYC Details Where Artificial Intelligence Meets Quantitative Finance Wall Street has always been a place where mathematics, technology, and financial markets intersect. Today, artificial intelligence is reshaping that intersection — from machine learning and systematic trading to reinforcement learning, portfolio optimization, and autonomous financial agents. We’re launching AI Finance, a new professional community bringing together the people building the future of quantitative finance. Join us for our inaugural networking social at a sophisticated Wall Street hotel lounge for an evening of cocktails, conversations, and new connections with New York City’s quantitative finance and AI community. No presentations. No pitches. No formal agenda. Just an opportunity to meet the researchers, engineers, traders, and innovators working at the intersection of AI and financial markets. The Conversation Financial markets have become increasingly driven by data, algorithms, and intelligent systems. As AI capabilities advance, new opportunities — and new questions — are emerging across quantitative research, investment management, and financial technology. What happens when reinforcement learning meets systematic trading? How are LLMs and autonomous agents changing investment research? Can machine learning uncover meaningful signals in increasingly competitive markets? How do we validate these systems and manage their risks? Bring your research, experiences, perspectives, and ideas. Whether you’re developing trading algorithms, researching new architectures, building financial AI platforms, or exploring quantitative finance for the first time, you’ll find others interested in the same challenges. What to Expect An informal, professionally focused networking evening with drinks, conversation, and opportunities to connect across New York’s financial and technology communities. Meet quantitative researchers, AI engineers, data scientists, systematic traders, portfolio managers, and fintech founders. Share what you’re building, discuss emerging research, exchange perspectives on financial markets, and establish connections beyond your immediate industry. We’ll also be discussing the direction of Quant Builders NYC — future research sessions, technical talks, industry speakers, and opportunities to collaborate. Who Should Attend? Quantitative researchers, algorithmic traders, portfolio managers, hedge fund professionals, AI and machine learning engineers, financial engineers, data scientists, fintech founders, academics, and anyone interested in the convergence of artificial intelligence and finance.
