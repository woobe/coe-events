About this space
The room is a bright, rectangular space arranged around a central working area, with neutral finishes, large windows and a practical, conference-ready character.
Room features: Presenters can show slides on a wall-mounted flatscreen or projector, and facilitators can capture ideas on a whiteboard or flipchart. Conference call facilities support remote participants.
Best for: Instructor-led training, facilitated workshops and presentation-style meetings that combine group discussion with projected content.
