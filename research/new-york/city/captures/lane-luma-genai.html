Free · Approval Required

You'll own the event end-to-end: the concept, the logistics, and the web of speakers, judges, and sponsors. You won't be doing it alone - we'll back you with our oiled machine: distribution to our 150k+ community, warm connections to sponsors and judges, venue help, and the playbooks we've built running events across seven cities. Over time, as you ramp up, you become the point of contact driving those relationships. You'll work directly with Sahar, one of the leading voices in AI, who has built and scaled four communities, and you'll get wired into the AI ecosystem fast: organizers, companies, marketers, and founders.
