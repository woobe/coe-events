About this space
A long, rectangular meeting space arranged in rows of moveable training tables and chairs. Carpeted flooring and recessed ceiling lighting create a neutral, professional environment.
Room features: A comprehensive AV setup supports presentations and remote participation, combining a ceiling-mounted projector, PA system and conference-call facilities. Air conditioning maintains a consistent temperature for extended sessions.
Best for: Instructor-led training, presentation-led workshops and classroom-style seminars.
What sets it apart: A full-length photographic mural of the London skyline runs along one wall and acts as a distinctive visual backdrop.
