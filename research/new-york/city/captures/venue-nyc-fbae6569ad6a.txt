About this space
Our main room, THE MIRAGE is our 4,000sf of venue with two separate rooms, that includes, high ceilings, a custom bar, video projection walls, sound system , VIP area, full DJ Booth, and restrooms.
Please note that the bar and DJ Booth are separate Add-ons and not included in the base price. The bar may only be attended by our licensed bartenders. If you wish to have access these areas, please let us know so we can provide you with the appropriate rate.
In addition, if you are serving alcohol, all drinking guest must be 21yo or over and you must add-on our bartenders and in-house security at an additional fee. Client selling of Alcohol is strictly prohibited.
Smoking is prohibited in all areas of the venue and building including Vapes. . Video security is active at all times in addition to an on-site building security guard 24/7. If you plan on decorating, please let us know well in advance as any decorating must be approved in advance. If you are using an outside decorator or event planner, they must provide us with their insurance certificate and include details of the decorating plan in writing for approval. If you are catering food, there is an addition $150 cleaning fee and any leftover food must be taken out by the caterer. No cooking is allowed in the venue. This includes fryers, microwaves and ovens. Sterno burners are allowed in specific areas.

FAQ
Are chairs available, and can they be arranged in theatre style?
Yes, the venue can offer up to 100 chairs set up in theatre style.
Is a microphone and PA system available?
Yes, a microphone and PA system are included.
Can clients bring their own DJ equipment?
Yes. Bringing all your own equipment, including speakers, means there is no DJ booth fee, and you can set up anywhere because the venue’s booth is in a fixed location.
Are decorations allowed at the venue?
Yes, but any decorating would need to be approved.
Are there restrictions on hiring entertainment vendors?
Yes. As this is an inside venue, fire dancers and aerialists are not allowed.
