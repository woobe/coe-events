About this space
Our newly renovated studio, situated in the heart of the Garment District at 38th Street and 8th Avenue, boasts abundant natural light and breathtaking views of NYC. It's perfect for seminars, workshops, meetings, presentations, networking events, photo and video productions, and even showrooms.
Since this platform doesn't display our tier pricing range please note that the pricing depends on the type of event and the number of people we have to set up for. The following is the tier pricing:
Pricing: Set up and cleaning fee $99, 3-hour minimum

26 -50 People: $250/hr.
51-75 People: $300/hr.
76-100 People: $350/hr.
101-125 People: $400/hr.
126 -150 People: $450/hr.
151-175 People: $500/hr.
The space includes over 150 comfortable chairs, couches, and seating areas, accommodating up to 150 guests. We also provide 20 professional 5' and 5 6' tables and several conference room tables.
Party Rate:
This studio is also available for parties for up to 150 people and the party rate is $500/hr and a $195 cleaning fee applies. Also for any event time after 10:00PM, a doorman fee of 50/hr applies. Please not that the building rule is that if there is any event including alcohol the renter and most of their guest must be at least 25 years old.

FAQ
Are power strips available in the room?
Yes. Power strips are available for the room.
Can chairs be arranged in theatre style with a central aisle?
Yes, 20–30 chairs can be arranged in theatre style with a central aisle, using whichever layout looks best in the room.
Are elevators available for access to the venue suite?
Yes. Five elevators are available to bring attendees to the suite.
Can guests use the venue's refrigerator?
Yes, guests are welcome to use the refrigerator. It will be empty and includes a freezer compartment.
Is there a curtain to screen the glass doors?
Yes, a curtain is provided to screen the glass doors.
