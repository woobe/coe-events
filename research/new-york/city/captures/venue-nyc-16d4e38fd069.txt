About this space
The Second Floor Banquet Room is a private dining room in Orsay Restaurant that occupies an enclosed upstairs space separated from the main dining floor.
Design: The room displays dark wood panelling, moulded plaster ceilings and patterned parquet flooring that reference traditional Parisian dining rooms. The layout places round and rectangular tables beneath warm pendant lighting and tall arched windows.
Features: The room includes Wi‑Fi, air conditioning and plentiful natural light from its tall windows. The room is prepared for formal dining with white tablecloths, glassware and polished cutlery already in place. The table layout can be adjusted between round and long arrangements to suit seating plans.
Ideal for: Groups seeking a formal, seated private dinner in an enclosed, quietly separated dining room.
What sets it apart: An integrated PA system plus permission to bring your own DJ allows hosts to run playlists, announcements or live sound during private hires.
What guests say: The room is beautiful and understated, creating a relaxed, elegant feel. The room projects a Parisian character and a comforting sense of familiarity to repeat visitors.
