About this space
The Atrium is a space that is perfect for a wide range of events, such as exhibitions, seminars, birthday celebrations, and many more.
The space has a versatile layout that guests and organizations can personalize to their liking, whether they prefer theatre-style seating, an open floor plan, or a combination of both.
The venue's adaptable layout is one of its best features. The space is bright and contemporary, with exposed brick, metal beams, white walls, and a glass ceiling feature that floods the space with natural light.
When you book The Atrium, you're not just getting a venue, you're getting a space that can be reimagined to suit your vision. The flexibility that comes with this space enables you to create an entirely unique experience for your event guests and attendees.
