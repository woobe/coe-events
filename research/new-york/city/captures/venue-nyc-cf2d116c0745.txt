About this space
The venue is a multi-use event space defined by continuous exposed brick walls and timber floors that create a raw, industrial backdrop.
Design: The interior is open-plan and punctuated by central brick columns and timber support posts, with low ceilings, exposed ducting and warm overhead bulbs. The room receives direct natural light from street-facing windows that balances the industrial finishes.
Features: The venue includes a PA system and Bluetooth speakers plus a projector with screen for AV needs. The space provides Wi‑Fi, air conditioning, a small bar counter and basic back‑of‑house fittings such as sinks and a fridge. The ground floor offers step‑free access and accessible toilets.
Ideal for: Creative and social groups will find the space best suited to relaxed, flexible standing receptions or informal seated workshops.
What sets it apart: The space provides a raw, unfitted interior that hosts can customise extensively to suit their own layout and styling.
What guests say: The space is clean and bright and supported by responsive, helpful management. The exposed brick interior and flexible layout receive praise for style and ease of setup.

Party · 74 guests
· November 2026
The venue itself is gorgeous, it has a good rustic vibe going for it. A lot bigger than it looks and a lot of moveability depending on how you set up and what your event is. Host was very kind (and during the online half) communication was on point, reached out in a timely manner even checking back in with us. Would love to use this space again!
There are just a few areas where things could be improved:
Cleanliness: There was an event the day before ours, and the bar area was left super sticky with no cleaning supplies available for us to wipe it down. Overall cleaning could be a bit sharper (for example, there was noticeable dust on the windowsills). One thorough deep clean followed by regular minor maintenance would make the space practically spotless. (Full disclosure: I am very neat and admittedly a bit of a clean freak, so aside from the sticky bar, these are minor details!)
Furniture & Listing Photos: All of the extra, unrented tables and chairs were left inside the venue, so we had to stack them in the corner ourselves—it would have looked much nicer if only the furniture we reserved was in the room upon arrival. Also, my biggest critique is that the short 2-top/4-top wood tables and matching chairs shown in the listing photos weren't available. We only had mismatched long wood tables and cushioned folding chairs. Takashi did explain this to us prior to the event, so it wasn't a surprise, though having uniform table sizes or those smaller wood tables would have been great for our layout.
Sound & On-Site Support: I had assumed there was a built-in speaker system, but it turned out to be a portable JBL speaker that died halfway through the event. Fortunately, we had a charger and kept it plugged in. We were told someone would be on-site in the basement or that we could text Takashi if we needed anything, but we didn't get a text back during the event and couldn't locate the on-site contact until a few hours in, so we had to troubleshoot on our own.
On a final note, the lighting does get fairly dim at night, though it still created a really nice atmosphere in our opinion. Despite the hiccups, it's a wonderful venue with tons of potential!

FAQ
Are tables and chairs available for seating?
Yes, the rental fee includes four wooden tables, three high tables and 20 chairs, as well as WiFi, a fridge, freezer and a speaker for music.
Can the microphone be paid for on the day?
Yes, the microphone can be paid for on the day.
Is DJ equipment or loud music allowed in the space?
No, loud music such as a DJ or live band is not allowed in the space. Instead, a Bluetooth speaker is available free of charge for background music and can be connected to a smartphone, tablet or PC.
Are tablecloths mandatory?
No, tablecloths are not mandatory.
