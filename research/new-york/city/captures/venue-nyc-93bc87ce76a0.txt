About this space
Bogart House (Main Floor) is the main-floor event space inside Bogart House. The space is an open-plan industrial shell designed for flexible, custom layouts.
Design: The main-floor interior pairs high, exposed-beam ceilings and white-painted structural elements with polished concrete floors and minimal furnishings, creating a neutral, airy backdrop ready for custom styling.
Features: The room has an open layout that accommodates staging or a dance area and can be dressed with movable lounge seating. The space includes a PA system and speakers, Wi‑Fi and air conditioning, and allows own music or an external DJ. The area is wheelchair accessible and has accessible toilets.
Ideal for: The space suits creative teams and social groups seeking a relaxed, adaptable setting for standing receptions or DJ-led parties.
What sets it apart: Expansive wall-to-wall windows flood the room with natural light and provide clear views of the city skyline.
What guests say: The space is clean and well managed. The space is supported by attentive staff and delivers a lively, community-focused atmosphere.
