Hourly Rates
Hourly booking rates are $75/hour and can be used for events with up to 8 attendees. There are various different rooms where you may be placed based on your preferences and what space is available. You are guaranteed at least 200sf in Conference Room 1, Conference Room 2, Meeting Room 1, Meeting Room 2, or Seminar Room A. You can book an event for any day of the week, but it must be for at least 4 consecutive hours between 8:00am and 9:00pm.
