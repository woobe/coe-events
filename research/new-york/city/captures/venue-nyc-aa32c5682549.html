About this space

Discover Blender’s 5,000 sq. ft. Auditorium—a versatile corporate event space conveniently located in Midtown Manhattan on the corner of Madison Avenue and East 31st Street. Situated on the 8th floor of a 14-story turn-of-the-century office loft featuring soaring 11.5' ceilings, our award-winning space is full-service with in-house event planning and production teams coordinating catering, staffing, and furniture rentals.

Capacity & Specifications:
+ Capacity: 250 seated theater-style | 300 standing reception
+ Footprint: 5,000 sq. ft. auditorium | 11.5 ft ceilings
+ Adjacent Spaces: Pairs seamlessly with an adjacent 3,000 sq. ft. Event Suite, plus several well-appointed meeting rooms including 2 large boardrooms overlooking Madison Avenue.

Technology & A/V Infrastructure:
+ Live streaming & multi-panel video recording capabilities
+ Samsung LED TV displays
+ Neat Bar all-in-one video conferencing
+ Shure handheld & lavalier microphones
+ Electro-Voice EVOLVE 30M 1000W Sound System & SONOS-powered JBL house sound system

Location & Access:
Located at 135 Madison Avenue in NoMad, steps from key subway connections at Herald Square and 33rd St, with seamless proximity to Penn Station and Grand Central Terminal.
