Carnegie

Welcome to the Carnegie Room, the jewel in Collectif MTL’s crown, an event space designed to meet all your expectations. With an impressive capacity, this multi-purpose hall can accommodate up to 200 people for dance events, 150 for convivial 5@7s or 120 participants for seated conferences.

Spanning a generous 98 x 25 feet, the Carnegie Room offers a spacious and elegant setting, perfect for large-scale events such as conferences, parties, anniversaries, galas and much more. With its flexible layout, it adapts to your needs to make your event a memorable one.

To guarantee your comfort and that of your guests, the room is equipped with two convenient bars, high-speed Wi-Fi, a high-performance air-conditioning system, and a fully-equipped kitchen for food preparation. What’s more, professional audio and visual equipment is on hand to enhance your presentations or liven up your evenings. Seven modern washrooms complete the facilities to ensure a pleasant experience for all your guests.

To simplify the organization of your event, we provide you with a list of experienced external caterers who have already worked with us. These professionals will meet all your culinary requirements and contribute to the success of your reception.

Additional services are also available on request to further personalize your experience. Whether it’s a photobooth to capture your memories, a coffee and tea machine to delight your guests, Bluetooth microphones for your speeches or a projector with a 100-inch screen for your visual presentations, we have everything you need to make your event a success.

Whether you’re planning a professional presentation, a dance party or a prestigious gala, the Carnegie Room is ready to welcome you in an exceptional setting. Trust us to turn your ideas into reality and create unforgettable moments.
