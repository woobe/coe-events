Book Your Space 
Tango Renaissance offers three beautifully designed studio spaces, each available for private rentals. Whether you’re hosting a dance class, workshop, rehearsal, or private event, our studios provide a versatile and welcoming environment in the heart of Montreal. Each studio comes equipped with high-quality sound systems, mirrors and elegant lighting options to create the perfect atmosphere for your gathering.
