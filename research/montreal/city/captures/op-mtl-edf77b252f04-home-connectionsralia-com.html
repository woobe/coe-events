our raison d'être

Hospitality done differently

Local first

We support local in everything down to the last detail, guided by the Seventh Generation Principle — Indigenous wisdom that emphasizes a caring relationship with the land in the present and for 7 generations to come.

Regenerative

We believe small choices add up: we stock all rooms with eco-friendly soaps, shampoos, and cleaning products and use only natural linens, so you can rest easy knowing your stay is kind to the planet.

Women-led

We’re a caring and compassionate team of moms who built something we genuinely believe in: spaces and experiences rooted in empathy and sustainability.
