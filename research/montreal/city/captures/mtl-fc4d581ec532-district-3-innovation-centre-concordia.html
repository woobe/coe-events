we make founders

With over 1400 startups supported in 10 years, District 3 is Quebec's leading incubator for bio, health, social and high tech entrepreneurs.
who we are
From conception to growth, we equip founders with the tools and guidance to succeed at every stage.

Our holistic approach combines tailored coaching, sector-specific expertise, ESG-focused strategies, and access to state-of-the-art labs, collaborative workspaces, and global networks.

More than just an incubator, District 3 is a vibrant community where creativity, collaboration, and passion ignite meaningful change and shape a better future.

our space
Welcome to District 3, a vibrant co-working space in downtown Montreal with shared tables, cozy seating, and natural light.

Access our dynamic community space—open weekdays 9AM-5PM—featuring diverse first-come-first-serve workstations, bookable conference rooms for hybrid meetings, complimentary coffee, and event venues with dedicated staff support for workshops and networking opportunities.

Sensory Space

At District 3, we believe supporting founders means caring for both their ventures and their nervous systems. Our downtown workspace includes a dedicated sensory space designed with neurodivergent founders in mind, but open to anyone who needs a moment to decompress between meetings, experiments or investor calls. The room offers soothing soundscapes, ambient lighting and soft seating that helps founders slow down, reset and recharge. It is a calm, restorative environment that reflects our commitment to building a startup culture grounded not only in hustle, but in wellbeing, clarity and sustainable growth.
