Creative event space 
Welcome to Collectif MTL 
Welcome to Collectif MTL, a multi-purpose space offering four unique rooms, all located within the same facility, to meet all your event and corporate needs. 
Whether you’re planning a large event or an intimate meeting, our rooms are designed to combine comfort, elegance and functionality. All our spaces include a professional sound and lighting system, a bar with fridge, toilets, Wi-Fi and more. 
We also offer additional services to provide the best possible experience for every event: catering, bar, staff, music (opera, jazz, DJ, etc.). For larger corporate events, you even have the option of using all our spaces, offering your guests a variety of atmospheres and uses. 
At Collectif MTL, we’re proud to offer unique and flexible spaces to meet your every need.
