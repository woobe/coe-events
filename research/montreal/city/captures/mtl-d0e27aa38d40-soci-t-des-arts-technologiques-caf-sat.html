Rent our spaces

In the heart of Montreal’s Quartier des spectacles, the SAT makes its spaces available for all your events

©
Maryse Boyce

Are you looking for a central, versatile, and charming space? The SAT is ready to meet your needs for a wide range of events, accommodating anywhere from 20 to 1,000 people:

  • Conferences, screenings, and launches, whether in-person or hybrid
  • Corporate events and cocktail receptions
  • Exhibitions, trade fairs, and fashion shows
  • Concerts of any scale, public or private
  • Courses, technical workshops, focus groups
  • Film shoots
TECHNICAL DETAILS OF OUR RENTAL SPACES

Espace SAT

A MODULAR ENVIRONMENT WITH A CLEAN, INDUSTRIAL DESIGN

Located on the ground floor with direct access to Saint-Laurent Boulevard, the Espace SAT stands out for its versatility and industrial character.

This venue is equipped with high-end features: lighting, video projectors, control room, stage, a high-performance sound system, and a bar service. Its curtain system allows for the creation of different atmospheres or for combining multiple functions within the space.

Whether for a day, an evening, or longer periods, this space adapts to your needs. Various packages are offered to closely meet your event project requirements.

General Information

Technical aspects

  • Accommodates 150 to 990 people
  • Complete sound and lighting systems, control room, and stage
  • 8 surfaces for projecting presentations or videos (fixed screens and wall mapping)
  • Technical grid for your hanging needs
  • General furniture: tables and chairs (high, low), sofas, etc.
  • Access to 2 dressing rooms and 1 production office
  • Universally accessible

Café SAT

©
Raphaël Thibodeau

A Luminous Space for Small Events

With its large bay window overlooking Place de la Paix and its summer terrace, Café SAT is a bright and ideal space for your smaller events. With or without furniture, the café can be quickly transformed to host your happy hours, networking events, launches, or more intimate celebrations.

Whether you need bar or café service, our team is available to ensure you can fully enjoy your event. For a more festive touch, SAT can even help you find the perfect artist to provide musical ambiance!

The café is available for rentals during the day and in the evening, on weekdays and weekends. Feel free to contact us to discuss your event!

General Information

Technical aspects

  • Accommodates up to 70 people
  • 1 mobile screen (4K, 75 inches, on a stand) and 2 microphones for your presentations
  • Basic sound system

Buvette and SAT Rooftop

©
Raphaël Thibodeau

Warm Atmosphere with a Spectacular View of Downtown

Nestled on the third floor of the building, the SAT’s culinary space offers a bright and welcoming setting, designed to host a wide variety of events. Inside, a long U-shaped bar, bench seating, and pendant lights create a warm atmosphere, with a dedicated DJ booth that contributes to a lively and relaxed vibe. Its terrace offers an unobstructed view of downtown and extends the outdoor experience during the summer season. The space is available for private events every day of the week.

General Information

Technical aspects

  • Accommodates up to 80 people
  • Catering service
  • Mobile screen (4K, 75 inches, on a stand)
  • Universally accessible
  • General furniture (tables and chairs (high, low), couches, etc.)

Satosphere

©
Ash KG

One-of-a-Kind Event Setting

Located on the top floor of the SAT, the Satosphere is an immersive theater in the shape of a dome, featuring a spherical projection screen 18 meters in diameter and 13 meters high. The Satosphere is an extraordinary event space that greatly enhances the audience experience through its unique setting.

Adjacent to the dome is a spacious lobby equipped with a service counter; lighting, projectors, televisions, speakers, and furniture can be arranged to suit your needs. The dome’s partitions can be opened or closed, offering numerous layout possibilities.

This universally accessible space is available for private events on Sundays and Mondays, and occasionally on weekdays.

General Information

Technical aspects

  • Accommodates up to 500 people
  • Full immersive sound system (93 channels), spherical screen, and control room
  • General furniture: tables and chairs (high, low), beanbag chairs, sofas, etc.
  • Universally accessible

TRAINING SPACES

A TECHNICAL SPACE OPTIMIZED FOR COLLABORATIVE AND CREATIVE WORK

Our training space, located in the basement of the SAT, is a multifunctional room designed to host meetings, technical workshops, intensive work sessions, and listening sessions in optimal conditions. Equipped with a professional-grade sound system, it delivers a precise audio experience. The layout is designed for comfort and productivity, with tables featuring built-in power outlets for each participant.

Practical Information

Technical aspects

Capacity: Up to 25 seated people

Area: Approx. 555 square feet

Dimensions: 19.11 feet x 27.11 feet

Audio equipment: 2 Genelec speakers, 2 KiiK speakers, 1 Genelec subwoofer, mixing console, and Focusrite interface (upon request).

Video equipment: Projector with HDMI connectivity and adapters provided.

A BRIGHT ROOM DESIGNED FOR LEARNING, COLLABORATION, AND MULTIMEDIA CREATION

Located at the heart of SAT’s activities, this room offers a bright and functional environment, ideal for conferences, professional presentations, or student cohorts. Its layout encourages direct interaction between instructors and participants, making it particularly well-suited for teaching software and digital creation.
