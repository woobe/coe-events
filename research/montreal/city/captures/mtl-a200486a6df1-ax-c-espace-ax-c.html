About

Ax.c is a vibrant meeting place that gathers leading stakeholders of the entrepreneurial, technological and innovative ecosystem, encouraging them to work closely and more effectively together to help Québec succeed.

Our vision: to gain worldwide recognition as the must-see destination for Québec’s innovative and tech entrepreneurship.

Discover a place unlike any other in Québec, specially designed to connect startups, investors and experts. At Ax.c, collaboration, networking and innovation meet each day to build the future of technology.

A modern, versatile rental startup space, created to welcome startups, investors, incubators, support organizations and corporations. Ax.c brings together key talent and resources to turn ambitious ideas into success.

Located in Place Victoria, on Montréal Exchange’s former trading floor, Ax.c is more than a simple location. It’s a driver of progress, and it expands strategic collaborations and bold ideas for the success of local startups.
