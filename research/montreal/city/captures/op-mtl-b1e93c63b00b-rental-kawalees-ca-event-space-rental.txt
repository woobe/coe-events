Kawalees Space 
Kawalees, is your premier choice for a versatile event space in Montreal, Quebec. 
Our multi-purpose venue is perfect for public performances, corporate events, and private parties. Whether you're organizing a cultural gathering, a professional function, or a personal celebration, Kawalees provides an exceptional event space for your needs. 
Conveniently located at mile-end; 5175A Avenue du Parc, in the heart of Montreal, our venue offers a unique rental solution with a house capacity of 99 people with approximately 70 to 80 seated depending on the setup. This makes it an ideal choice for a variety of events, ensuring a memorable experience for your audience. 
The main stage at Kawalees, measuring 15 feet wide by 15 feet deep, is tailor-made for performances, shows, presentations, and gatherings, ensuring ample room for larger productions. 
Immerse yourself in the vibrant Montreal Plateau area, where Kawalees transcends being just a venue; it's a destination where creativity and community converge. Our versatile event space is meticulously designed to meet your event requirements and captivate your audience. 
Planning an event and searching for the perfect space? Look no further than Kawalees. Reserve your date now and elevate your event with the unmatched ambiance of Kawalees.

Our weekend rental rates are a bit higher because these events play a big role in helping us cover our costs and keep this space thriving. We truly appreciate your understanding and support—it means so much to us! 
Here is a breakout to understand rental rates better: 
Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Sunday: rental rate is at $100 CAD + taxes per 4-hours bracket. 
Friday and Saturday: rental rate is at $150 CAD + taxes per 4-hours bracket. 
Kawalees intentionally keeps its venue rental rates affordable to support artists, performers, cultural events and community initiatives. The rental fee covers only part of the cost of operating the venue.

Frequently Asked Questions 
Planning a show, concert, comedy night, screening, cultural event or private gathering? Find practical information about the stage, sound and lighting systems, ticketing, rental times, food and bar service, setup requirements and venue policies. 
Shows, Stage & Technical Setup 
Can we have a DJ and dancing? 
Yes. You may have a DJ and may bring your own DJ controller and laptop to connect to the venue sound system. Dancing is permitted in the available open space. Tables cannot be removed simply to convert the entire venue into a dedicated dance floor. 
Can we organize karaoke? 
Yes. Karaoke events are permitted. Kawalees does not provide a karaoke machine, so organizers must bring their own karaoke equipment. There is no additional venue fee specifically for karaoke. 
What sound equipment is available? 
Kawalees has professional sound and stage equipment on site, including digital and analog mixers, microphones, stage monitors, main speakers, lighting equipment, cables and related stage equipment. Please refer to the equipment section above for the current detailed equipment list. 
Is a sound technician included? 
No. A sound technician is not automatically included. If you or your team can operate the equipment and handle your own sound check, you may not need one. For live music or more complex technical setups, hiring a technician may be advisable. Kawalees provides a list of technicians here that you may contact directly. Availability and pricing are arranged directly with the technician. 
Can we rehearse at Kawalees? 
Yes, if you have already reserved an event. Rehearsal time must also be reserved and paid for. 
Can we come earlier just to install equipment or sound check? 
Only if you have reserved the earlier time slot. Sound check and equipment installation normally take place during your reserved time. 
Does Kawalees sell tickets or manage the door? 
No. Ticketing and admission are generally the organizer’s responsibility. The organizer manages ticket sales, door admission and guest entry. 
Does Kawalees promote my event? 
The organizer remains responsible for marketing and promotion. Kawalees may help by sharing a digital event flyer on its social media shortly before the event. A digital flyer should generally be provided around one to two weeks before the event. 
Is parking available near the venue? 
Yes! Metered street parking is available on Avenue du Parc and nearby streets, generally at $4.25/hour . Parking is usually free during these hours: 
Monday to Friday: After 9 p.m. 
Saturday: Before 9 a.m. and after 6 p.m. 
Sunday: Before 1 p.m. and after 6 p.m. 
Free, non-metered parking may also be available on parts of: 
Rue Jeanne-Mance: Between Laurier and Fairmount 
Avenue Fairmount Ouest: Between Jeanne-Mance and du Parc 
Avenue de l’Esplanade: North of Fairmount 
Paid parking lot: The nearest option is EyePark at 5030 rue Jeanne-Mance , about a 5–7 minute walk from the venue. 
Parking availability varies. Always check street signs and meters for current hours, restrictions, and time limits. 
Booking & Rental Time 
What types of events can I host at Kawalees? 
Kawalees is available for public and ticketed events such as concerts, comedy shows, performances, screenings, cultural events, karaoke and release events. The venue can also be rented for private celebrations , birthdays, corporate events and similar gatherings. 
How are rental times structured? 
The venue is rented in four-hour blocks. Your reservation gives you access to the venue only during the time slot you booked. Setup, the event itself and cleanup must all take place within your reserved time unless additional time has been booked. 
Can I arrive before my reserved time? 
No. Access begins at the start of your reserved time. We cannot guarantee earlier access because another organizer may have booked the previous time slot. 
What if I need more time for setup, decorating or sound check? 
Reserve the previous four-hour block as well. This is recommended for events that require significant setup, decoration, DJ installation, rehearsal, sound check or additional preparation. 
Can I reserve two consecutive time slots? 
Yes. Booking two consecutive blocks is often the best option for events that require substantial preparation, rehearsal or cleanup time. 
Can we stay after midnight? 
Additional time after midnight may be possible depending on the venue schedule and the nature of the event. Additional time is normally charged at $50 per hour plus taxes and should be arranged in advance 
How can I check availability, and can you hold a date? 
Current availability can be checked directly on the booking form below, select a day, and the available times for booking will be shown. We generally do not hold dates. Reservations are made on a first-come, first-served basis, so an available date may be booked by another organizer at any time. 
Capacity, Setup & Venue Rules 
What is the maximum capacity? 
The maximum capacity is 99 people total. This includes guests, organizers, artists, technicians, employees and anyone else present in the venue. 
What is the seated capacity? 
Approximately 70 to 80 people can be seated, depending on the event setup and furniture arrangement. 
Is setup and cleanup time included in my rental? 
Yes, but both must take place within your reserved time. For example, if your reservation ends at midnight, cleanup and resetting the venue must also be completed by midnight unless additional time has been arranged. 
Can we rearrange the venue? 
Within reason, tables, chairs and other furniture may be rearranged to suit your event. Major alterations are not permitted, and all furniture, stage equipment, cables and venue items must be handled carefully and returned to their original positions before the end of your booking. 
What are the cleaning and reset requirements? 
The venue must be left in a reasonable condition. A $50 cleaning fee may apply if additional cleaning is required. A separate $50 fee may apply if furniture, stage equipment or other venue items are not returned to their original setup. Missing or damaged venue property will be invoiced to the organizer. 
Can we decorate the venue? 
Yes. Simple decorations are allowed. Glitter, confetti and other decorations that create excessive mess are not permitted. Decorations must be removed before the end of your reservation. 
Food & Drinks 
Why are food and beverage sales important for rental events? 
Kawalees intentionally keeps its rental rates affordable to support artists, performers and the cultural community. A significant part of the cost of operating the venue is supported through food and beverage sales during events. 
We therefore expect organizers to make a genuine effort to promote their event and attract an audience. Organizers should not reserve the venue for an event they do not reasonably expect people to attend. Events with very low attendance or food and beverage consumption will not be approved for future rentals. 
Can we bring outside food? 
No. Outside food is not permitted. The only exception is a birthday cake. 
Can we bring our own alcohol or other beverages? 
No. Outside beverages and alcohol are not permitted. Food and beverages must be purchased through Kawalees. 
Can guests order food and drinks individually? 
Yes. Guests may order à la carte from the Kawalees menu during the event. 
Can we arrange food in advance for a group? 
Yes. Organizers may arrange food in advance. Options can include preselected dishes, shared plates, mezze-style service, buffet-style arrangements, a fixed amount per guest or another arrangement based on the organizer’s budget. 
Can I give Kawalees a food budget? 
Yes. Let us know your approximate budget and expected number of guests and we can suggest suitable options. 
Is there a minimum food or beverage purchase? 
There is currently no standard minimum purchase requirement for regular rentals. However, Food and beverage sales are an important part of the venue’s operation. Events generating less than $225 in total food and beverage consumption on weekdays or $300 on weekends may not be approved for future events. 
Can I bring a birthday cake? 
Yes. Birthday cakes are allowed. There is no standard cake fee. If you require special plating, serving or another arrangement, please ask in advance. 
Still have a question? 
If your event has unusual technical, catering or scheduling requirements, contact us before completing your reservation.
