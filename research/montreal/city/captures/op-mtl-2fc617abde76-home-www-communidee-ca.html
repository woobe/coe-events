Building community for homeschooling families

Communidée is a non-profit cooperative in Saint-Henri, Montréal, where homeschooling families connect, learn, and build friendships. Our centre offers a welcoming space for children, teens, and parents to explore their interests through activities, creative projects, and outings. Run entirely by parents and guardians, Communidée comes to life through each family’s ideas, skills, and involvement. We share resources, support one another, and create opportunities for everyone to grow.

Life at Communidée

Meet other homeschooling families

Homeschooling is an ambitious initiative - Communidée offers opportunity to meet like-minded families, share experiences, learn from each other or simply socialize!

Activities to enrich your learning experience

What better way for your homeschooled kids to learn than by doing? Communidée offers a constant stream of parent-led activities and workshops from art to music to science to whatever your mind can imagine.
