loft108 : events & Wellbeing 
Loft108 is an enchanting wellness clinic and event venue located in on the west side of Old Montreal. By day, we offer consultations in naturopathy and ayurveda as well as corporate yoga programs. Our space is equally used to host corporate and private events like press conferences, networking lunches, conferences, weddings, baby showers, birthday parties. Loft108, which its high ceiling and unique walls, is also an incredible studio for photoshoots and video productions. 
Features: 
Gorgeous floor to ceiling arched windows offering natural light; 
Open studio space; 
Lounge area with vintage couches; 
Ambient twinkle lights strung throughout the space; 
Full kitchen & bathroom; 
Private dressing room with professional makeup area; 
Projector, drop screen & microphone available; 
Sonos surround sound system with five zones.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS 
Why the name LOFT108? 
First, the number 108 is not our suite number. We understand how this might be confusing (especially to delivery people), which is why we want to clarify. The number 108 is an ultra sacred number in many spiritual traditions, including yoga. It is also particularly special to our director, Yasmin Fudakowska-Gow , who did 108 sun salutations for 108 days and then went on to beat a Guinness World Record. Yeah, she’s kinda nuts! 
How many guests can I have? 
At Loft 108, we are currently hosting groups of up to 50 guests (standing), and can accommodate about 35 seated guests. Cocktail parties where guests can meet and mingle, some standing, some seated work best at Loft108. For wellness events, we can accommodate about 40-45 yoga mats once the lounge is cleared. 
What services are included? 
When booking time at Loft108, you get the space as shown in the virtual tour and layout and the support of one of our amazing event people, who will be with you for the duration of the event (including set up and closing). Your booking also includes pre and post-event cleaning services. We can also support you with event planning, catering, event photography, entertainment, decorations, rentals, etc. 
What is available for rent? 
In order to make your event as seamless as possible, we offer rental services for additional seating, cocktail tables, dish and glass ware, linens as well as audio visual equipment (ie wireless microphones and a projector). 
What are the food and beverage options? 
While we currently do not offer in-house catering services at Loft108, we would be happy to refer you to one of our partner caterers. We have a full kitchen available for use and you are also welcome to bring your own catering and beverages. 
How accessible is Loft108? 
We are in the heart of the city on busy St Paul Street. Our space is on the third floor and unfortunately, we do not have an elevator and therefore we are not wheelchair accessible. This breaks our hearts, because we value inclusivity so much, but alas, because we’re in a historical building, we don’t have many options. 
What is the transit & parking like around Loft108? 
We are located on the west side of Old Montreal on St Paul Street West between Blvd McGill and St Pierre Street. We are only a 7-minute walk from Square Victoria-OACI metro station. For parking, there are two parking lots nearby, one adjacent, and one directly across from Loft108, on St. Paul West. There is also street parking available on St Pierre, Youville, McGill and on St Paul.
