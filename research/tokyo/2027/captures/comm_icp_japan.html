About

About us

ICP Japan is a community dedicated to promoting the Internet Computer (decentralized cloud) ecosystem in Japan. It started in 2022 as the industry-academia research project "C3F", and operated as ICP HUB Japan from 2024.

Following the DFINITY Foundation's restructuring (DFINITY 2.0) in December 2025, the ICP Hub program concluded — but ICP Japan continues as a volunteer-led, self-organized community initiative.

Featured · Sovereign Cloud

ICP as a sovereignty-preserving cloud

Centralized clouds (AWS / Azure / GCP) carry single points of failure and jurisdictional risks. In March 2026, a fire at AWS's Dubai facility caused widespread impact. ICP, running on distributed subnets as a "sovereign cloud", is drawing attention from governments — including Switzerland and Pakistan.

🇨🇭
Switzerland · Swiss Subnet AG

Swiss Subnet — "Sovereign Secure. Cloud infrastructure for the AI era."

Zug-based Swiss Subnet AG provides a "sovereign execution environment built in Switzerland". All execution environments operate under Swiss law and support AI-native, regulated, and mission-critical workloads. Runs as a subnet on ICP (Internet Computer).

In February 2026, DFINITY and the Pakistan Digital Authority (PDA) announced a strategic partnership for sovereign cloud infrastructure on Internet Computer, generative-AI app development, and secure national communications. Pakistan Subnet and Caffeine AI Licenses are also planned.
