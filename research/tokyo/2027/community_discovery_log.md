## Method notes

- A group was kept only when its own page (or its own event page on Luma) carried either a dated activity fact or an explicit cadence statement that could be transcribed.
- Directory and aggregator pages (connpass listings, cryptoevents.global, JapanConf, kaijosearch) were used only to find candidate names; none is cited as final evidence.
- Member counts are recorded only where the group's own captured page shows a number (Tokyo AI Community: 156). Everything else is null rather than estimated.
- No email address, phone number or private handle is stored. Contact routes are public pages only.
