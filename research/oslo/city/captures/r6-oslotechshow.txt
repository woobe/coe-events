Keynote speakers 2027

Dr Wendy A. Okolo

A research engineer and Associate Project Manager at NASA, working at the intersection of aerospace innovation, intelligent systems and advanced technology. Her work explores how flight safety, vehicle controls and intelligent systems can help shape the future of aviation and space exploration. She is also a passionate advocate for diversity, education and inspiring the next generation of engineers and innovators.

Research engineer at NASA

Elin Hauge

A business and data strategist, pragmatic futurist and AI expert, helping leaders turn data-driven technologies into meaningful business value. With more than 20 years of experience, she offers practical insights into responsible AI adoption, digital transformation, algorithmic risk and the future of technology.

Where the Industry Connects

Exhibition

Step into the future of technology on the exhibition floor – where tomorrow is created today. Explore 150+ leading exhibitors showcasing breakthroughs in AI, cybersecurity, IoT, cloud, and sustainability. Connect with industry leaders, discover innovations, and experience it all for free.

Morning Seminars

Start your day at the exhibition with our free morning seminars! Gain fresh insights, discover new perspectives, and be inspired by industry experts. The perfect way to kick off the day with knowledge, inspiration, and valuable takeaways.

Big Speakers on Open Stages

Free stages. World-class voices. Unmissable insights. Experience the future come alive through bold ideas and inspiring talks on AI, sustainability, digitalization, IoT, and mobility. Join the conversations shaping tomorrow and leave with insights that keep you ahead.

Awards

Join us as we celebrate the innovators shaping the future of technology. The awards recognise outstanding organisations and individuals who are driving progress through innovation, creativity, and forward-thinking solutions. A highlight of the event, the awards showcase the people and companies making a real impact across the tech industry.

Hackathon

Take part in our standalone Hackathon on day two of the event. Work alongside fellow innovators to explore industry challenges, exchange ideas and develop solutions with real-world potential. Challenge your thinking, combine diverse perspectives and turn ambitious ideas into practical concepts in just one day.

Hosted Buyer

The Hosted Buyer Programme connects senior decision-makers with leading tech innovators in an exclusive networking environment. Participants gain access to valuable business meetings, discover breakthrough solutions, build strategic partnerships, and explore new opportunities that drive innovation and growth.

Mingle

When the exhibition day comes to an end, the networking event begins. Join an evening of energy, celebration, and live entertainment, alongside award ceremonies, valuable networking opportunities, and memorable moments where innovation, inspiration, and new connections come together.

International Breakfast

The International Breakfast is a unique networking event bringing together business leaders, innovators, international delegations, and technology professionals from across Europe and beyond. Held directly on the exhibition floor, this breakfast gathering provides a valuable opportunity to build new connections, exchange ideas, and explore international business opportunities in an inspiring and collaborative environment.

Networking & Matchmaking

Maximise your exhibition experience with our digital networking and matchmaking tools. Connect with like-minded professionals, discover new business opportunities, and build valuable relationships that extend far beyond the event itself.

The Ultimate Expo Mingle!

After the first expo day, the exhibition hall transforms into a vibrant industry mingle with a festival vibe—packed with entertainment, activity zones, and spontaneous networking. The night culminates in award ceremonies and celebrations in a festive atmosphere!

How to get here

Live Expo organizes trade fairs, meetings, conferences, and events in the Scandinavian market. Our headquarters are located in Gothenburg, Sweden. We bring people and companies together to do business, network, and inspire one another. Live Expo was founded by Sweden’s most experienced entrepreneurs in the trade fair and events industry, who have launched more than one hundred new trade fairs, many of which are today leaders in their respective industries. With content packed with value, we inspire, develop, and keep our visitors up to date, taking the exhibition medium to a whole new level. As of 26 June 2026, Live Expo is a wholly owned subsidiary of Easyfairs Group, an international company that organizes 110 market-leading events in 16 countries and operates eight event venues in Belgium, the Netherlands, and Sweden.
