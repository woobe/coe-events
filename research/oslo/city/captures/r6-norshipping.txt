04

Hall G – A New Landmark for Nor Shipping

Nor-Shipping is growing, and 2025 marked a record-breaking edition. In 2027, a striking new glass hall will be built on the exhibition parking area, forming a bold new main entrance to the event and exhibition hall. The new addition will also feature a dedicated stage with free content throughout the week, with a programme focused on key industry topics, such as AI, ports, and maritime marketing and communication.
