About us
Oslo.AI is the one of the oldest and largest AI communities in Oslo. It's a non-commercial initiative made possible by volunteers and sponsors – for the community, by the community.
The Oslo.AI team consists of 6 volunteers right now. Currently the main activity is to organize quarterly meetups where AI professionals and enthusiasts get together and share lessons learned from implementing AI & machine learning.
Oslo.AI is dedicated to building a leading community for knowledge sharing and networking within the AI space.
