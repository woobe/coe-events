The Hive
Step into The Hive, the buzzing event space at the heart of Epicenter. With its vibrant atmosphere, customizable LED lighting, and immediate access to the bar and restaurant, this space is perfect for creating experiences. The Hive is ideal for dinners, breakfast seminars, or afterworks. The Hive’s unique setup also makes it perfect for pop-up events or networking sessions.
More details:
90 sqm venue.
Fits up to 100 people in mingle setting.
Fits 50 for a dinner set up.
2 x LED 95" screens and soundsystem.
Catering and bar.
