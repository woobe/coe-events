Meeting rooms and event venues in Oslo
Looking for the perfect event venue? At Mesh, we offer versatile spaces and flexible packages to suit your needs. With over 500 events hosted annually, we are a complete event provider. As a key part of Norway’s startup community, hosting with us supports and grows the local scene. Our creative, non-traditional spaces create a fresh atmosphere for any occasion. Let us help make your event memorable!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about Meeting Room Bookings at Matrikel1:
Do you offer additional services?
Yes, we offer full service, from coffee/water to breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
How do I book a room?
Simply visit our website’s booking section, choose your preferred room and date, and fill the required details.
What facilities are included in the rooms?
All our rooms come equipped with AV equipment, whiteboards and Wi-Fi.
How is the pricing determined?
Pricing varies based on room size, facilities, and booking duration. Submit a request, and we’ll get back to you.
Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?
Yes, cancellations or rescheduling can be done up to 48 hours before the booking time.
Can you also arrange dinners/parties?
Yes, we are a complete event provider with various venues and packages to choose from.
