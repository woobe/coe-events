The compact pan-Scandinavian trade fair event Sectech focuses on technology-based services and products for physical security – both in the exhibition and in the seminars.
Exhibit
As an exhibitor, you get the opportunity to effectively show your services and products to visitors,
who represent the entire supply chain, from end customers to system integrators and installers.
As a meeting place,
Sectech is an unparalleled forum, whether it is about establishing contacts for future business or to strengthen
relationships with existing customers.
Read more
VISIT SECTECH
As a visitor, you get an insight into the possibilities of technology to create increased physical security.
With both world-leading suppliers and innovative niche actors on site, the exhibition, despite its compact format,
is truly a world-class event. Add to that the content of the seminars where the security industry's challenges
and opportunities are always in focus and where there is a lot of current knowledge to be gained.
