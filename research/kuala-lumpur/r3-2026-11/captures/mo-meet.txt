Frequently Asked Questions
Can I host meetings at Mandarin Oriental, Kuala Lumpur?
Mandarin Oriental, Kuala Lumpur holds one of the largest ballroom in the city with a smaller second ballroom, seating 1,100 and 300 guests respectively, as well as a selection of additional venues. The hotel is well equipped to host meetings tailored to your specific requirements. With advanced facilities and dedicated technical support, the team ensures a seamless and successful event experience.
Are meeting packages available at Mandarin Oriental, Kuala Lumpur?
Yes. The hotel offers dedicated meeting packages, including programmes such as ‘Mandarin Oriental Loves Meetings’, which provide added value and savings on event bookings.
What is the Mindful Meetings programme?
Mindful Meetings is a wellness-focused programme at Mandarin Oriental, Kuala Lumpur, that integrates elements such as nutrition, movement and wellbeing into meetings to enhance productivity and engagement.
