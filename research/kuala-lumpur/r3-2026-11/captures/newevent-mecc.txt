About Matrade Exhibition Convention Centre (MECC)
MATRADE Exhibition & Convention Centre (MECC) complex covers more than 80,000 gross sqm at MATRADE’s 24-storey architectural icon with a unique archway design through the building and is one of the best venue for Meetings, Incentives, Conventions and Exhibitions with emphasis on Trade-Centric events. MECC is established in 2007.
MECC Complex houses:
- 3 Exhibition Halls
- 1 Ballroom
- 9 Function Rooms
- Theatrette
- Amphitheatre
Visit:
There are several ways to get to MECC
By Car
Strategically located midway between Kuala Lumpur and Petaling Jaya. MECC is accessible through major highways connection from the north and south of Klang Valley through NKVE-PLUS and from the east through DUKE and SPRINT, making it convenient for visitors to access to the venue. Ample car park bays are available for the visitors at MECC’s compound.
By Taxi
There are numerous taxis companies in Kuala Lumpur that offer reliable services. Make sure it is a metered taxi and avoid negotiating prices upfront.
There are also secured taxi booking apps available, like My Teksi, TaxiMonger, or Easy Taxi. Drivers are certified safe and all will use taxi-meters. The app will also allow you to contact the assigned driver directly and track his whereabouts on a real time map [however do note that all apps requires data access or wifi]
Taxis or Airport Limos are also available from the Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) to MECC and the drive to MECC takes about an hour.
By Train - Bus
If you are travelling by train, you can take a Light Rail Transit (LRT) using Star-LRT Line to Lake Titiwangsa. At the LRT Station, there is a bus no. U618 that stops at MECC.
Facilities:
The MECC complex are supported by a wide range of facilities to ensure that the needs of both planners and visitors are taken care of:
Parking
Ample indoor and outdoor parking bays are available at the MECC’s compound.
Prayer Rooms
Prayer rooms are available on the 4th Floor, West Wing Tower Block of MECC. Prayer mats, slippers and ablution wash area are provided in the prayer rooms.
For private functions, a larger prayer room can be arranged at various function rooms in the MECC. The Wilayah Mosque is located in the vicinity of MECC and is about a 10-min walk away.
ATM
There is a Automated teller machine (ATM) located at the Ground floor, West Wing of MECC. This machine also accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, Cirrus and Maestro cards. The nearest banks are located in the vicinity of MECC and is about a 5-min driving distance.
Convenience Kiosk
A convenience kiosk is located at the Ground floor, West Wing of MECC. The kiosk operated by Coop MATRADE sells sundries, newspapers and magazines, tobacco and last minute exhibitor requirements.
Traders Café
The café is located at the Ground floor, East Wing of MECC. The café serves a selection of favourite Malaysian style dishes perfect for breakfast, lunch and tea-time.
Information / Registration Counter
A built-in registration counter is available at the Ground Floor, Hall B of MECC.
Cargo / Service Lift
For ease of movement of exhibition and conference material, MECC has cargo and service lifts servicing the Exhibition Hall, MATRADE Hall and Function Rooms. Located at both the East Wing and West Wing and also at the Loading Bay. The lifts are easily accessible at the loading areas of MECC.
