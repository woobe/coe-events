FAQs About Our Coworking Space
Is there any free parking in your coworking space in Kuala Lumpur?
Complimentary and covered parking is available at most Colony locations for members and guests. Availability varies by centre — our team will confirm the options for your preferred location.
Do you offer coworking space in KL for the short term, like 1–2 hours?
Yes. Our hot desks and day passes let you work by the hour or by the day with no long-term commitment — perfect for short stints or a change of scenery.
Can I access the coworking space after business hours?
Yes — private office and dedicated desk members enjoy 24/7 access with their own secure entry, so you can work on your own schedule.
Are there any rules or guidelines to follow in your coworking space?
We keep it simple: respect the shared spaces, keep noise considerate in quiet zones, and our community team takes care of the rest so you can focus on work.
Can I use your coworking space in Kuala Lumpur as a registered business address?
Yes. Our virtual office and office plans can serve as your registered business address in KL. Ask our team about the virtual office package.
Can I have a tour of your coworking space before subscribing?
Of course — book a private viewing and our specialists will walk you through the space and design a plan around your needs.
