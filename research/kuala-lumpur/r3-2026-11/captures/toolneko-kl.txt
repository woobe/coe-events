Malaysia public holidays 2027: questions
How many public holidays are there in Kuala Lumpur in 2027?
Kuala Lumpur has 18 public holidays in 2027: New Year's Day (Tahun Baharu), Friday 1 January; Thaipusam, Friday 22 January; Federal Territory Day (Hari Wilayah Persekutuan), Monday 1 February; Chinese New Year (Tahun Baharu Cina), Saturday 6 February; Chinese New Year (second day), Sunday 7 February; Nuzul Al-Quran, Wednesday 24 February; Hari Raya Puasa (Eid al-Fitr), Wednesday 10 March; Hari Raya Puasa (second day), Thursday 11 March; Labour Day (Hari Pekerja), Saturday 1 May; Hari Raya Haji (Eid al-Adha), Monday 17 May; Wesak Day, Thursday 20 May; Awal Muharram (Islamic New Year), Sunday 6 June; Official Birthday of the Yang di-Pertuan Agong, Monday 7 June; Prophet Muhammad's Birthday (Maulidur Rasul), Sunday 15 August; National Day (Hari Kebangsaan), Tuesday 31 August; Malaysia Day, Thursday 16 September; Deepavali, Thursday 28 October; Christmas Day (Hari Krismas), Saturday 25 December. Replacement days: 3 (all expected by law).
What is the weekend in Kuala Lumpur?
Saturday and Sunday, as everywhere except Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu, which rest on Friday and Saturday.
What happens when a public holiday falls on the weekend?
Under the Holidays Act 1951 (section 3), a public holiday on the weekly holiday (Sunday) moves to the next day, or to the day after if that is already a holiday. A holiday on a Saturday gets no replacement day. Replacement days follow from the law and are often not announced separately, so they are dashed here as expected until a notice confirms them.
Which replacement days (cuti ganti) are there in Kuala Lumpur in 2027?
Monday 8 February (Chinese New Year), expected by law; Tuesday 8 June (Awal Muharram), expected by law; Monday 16 August (Maulidur Rasul), expected by law.
When are Hari Raya Puasa, Hari Raya Haji, Chinese New Year and Deepavali in 2027?
Hari Raya Puasa: Wednesday 10 March and Thursday 11 March. Hari Raya Haji (Hari Raya Qurban): Monday 17 May. Chinese New Year: Saturday 6 February and Sunday 7 February. Deepavali: Thursday 28 October. The government list marks Hari Raya Puasa (Wednesday 10 March and Thursday 11 March), Hari Raya Haji (Monday 17 May) and Deepavali (Thursday 28 October) «Tertakluk kepada perubahan» (subject to change), so they are dashed here until the date is confirmed. The Islamic dates follow the Keeper of the Rulers' Seal's announcement after the moon sighting and can move by a day.
What are the long weekends in Kuala Lumpur in 2027?
7 long weekends in Kuala Lumpur with no leave, counting its Saturday and Sunday weekend and replacement days: New Year, Fri, 1 Jan – Sun, 3 Jan (3 days); Thaipusam, Fri, 22 Jan – Sun, 24 Jan (3 days); Federal Territory Day, Sat, 30 Jan – Mon, 1 Feb (3 days); Chinese New Year, Sat, 6 Feb – Mon, 8 Feb (3 days); Hari Raya Haji, Sat, 15 May – Mon, 17 May (3 days); Awal Muharram and the Agong's Birthday, Sat, 5 Jun – Tue, 8 Jun (4 days); Maulidur Rasul, Sat, 14 Aug – Mon, 16 Aug (3 days). The leave-day ideas are in the long weekends section.
What is a cuti peristiwa?
A cuti peristiwa is a one-off holiday a state declares under the Holidays Act 1951 (section 9), for example for a royal occasion or a sports win, usually at short notice. None is announced for 2027 in Kuala Lumpur yet; in 2026 there were 12 (Perlis 1 January, Kedah 1 February, Perlis 19 February, Kelantan 19 March, Terengganu 19 March, Kedah 23 March, Terengganu 23 March, Johor 25 May, Johor 13 July, Negeri Sembilan 3 August, Selangor 1 September, Selangor 25 October). The Labour Department (JTKSM) notes that they are optional for private employers.
Do private sector employees get every public holiday?
In Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan, private employers follow the Employment Act 1955, section 60D. The Labour Department (JTKSM) explains it in its notices: five paid public holidays are compulsory and the employer chooses six more from the gazetted list; a paid holiday that falls on a rest day moves to the next working day, and with the employee's agreement one of the six chosen holidays can be swapped for another day (the five compulsory ones cannot). Special state holidays (cuti peristiwa) are optional for private employers. Sabah and Sarawak have their own labour laws. This calendar shows the public holiday list; your employer's holiday list may differ.
When are the school holidays in 2027?
School holidays are not on this calendar. The Ministry of Education (KPM) publishes them in its school calendar (Kalendar Akademik), which also lists the two weekend groups.
Can I print the 2027 calendar for free?
Yes. Three free A4 PDFs for Kuala Lumpur: a year calendar (landscape and portrait) and a monthly calendar of 12 pages, with every holiday written in full and weeks starting on Sunday.
How many working days are there in Kuala Lumpur in 2027?
246, Monday to Friday, after the 15 days off that fall on those days; the number for each month is next to the month's name in the calendar. Some workplaces also work on Saturdays or half days.

Sources and method
All sources checked 21 October 2026.
Public holiday dates come from the Prime Minister's Department (BKPP) lists for each year and the later federal and state gazettes and notices linked below; replacement days not announced separately are worked out from the Holidays Act 1951 and the Sabah and Sarawak ordinances. The layout, the PDFs, the working-day counts and the long-weekend ideas are made by ToolNeko; this is not a government publication.
Working days: the five days outside the weekend (Monday to Friday; Sunday to Thursday in Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu) minus the public holidays and replacement days of the page; the All of Malaysia page counts the national holidays only. Replacement days (cuti ganti): a holiday on the weekly holiday moves to the next day that is not already a holiday (Holidays Act 1951, section 3); in Sabah a Sunday holiday moves to the next day, and in Sarawak to the following Monday for the days the ordinance's First Schedule lists above its note. Where no notice confirms a replacement day it is dashed as expected. In Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu a holiday on a Friday or Saturday gets a replacement day only if the state announces one, so none is drawn. Some workplaces also work on Saturdays or half days, and private employers follow the Employment Act 1955. Long weekends: three or more days off in a row with a public holiday; leave plans: the fewest leave days that join a holiday to the weekend.
