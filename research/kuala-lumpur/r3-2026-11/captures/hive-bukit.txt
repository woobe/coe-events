About This Space
Flexible Coworking & Serviced Office Space in Desa Petaling, KL
Positioned along the high-visibility KL–Seremban Expressway corridor in Desa Petaling, the Hive Bukit Kuala Lumpur establishes a strategic benchmark as southern Kuala Lumpur’s premier flexible workspace, offering a high-impact work space in Kuala Lumpur for enterprises scaling across Taman Desa, Sri Petaling, Kuchai Lama, and Bukit Jalil. Encompassing nearly 17,000 square feet within the dynamic Bukit Kuala Lumpur Retail Park development, this contemporary corporate hub integrates biophilic timber design and sun-drenched collaborative zones with enterprise-grade connectivity, fully serviced private suites, ergonomic hot desks, and tech-enabled boardroom facilities.
Designed to optimize operational efficiency for hybrid workforces, satellite corporate teams, and agile ventures, members leverage turnkey business hospitality, bespoke event venues, and abundant commuter parking alongside integrated lifestyle, dining, and retail infrastructure, delivering unmatched regional accessibility, workplace agility, and executive presence in the Greater Kuala Lumpur market.
