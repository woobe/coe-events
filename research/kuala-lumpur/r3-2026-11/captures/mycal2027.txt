Long Weekends in Malaysia 2027
Long weekends are three or more days off in a row without annual leave. They are our own calculation. They assume Saturday–Sunday rest days, and Friday–Saturday for Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu. Confirm with your employer. See theMalaysia calendar 2027to plan around these dates, and theMalaysia school holidays 2027if you are planning around school breaks.

Frequently asked questions
How many public holidays are there in Malaysia in 2027?
The Cabinet list has 15 federal public holiday dates. With state holidays added, Terengganu has the most (21). See the state table above for your total.
Which states have different weekend days?
Rest days are not part of the Cabinet list. Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu are reported to use a Friday–Saturday weekend and other states Saturday–Sunday. Check with your employer.
How do replacement public holidays (cuti ganti) work?
No 2027 replacement holiday has been announced. If the usual practice is followed, a federal holiday on a Sunday moves to the next working day. That would affect Chinese New Year day 2 (7 Feb), Awal Muharram (6 Jun) and Maulidur Rasul (15 Aug) in most states. Rules can differ by state.
