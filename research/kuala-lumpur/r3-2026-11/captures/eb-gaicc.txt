GAICC AI Governance Conference Malaysia 2027 | Governing the AI Future
ByGlobal Master Training & Consultancy Sdn Bhd
Follow
The Majestic Hotel Kuala Lumpur, Autograph CollectionKuala Lumpur, Wilayah Persekutuan Kuala Lumpur
Sunday, January 24, 2027  •  9 AM - 5 PM
Overview
Explore responsible AI, ISO/IEC 42001 and practical AI governance through expert keynotes, panel discussions and a hands-on workshop.
GAICC AI Governance Conference Malaysia 2027
Governing the AI Future | Building Responsible, Safe and Human-Centric AI
Artificial intelligence is transforming the way organisations operate, innovate and make decisions. But as AI adoption accelerates, an important question emerges:
How can organisations harness the opportunities of AI while ensuring responsible governance, accountability and effective risk management?
Join us at theGAICC AI Governance Conference Malaysia 2027, a one-day professional conference organised by theGlobal AI Certification Council (GAICC), withGlobal Master Training & Consultancy Sdn. Bhd. as the Malaysia Partner.
Bringing together business leaders, technology professionals, policymakers and governance practitioners, this conference explores how AI governance principles can be translated into practical approaches that support responsible and trustworthy AI adoption.
What to Expect
Expert Keynote Presentations
Gain insights into emerging AI governance challenges, organisational readiness, responsible innovation and the evolving AI landscape.
AI Accountability & Governance Panel
Explore perspectives on AI accountability, regulatory developments, ethics, risk management and the responsibilities of organisations deploying AI.
ISO/IEC 42001 and AI Governance in Practice
Understand the relevance of international AI management system standards and how governance frameworks can support organisational oversight and responsible AI adoption.
Hands-On AI Governance Workshop
Participate in an interactive workshop featuringGovern365.ai, exploring practical AI governance approaches and techniques. Workshop participants will receive aCertificate of Participationissued by GAICC.
Professional Networking
Connect with industry leaders, AI professionals, technology experts and governance practitioners to exchange perspectives and build meaningful professional relationships.
Who Should Attend?
This conference is particularly relevant for:
CEOs, directors and senior business leaders
CIOs, CTOs, CISOs and technology leaders
AI practitioners and digital transformation professionals
Risk management, compliance and legal professionals
Corporate governance and internal audit specialists
Consultants, researchers and AI governance practitioners
Professionals interested in responsible AI implementation
What Will You Gain?
By attending, you will have the opportunity to:
Develop a better understanding of responsible AI governance and accountability.
Explore AI-related risks, regulatory developments and international standards.
Learn practical approaches to strengthening organisational AI governance.
Experience hands-on governance activities through Govern365.ai.
Connect with professionals working across AI, technology, risk and compliance.
Event Details
Date:Sunday, 24 January 2027
Time:9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (MYT)
Venue:The Majestic Hotel Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
Format:In-person conference with keynote presentations, an expert panel, networking and a practical workshop.
Included:Conference sessions, practical workshop, buffet lunch, morning and afternoon refreshments, and a Certificate of Participation for the workshop.
Registration
Early Bird:RM 488 until 24 December 2026
Standard:RM 788 for non-members from 25 December 2026
Group Registration:RM488 per participant for groups of five or more from the same organisation.
Visit the official event website for registration and the latest programme updates:
https://hub.gaicc.org/events/ai-governance-conference#register
Be Part of the Conversation
The future of AI is not only about what technology can do, but how responsibly we choose to use it.
Join the GAICC AI Governance Conference Malaysia 2027 and take part in the conversation shaping a more responsible, trustworthy and sustainable AI future.
