About This Space
Serene Shared Offices & Coworking Space in Ampang Jaya
Situated along Jalan Kolam Air Lama, Common Ground Ampang offers a peaceful flexible workspace retreat less than 10 minutes from Kuala Lumpur’s Golden Triangle. Surrounded by prestigious embassy enclaves, top international schools, reputable hospitals, and vibrant dining spots, this location provides the ideal balance of city access and suburban calm. Featuring stylish timber interiors, high-speed fibre internet, quiet private office suites, and an in-house café vibe, this co-working space is tailored for neighbourhood entrepreneurs, creative small teams, and satellite offices. Stop by today for a personal walkthrough tour and see why top locals love working here in Ampang.
