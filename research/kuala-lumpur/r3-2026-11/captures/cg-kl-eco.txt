About This Space
Grade-A Enterprise Coworking & Private Offices in KL Eco City
Situated inside the sustainable Grade-A Menara 2 tower, Common Ground KL Eco City places your team at the intersection of Kuala Lumpur’s major commercial enclaves. Featuring a direct covered bridge to Mid Valley Megamall and seamless transit access via Abdullah Hukum LRT and KTM stations, your daily commute and dining options are effortless. Designed with double-height ceilings, light-filled breakout zones, state-of-the-art boardrooms, and flexible private suites, this co-working space delivers a high-performance setting for tech companies, corporate satellite teams, and fast-scaling start-ups. Schedule your private walkthrough tour today to unlock your team’s full potential.
