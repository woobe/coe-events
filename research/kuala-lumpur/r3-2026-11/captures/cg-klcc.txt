About This Space
Premier Coworking Space in KLCC for Ambitious Teams & Founders
Situated in the heart of Kuala Lumpur’s CBD inside Menara Darussalam, Common Ground KLCC offers an inspiring environment directly adjacent to Grand Hyatt KL and steps away from Suria KLCC. Designed with sleek modern interiors and floor-to-ceiling city views, this premier co-working space combines luxury aesthetics with high-speed corporate connectivity. Whether you need a prestigious business address, state-of-the-art meeting rooms, or flexible private office suites, this prime central location connects you directly to KL’s top financial and creative minds. Walk in today for a personal walkthrough tour and experience a flexible workspace that elevates your business in KLCC.
