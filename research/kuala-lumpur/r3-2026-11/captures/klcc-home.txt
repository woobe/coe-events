Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre is recognised as one of the best convention centres in Asia, strategically located in the heart of Kuala Lumpur. As a world-class international convention centre, we are the preferred venue for global events, trade shows, and conventions. Offering state-of-the-art facilities, exceptional services, and versatile event spaces, we are designed to host international exhibitions, conferences, and corporate events in Malaysia.
