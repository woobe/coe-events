Our Difference
Immersing You in the Joys of Discovery
What makes one city more attractive than another? It could be the sights, the cuisine, or the architecture. More often than not, it’s the people and their culture.
A stay at PARKROYAL will immerse you in these and more—all within the comforts of your hotel.
Dine on dishes made with local ingredients, admire art inspired by indigenous traditions, and enjoy sounds and scents that speak volumes about the region’s heritage.
It is more than a hotel experience. It’s a taste of your destination, one joyful discovery after another.
Signature Experiences
Uniquely at PARKROYAL
The Lobby: Alive with life
The moment you set foot in a PARKROYAL lobby, you know you are in a special place.
Vibrant decor, uplifting music and an intimate bar provide the perfect setting for socialising. Share stories with fellow travellers. Make new friends. Or chat with our PARKROYAL Local for tips on where to explore next.
Just feel like soaking in the energy? Pick a relaxing corner. Our comfortable seatings practically invite you to sink in, unwind, and watch the world go by.
Gateway to the Locale
It takes an expert to show you the heart and soul of a locale. That’s where our PARKROYAL Local come in.
They’ll tell you all about scenic spots off the beaten track, and diners only locals know about. Shopping? Trust them to show you a hidden gem or two.
At select hotels, you can even join culinary, artisanal and traditional programmes. They are an excellent way to get a hands-on understanding of local culture.
Simply Yours Celebrations
Whether it's an intimate occasion or a milestone in life, there's always a reason to celebrate.
At PARKROYAL, you’ll find the perfect settings, people and experiences to turn your celebrations into unforgettable masterpieces.
Our Simply Yours Celebrations Programme offers meticulously organised packages for you show love, commemorate life, and create memories.
Speak to us and let us help you make your dream moments shine.
