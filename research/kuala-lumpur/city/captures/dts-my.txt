Digital Transformation Summit
The 56th Edition of the Digital Transformation Summit is part of a global series spanning over 11 countries across multiple continents. Under the theme “Redefining Leadership for the Digital Age,” the summit will bring together 150+ delegates, including C-level executives, directors, and heads of technology, in Kuala Lumpur to explore how digital transformation is reshaping business strategy and leadership.
The summit offers a platform for knowledge-sharing and networking, with discussions on AI, cybersecurity, cloud computing, automation, and emerging technologies. It will also address digital governance, workforce transformation, and change management, equipping leaders with strategies to drive innovation, improve operational efficiency, and achieve sustainable business growth.
What Sets Us Apart?
The summit brings together the entire ecosystem behind enterprise transformation technology leaders, AI innovators, cloud providers, and government stakeholders to address the shift toward autonomous enterprises, while focusing on the most impactful enterprise technology trends discussed at global summits, including AI-driven automation, digital infrastructure, and secure, scalable transformation.
Outcome-focused insights
Gain actionable insights from Malaysia's foremost technology and business leaders driving the nation's digital transformation journey. Explore the strategies, technologies, and leadership priorities shaping the primary goals of Malaysia's Digital 2045 vision and accelerating enterprise innovation at scale.
Exclusive attendance model
Join a carefully curated community of CIOs, CTOs, CISOs, digital transformation leaders, policymakers, and enterprise innovators contributing to Malaysia's digital economy initiatives for 2045. This invitation-led format ensures focused discussions, premium peer engagement, and direct access to decision-makers shaping the country's digital future.
Purpose-driven networking
Engage with 150+ senior delegates from across Malaysia in an environment built for strategic conversations, executive collaboration, and high-value relationship building. From curated networking breaks to leadership panels and luncheons, every interaction is designed to create meaningful business connections.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Digital Transformation Summit Malaysia 2027?
The Digital Transformation Summit Malaysia is a C-suite technology summit. It brings together 200+ CIOs, CTOs, CISOs & heads of digital from Malaysia’s enterprises to shape the country’s next phase of digital growth. Built around the theme “Redefining Leadership for the Digital Age,” it covers scaling enterprise AI, responsible AI governance, cyber resilience, and the future of the workforce. Through keynotes, panel discussions and peer networking, the summit helps leaders turn national digital plans into measurable business results.
What topics are covered at Digital Transformation Summit Malaysia 2027?
The 2027 agenda covers:
– Malaysia’s AI Decade: From National Roadmap to Enterprise Reality
– AI with Purpose: Aligning Innovation with Business Priorities
– The Executive Cyber Dialogue: Cyber Resilience Under Act 854 and NCII
– The AI Governance Exchange: Leading AI Responsibly Before Regulation Catches Up
– Workforce Reinvention in the Age of Agentic AI
When and where is the Digital Transformation Summit Malaysia taking place?
The 54th edition of the Digital Transformation Summit Malaysia takes place on 14 April 2027, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.
Who attends Digital Transformation Summit Malaysia?
The summit brings together 200+ C-level executives, directors and heads of technology. That includes CIOs, CTOs, CDOs and CISOs, plus heads of digital transformation, cloud, data, AI, cybersecurity and IT infrastructure. Government CIOs and CTOs also attend.
What networking opportunities does the summit offer?
The Digital Transformation Summit Malaysia is a one-day, invitation-focused summit, so attendees connect with 200+ senior technology leaders, not a general crowd. There are pre-arranged one-to-one meetings with technology providers, networking breaks and a networking lunch, and peer-led roundtables on AI, cloud and cybersecurity.
How to register for the summit?
To attend the summit, submit the Delegate Enquiry form on the event page. Registration is open to CIOs, CTOs, CISOs, CDOs and other senior technology decision-makers.
