Questions and Answers
What is Malaysian AI?
Malaysian AI is a public community hub for people learning, building, and starting companies with AI in Malaysia. We connect workshops, events, independent builder communities, and a residency for teams shipping AI products.
Who is Malaysian AI for?
First-timers, developers, founders, organisers, and anyone curious about AI. You can come with a question, a side project, or a company already in motion — both newcomers and experienced builders are welcome.
Are events free to attend?
Most Malaysian AI community events are free and open. Check the event listing on our Luma calendar for details, capacity, and anything you should bring.
Do I need coding experience?
No. Many workshops are designed for beginners who want to try AI tools with help nearby. Experienced builders also join for demos, hackathons, and deeper technical sessions.
How do I find and join an event?
Browse upcoming events on the homepage calendar or on luma.com/malaysianai. Pick a session that fits, register on Luma, and show up ready to learn or share what you are building.
What is the AI Residency?
The AI Residency is a working home in Kuala Lumpur for founders building serious AI products. Residents get co-working space, regular demos, and support across product, engineering, and fundraising. Explore the residency page to learn more and apply.
How can I add my community to the directory?
If you run an AI community, meetup, or builder group in Malaysia, contact us on WhatsApp. We will review it and get back to you about listing.
Who hosts Malaysian AI?
Malaysian AI is an initiative started by 500 Global. It brings founders and community organisers together around building useful AI products in Malaysia.
Where is Malaysian AI based?
We are based in Kuala Lumpur at 500 Global, Bangunan AICB, 10 Jalan Dato' Onn. Events may also happen with partner communities across Malaysia — always check the event listing for the venue.
