About us
LFDT Meetup groups have an informal relationship with LF Decentralized Trust and make up a key part of the LFDT ecosystem. Participation in a LFDT Meetup group is open to anyone--employees of a LFDT member company, LFDT contributors and developers, and people just passionate about distributed trust technology.
For more information about LFDT, please visit:
http://lfdecentralizedtrust.org/
For LFDT Meetup Guidelines, please visit:
http://meetups.lfdecentralizedtrust.org
To stay in touch with the latest LFDT news, sign up to our weekly newsletter:

Hyperledger Fabric for Aerospace Part Traceability: A Doctoral Research Overview
Wed, Dec 2 · 1:00 AM MYT
·
Online
Online
41 attendees from 150 groups
Aircraft parts and components move across airlines, manufacturers, suppliers, and maintenance organizations throughout their service lives. Keeping their maintenance and ownership records accurate and
consistent across these organizations is a continuing challenge, especially when each organization uses different enterprise systems.
In this session, Abhinag Vangala, a doctoral candidate at the University of the Cumberlands, will share an overview of his qualitative research on the use of Hyperledger Fabric for part and component traceability in
multi-organizational environments. The session will cover:
The traceability challenge in aerospace maintenance, repair, and overhaul (MRO)
What prior research has found, and where gaps remain
Why Hyperledger Fabric was selected for this study
The study’s three focus areas: system performance, enterprise system integration, and operational suitability
This session may interest Fabric developers, architects, and practitioners who have worked on networks involving multiple organizations.
Abhinag will also share how U.S. based professionals with experience in Hyperledger Fabric or enterprise permissioned blockchain systems can voluntarily take part in the study.

Network event
Talk to EUDI Wallets Without Reading 1,400 Pages of Specs
Wed, Dec 9 · 1:00 AM MYT
·
Online
Online
198 attendees from 150 groups
Connecting a service to EUDI Wallets is like a family dinner with 27 relatives who each speak their own versioned dialect of the same language. You need fluent OID4VCI, OID4VP, SD-JWT VC, ISO mdoc, status lists, trust lists and X.509, ideally by yesterday.
EUDIPLO (https://github.com/EUDIPLO) is an open source project hosted by LF Decentralized Trust. It’s the diplomat you send so you don’t have to go yourself. Your backend makes plain HTTP calls, and EUDIPLO handles the handshakes and cryptographic small talk with the wallets.
The meetup will cover the following parts:
- Issue, request and “show me your ID before I give you one,” all in one API
- Conformance-tested against the OpenID Foundation suites
- Multi-tenancy, key and certificate management, ETSI trust lists
- From zero to running with eudiplo demo
- your questions how to use and contribute
The session ends with a live demo, if the demo gods cooperate.
