Frequently Asked Questions
Find answers to common questions about our services, membership benefits, amenities, and more.
Hit us up
1. How do I book an event space?
You can book directly via our website or contact our team to check availability and discuss your requirements.
2. Can I bring my own catering?
Catering services can be arranged through our preferred partners for convenience.
3. What technology is available in the rooms?
WIFI, projector or Smart TV, flipchart board, drinking water, and on-site support.
4. Are there extra charges for weekends or after office hours?
Yes, non-office hours have a different rate with minimum booking of 3 hours. Air-conditioning is charged separately if required.
Find A Workspace Solution That Fits Your Business.
If your business needs something different, explore our other membership options:
Virtual Office – a professional business address and mail handling service without a physical desk, ideal for remote teams and registered companies.
Hot Desk – drop into any available desk on a flexible, pay-as-you-need basis, well suited to freelancers and frequent travellers.
Dedicated Desk – reserve your own desk in a shared space for daily consistency without the cost of a full office.
Meeting Rooms – book by the hour for client presentations, interviews, or team workshops.
Event Space – a fully equipped venue for workshops, launches, or larger gatherings.
Day Pass – try WSPACE for a single day with full access to amenities and no long-term commitment.
Corporate Flexible Office – enterprise-grade flexible office solutions for larger teams.
