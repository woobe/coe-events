Exito Media Concepts Announces Its 2027 APAC Event Calendar, Connecting Industry Leaders Across Asia-Pacific
A year-long series of summits spanning cybersecurity, digital transformation, BFSI technology, manufacturing IT, DevOps and workforce leadership
Exito Media Concepts, which runs invite-only B2B technology summits across EMEA and APAC, has announced its APAC event calendar for 2027. Running from February to September 2027, the calendar brings together senior technology and business leaders across key markets in Asia-Pacific to address the region’s most pressing enterprise priorities.
The 2027 programme spans seven focus areas: Cyber Security, Digital Transformation, BFSI IT, Manufacturing IT, DevOps, Lodestar and Workforce Leadership. Events will take place in the Philippines, India, Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Vietnam, and Australia. The Cyber Security Summit and the BFSI IT Summit, each hosted in six cities, form the largest share of the calendar. The Digital Transformation Summit will be hosted in three cities, and the Manufacturing IT Summit in two.
The calendar opens with the Cyber Security Summit in Manila on 17 February 2027 and concludes with the Cyber Security Summit in Kuala Lumpur on 22 September 2027.
Introducing Lodestar: The Southeast Asia CISO Assembly
The 2027 calendar also marks the debut of Lodestar – the Southeast Asia CISO Assembly, a new flagship format from Exito. Edition One will take place in Bali from 3 to 5 June 2027. Lodestar will host 120 vetted security buyers from six Southeast Asian markets alongside 24 partners, all under one roof. Every meeting is agreed by both sides in advance, so each conversation is relevant before anyone travels.
“Senior leaders in Asia-Pacific have very little time, and they expect an event to earn it. That makes the room the whole product, so for 2027, we have built twenty of them across nine cities, each one shaped so that a buyer and a provider who should be talking actually sit down together. With Lodestar in Bali we have taken it further, putting only intent-qualified senior buyers into the meeting schedule, in 25-minute slots,” said Sanpriya Mohanty, Director, Sponsorship Sales (APAC), Exito Media Concepts.
