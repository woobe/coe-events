Our Locations
Strategic Locations with Great Accessibility
Now with two locations to choose from, stay or live with us and find yourself in two different but equally vibrant neighbourhoods, both just minutes away from Kuala Lumpur’s CBD and all the fun places.
Can’t decide on Cheras or Bangsar South?
Find out more about the two co-living spaces below and pick the one that best suits your needs.
Cheras
Our latest development to open in March 2022, Komune Living & Wellness is nestled in the heart of a 99-acre tropical, public park in Cheras, Kuala Lumpur. Well-connected through major highways and easily accessible by public transportation, find yourself in a vibrant township with shopping malls, medical facilities, education centres, and numerous entertainment spots.
An integrated co-living and wellness hub focused on providing a better way of living, revitalizing, and rejuvenating, the key features of Komune Living & Wellness include a Wellness Hub, a Co-living hotel and Senior Care facilities, suited for people from all walks of life.

Bangsar South
Our first co-living hotel to open in October 2019, this Komune Living outlet is part of the dynamic integrated development of Bangsar South where business, social and entertainment coexist perfectly. Find yourself surrounded by lots of gastronomical, retail and recreational options all at your convenience.
Strategically located at the intersection of KL and PJ, Komune Living is easily accessible via major highways and well served by public transportation. You’re also just minutes away to must-see attractions and tourist landmarks in Malaysia’s capital city.

About
More than a stay
Not just a hotel or serviced residence, Komune Living’s co-living space concept is all about community, engagement and a co-sharing experience with like-minded individuals and travelers.
Beside the luxury of your own personal space, discover an all new value-smart, hassle-free co-living experience and make the most of the readily available communal spaces, facilities and events as you become part of a community.
Introduction
What is co-living?
Our take on co-living is all about a new way of living focused on community and convenience as well as an alternative accommodation option or hotel stay for experience-seekers when travelling or on holiday.
Move in for months or stay a few nights, whether as a short-stay guest or as a long-stay resident, you’re welcome to live as part of our community and share custom designed spaces and events with the comfort of being able to retreat to your own fully furnished private apartment or studio room at the end of the day.
Our Story
Why Komune Living?
The world is changing in exciting ways, and so is the way of living. All about creating an uplifting community and an ideal lifestyle based on openness and sharing, Komune Living is all about the future of living.
Great as an accommodation option for both long and short stays, Komune Living is designed to be your home as long as you need. Discover a smarter and more convenient way to live where you can live, work, play, and more with a sense of community when you want it and privacy when you need it.
Facilities
Living spaces, get-togethers and more
Because Komune Living is your home, your wellness retreat and your playground, the co-living facilities offered are one of a kind shared-spaces designed to bring people together while providing the opportunity for you to do something different every day.
From community spaces like the kitchen, dining, living area, and games room, to fitness facilities like the gym and pool, we take life and adventures beyond the traditional borders. Explore both our properties in Cheras and Bangsar South to see what we have to offer.
What we do
A new way of hassle-free, value-smart co-living and accommodation with a focus on comfort, convenience and community. Enjoy the comfort and privacy of your own studio room, or pop out to the many shared-spaces created for you to engage and get social.
