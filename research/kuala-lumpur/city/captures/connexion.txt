About Us
Discover Connexion Conference & Event Centre (CCEC) in Kuala Lumpur – a modern venue designed for versatile events. Situated within the Bangsar South integrated development, CCEC offers two dynamic spaces: Nexus and The Vertical.
Perfect for corporate functions, weddings, cocktail receptions, and intimate dinners, our center seamlessly connects both buildings via a covered bridge, providing a unique and flexible event experience.
Explore how CCEC can make your next event exceptional.

Get quick answers to your most common inquiries here:
How can I arrange a site inspection of the event spaces at CCEC?
To arrange a site inspection, you can contact CCEC directly through email at [email protected] or give us a call at 603 2386 9188 or send us a message via WhatsApp at 012-3989643 . We will schedule a convenient time for you to visit and explore the venue.
What are the parking charges at CCEC?
On weekdays, guest can validate their parking for only RM7 by bringing their debit card/credit card or TNG to our concierge. For weekends and public holidays, no validation is needed as it is a flat rate of RM2.
How to get to CCEC from the train stations?
From Kerinchi LRT Station: It’s an 8-10 minute walk to Connexion Conference & Event Centre via the Kerinchi Link Bridge.
From KL Gateway-Universiti Station: Enjoy a 5-7 minute walk outdoors or take the free hourly shuttle service (available on weekdays).
What amenities are available nearby?
There are convenience stores, coffee shops, clinics, grocery stores, and Masjid Ar-Rahah just 2-5 minutes away on foot or by car.
Are there any hotels nearby?
Event attendees, whether at The Vertical or Nexus, can enjoy two excellent hotel accommodation options.
VE Hotel & Residence
Located in the same building as Vertical and just a 2-minute walk across a covered link bridge to Nexus, it’s perfect for event attendees.
Komune Living
Another nearby option, offering a modern and affordable stay with easy access to all Bangsar South amenities, just a 10-minute walk away.
