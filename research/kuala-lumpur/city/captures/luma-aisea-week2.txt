About Event
​aisea week 2: electric boogaloo
13–21 november 2026 · kuala lumpur
​one week. builders across kl in the same rooms.
​aisea week is a week of workshops, talks, hangouts and build days, run by aisea and the communities in our network.
​every session has its own page on the aisea week calendar. pick the ones you want and register for each.
​what’s on
​workshops: hands-on sessions with the tools builders are using now
​talks and panels
​hangouts: evenings to meet the people building around you
​the schedule fills in as sessions are confirmed.
​run something?
if you run a community or a meetup, or your company has something worth showing, submit your event to the calendar and we’ll list it as part of the week.
​who it’s for
builders, founders, engineers and designers shipping with ai, in kl or passing through.
​
​hosted by aisea, southeast asia’s builder coalition.
