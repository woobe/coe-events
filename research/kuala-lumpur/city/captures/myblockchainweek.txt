FAQ
What is Malaysia Blockchain Week 2026?
Malaysia Blockchain Week 2026 is a two-day event connecting Malaysia and Southeast Asia’s Web3 ecosystem through talks, exhibitions, activations, networking, side events, and real conversations around blockchain adoption.
When and where is MYBW 2026 happening?
MYBW 2026 will take place on 29–30 July 2026 at World Trade Centre Kuala Lumpur.
Do I need to understand crypto or blockchain to attend?
No. MYBW 2026 is built for both Web3 professionals and people who are new to blockchain, crypto, and digital assets. You can attend to learn, explore, network, or simply understand what the space is about.
Who should attend MYBW 2026?
MYBW 2026 is suitable for businesses, brands, students, creators, investors, developers, founders, communities, media, and anyone curious about the future of Web3 and digital technology.
What can I expect at the event?
Expect keynote sessions, panel discussions, exhibitions, brand activations, networking areas, side events, community gatherings, and opportunities to meet people across Malaysia and Southeast Asia’s Web3 ecosystem.
How can my company get involved?
Companies can submit their interest through the sponsorship or partnership form on the website. The MYBW team will follow up with available packages, activation opportunities, and collaboration options.
