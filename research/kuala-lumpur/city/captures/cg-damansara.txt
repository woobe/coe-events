About This Space
Flexible Shared Office Space in Damansara Heights KL
Situated in one of Kuala Lumpur’s most exclusive commercial enclaves, Common Ground Damansara Heights offers a dynamic co-working hub for corporate innovation and entrepreneurial growth. Located just a short two-minute walk from Semantan MRT station and surrounding upscale eateries, this venue balances high-grade professional infrastructure with a warm, collaborative atmosphere. Featuring expansive breakout lounges, flexible private office suites, and bespoke enterprise solutions, it provides the ideal setting for teams seeking seamless connectivity away from CBD congestion. Contact us today to schedule your visit and join our thriving business network in Damansara Heights.
