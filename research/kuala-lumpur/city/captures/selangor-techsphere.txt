ABOUT THE SUMMIT
Malaysia’s Leading Platform for Industrial Transformation
Where Industry, Technology and Government Connect
Selangor Techsphere Summit (STS) is an industrial technology platform connecting manufacturers, technology providers, government, investors and industry leaders to advance the next generation of manufacturing and industrial growth.
Powered by HANNOVER MESSE, STS brings together the technologies, expertise and decision-makers shaping areas including smart manufacturing, automation, digitalisation, AI, sustainability and workforce transformation.
In 2027, STS returns to the Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre from [DATES TBC] as a flagship component of the Selangor International Business Summit (SIBS). Backed by the Selangor State Government, the Summit provides a gateway for companies looking to connect with Malaysia’s industrial ecosystem and build opportunities across the wider ASEAN market.

2027 EDITION
What to Expect in 2027
Industrial Technology Showcase
Explore technologies across Robotics & Automation, AI & Machine Learning, Industrial Software & Data Management, Additive Manufacturing, Smart Logistics, Cybersecurity, Cloud & Infrastructure and Carbon-Neutral Production, bringing the technologies shaping next-generation industry into one platform.
Industry Conference & Insights
Hear from manufacturing leaders, technology experts, policymakers and industry decision-makers on the forces reshaping industrial growth, from AI and automation to advanced manufacturing, digitalisation, sustainability and the future workforce.
Business & Investment Access
Connect beyond the STS floor through the wider Selangor International Business Summit (SIBS) ecosystem, alongside platforms including the Selangor Investment & Industrial Park Expo (SPARK), Selangor ASEAN Business Conference (SABC), Selangor Investment Forum (SIF) and AiSEM @ Selangor, opening access to business, investment and partnership opportunities across Selangor and ASEAN.
Government & Industry Engagement
Engage within a business ecosystem initiated by the Selangor State Government and powered by Invest Selangor Berhad, bringing government, investors and industry into the same conversation around technology adoption, industrial development and future investment.
