About This Space
Strategic Enterprise Coworking Hub in KL Sentral Station Precinct
Positioned on a high floor in the Green Building-certified Q Sentral tower, Common Ground KL Sentral offers unmatched transit convenience for mobile professionals and global enterprises. Directly linked to the main KL Sentral transportation hub, travelling to KLIA or across the Klang Valley takes just minutes. This premier co-working space combines expansive floor plans, panoramic skyline views, flexible private office suites, and micro-roasted coffee bars designed to foster high-level corporate networking. Book your private walkthrough tour today, meet our local community team, and give your growing business the ultimate transit-oriented workspace advantage in Kuala Lumpur.
