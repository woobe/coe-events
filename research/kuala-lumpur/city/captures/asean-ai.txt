AiSEAN Summit 2027: One Vision. Shaping Tomorrow.
The Global Event for AI in Asia. 10–11 June 2027, Hilton Hotel, KL Sentral, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.
At a glance
1000+ Delegates
100+ Speakers
20+ Ministerial Keynotes
20+ Executive Panels
Eight strategic pillars
AI Strategy & National Policies – From national strategy to global leadership: building AI-ready nations across ASEAN and beyond.
Generative AI – Generative AI has changed everything: what happens next, from the people building the next generation.
AI Governance & Ethics – Innovating fast enough, and governing AI responsibly. Who draws the line, and where?
Cybersecurity for AI · CyberAI Briefing – The CyberAI Briefing: NACSA, CSA, KISA, INTERPOL, WEF and their peers on defending the new AI attack surface.
AI Infrastructure, Data Centres & Cloud – The infrastructure behind the AI revolution: can Asia build enough, and who powers the compute race?
Agentic AI – From copilots to autonomous enterprises: when AI starts making the decisions.
Enterprise AI – From AI ambition to AI at scale: including the industry leadership session and the ROI conversation.
Future Workforce & Skills – Will AI take our jobs or change them? Closing the summit with the workforce question, and the Future Declaration.
