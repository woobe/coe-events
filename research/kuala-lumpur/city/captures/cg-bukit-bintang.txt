About This Space
Grade-A Coworking & Private Offices in Bukit Bintang KL
Spanning 21,000 square feet across two floors at the top of the prestigious Menara Worldwide tower, Common Ground Bukit Bintang places your business in Kuala Lumpur’s vibrant Golden Triangle. Boasting unobstructed, 360-degree views of the Petronas Twin Towers and downtown skyline, this MSC Cybercentre co-working venue provides high-speed enterprise connectivity, stylish lounge areas, and fully serviced private office suites. Just a 5-minute walk from Conlay MRT station and surrounded by world-class dining at Pavilion KL, it is the ultimate destination for corporate teams and global founders. Schedule your private tour today to elevate your work experience in Bukit Bintang.
