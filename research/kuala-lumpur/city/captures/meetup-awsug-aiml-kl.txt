About us
Welcome to the AWS User Group AI/ML, Kuala Lumpur! This group is for professionals and enthusiasts interested in all things artificial intelligence and machine learning on the Amazon Web Services platform. Join us for informative talks, workshops, and networking opportunities to help you stay up-to-date with the latest trends and advancements in AI and ML. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned expert, come learn, share, and connect with like-minded individuals in the AI/ML community. Let's explore the endless possibilities of AI and ML together!
