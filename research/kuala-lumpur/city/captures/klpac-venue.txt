Space In A Nutshell
klpac is Malaysia’s only fully-integrated arts centre housed in an award-winning heritage building with a 124-year-old history.
Besides presenting a varied programming, its venue is also open for bookings for performances, events and launches, festivals, shoots, recording and more.
Aside from its 7,614 sq metres of built up space, klpac also boasts a lawn area overlooking a lush 35-acre park, one of the few green lungs in KL.
The restaurants located in Sentul Park including Craf (in the same building), Bistro Richard and NuelBom Garden Korean Restaurant (opposite), and Grumpy Bagels @ Sentul Depot, and ample free outdoor parking.
Book An Appointment
Our team is happy to show you around but do schedule an appointment in advance. Recce is strictly by appointment only as it is a working arts centre with various events / users all year round (subject to availability of space & staff).
