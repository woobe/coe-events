Welcome to InnoAI 2027
Shaping the Future of
Artificial Intelligence
Following the success of its 2026 edition, InnoAI returns in 2027 with an expanded vision, broader
themes, and a distinguished lineup of global AI leaders. Scheduled for April 22-23, 2027, in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, the summit continues its mission to explore the technologies, trends, and ethical
dimensions shaping the future of artificial intelligence. Building on its reputation as a premier
gathering for innovators, policymakers, and industry pioneers, InnoAI 2027 will delve deeper into
transformative areas such as generative AI, autonomous systems, AI governance, and human AI
collaboration.
InnoAI-2027 places a strong emphasis on responsible AI development, sustainability, and the societal
impact of emerging technologies. A distinguished two-day event brings together industry leaders,
renowned academics, visionary innovators, and AI practitioners to shape the future of AI.
