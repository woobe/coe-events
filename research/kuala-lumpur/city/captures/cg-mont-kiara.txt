About This Space
Boutique Coworking & Virtual Offices in Mont Kiara
Located inside Wisma Mont Kiara, this boutique two-level venue seamlessly merges suburban convenience with enterprise-grade professionalism. Tailored specifically for expatriates, neighbourhood entrepreneurs, and growing satellite teams, Common Ground Mont Kiara sits directly above 1 Mont Kiara Mall, giving you immediate access to vibrant cafés, fitness centres, and retail outlets. This premier co-working space spans two floors and boasts warm timber finishes, quiet phone booths, flexible private office suites, and high-speed fibre internet designed for seamless global communication. Stop in for a tour today to discover your ideal work-life balance in the heart of Mont Kiara.
