Words That Defined Malaysia's
Digital Infrastructure Ambition at DCCI 2026
Malaysia has emerged as one of Southeast Asia's premier destinations for datacentre investment.
Global technology companies are drawn to Malaysia's strategic locations, strong connectivity, reliable utility infrastructure, and political stability. These advantages position Malaysia as a trusted and competitive hub for the next phase of digital growth in the region.

Malaysia’s Projected Datacentre Market By 2030
$13.57 Billion
Malaysia’s digital infrastructure revolution is being powered by one of Southeast Asia’s Fastest-growing Datacentre Ecosystems. Fuelled by unprecedented hyperscaler investments, surging AI infrastructure demand, rapid cloud expansion, and strong government backing, the nation is rapidly emerging as the region’s preferred destination for next-generation, AI-ready datacentres.
Malaysia’s Projected Cloud Services Market By 2032
$15.02 Billion
Malaysia’s hottest investment sector, its cloud market is being rapidly accelerated by enterprise-wide digital transformation, the rising adoption of AI, Generative AI & advanced analytics, the growing demand for hybrid and multi-cloud environments, and government-led cloud-first initiatives. The nation is now the engine behind ASEAN’s next wave of innovation, resilience, and digital economic growth.
Datacentre & Cloud Infrastructure (DCCI) Expo
Malaysia’s largest and most reputed expo that delivers the clearest, most distinctive and strategic focus on what’s next across the digital infrastructure ecosystem.
Providing a direct gateway to the multi-billion-dollar market, the 2027 edition will bring together the nation’s most elite community of datacentre and cloud experts on 4 - 5 May at the Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre to discuss – applied research, operational insights and practical solutions to advance the nation’s joint vision on datacentre and cloud infrastructure.
