About us
Kuala Lumpur is home to some of the world's best known Ethereum projects and it is the mission of ETHKL to be a meeting place to learn about Ethereum and similar blockchain technology powering some of today's most interesting innovations.
Sometimes referred to as Web 3.0, Ethereum smart contracts permit trust-less interaction and enable parties to exchange digital assets with confidence and undertake complex transactions knowing that the transaction is governed by a set of clearly defined rules.
