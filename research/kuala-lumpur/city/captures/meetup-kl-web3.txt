About us
Welcome to the Kuala Lumpur Web3 Meetup group! This group is for individuals interested in learning and discussing topics related to Web3 technologies, decentralized applications, blockchain, cryptocurrency, and the future of the Internet.
Whether you are a developer, entrepreneur, investor, or simply curious about the potential of Web3, this is the place to connect with like-minded individuals, share ideas, and stay updated on the latest trends.
Join us for engaging discussions, workshops, and networking opportunities to advance your knowledge and explore the exciting possibilities of Web3.
