About us
Welcome to Kong! We are so happy you are joining our global community!
This group is for everyone who is interested in learning more about APIs, microservices, Kubernetes, service mesh, and more. We are best known as the creator and primary supporter of Kong Gateway, the most widely adopted open-source API gateway.
Become a Community Organizer
We are always on the look for awesome community members to lead our user groups. The community organizer’s role is crucial to the success and vitality of Kong’s community. If you are interested in learning more, please email us at: meetups@konghq.com
Speak at a Meetup
We always want to hear from our community members about their awesome use cases! You can do a short talk of 15/20mins or a detailed technical presentation of 25/45min. Our team is happy to guide you through the process and help you with the presentation. And of course, all our community speakers get awesome swag!
Apply to speak: https://bit.ly/3JMA9Ws

Network event
Demystifying Intelligent Model Routing: What Works…and What Doesn’t
Fri, Dec 4 · 12:00 AM MYT
·
Online
Online
34 attendees from 80 groups
Multi-model architectures are becoming the default, and intelligent routing is often pitched as the layer that makes them simple and more cost effective. The reality is much more complicated.
In this session, Kong experts share where model routing is heading and which ideas don’t hold up in production. We’ll explore different routing philosophies and which philosophies work best for coding harness, chatbot, and other agentic use cases.
What you’ll learn:
Why routing logic belongs in the infrastructure layer as you adopt more models and providers
Why most routing claims in the market are questionable at best
Where and when to use certain routing strategies to improve cost efficiency, quality, security, and resilience
