Meetings Magnified
Create a dynamic setting for your next occasion by hosting it at W Kuala Lumpur. Featuring fully adaptable conference equipment that makes business beautiful, meetings memorable, and special events spectacular. Choose from our various venues in our luxury meeting room hotel in Kuala Lumpur​ to suit your needs.
Great Room
The sprawling Great Room with more than 1000sqm, is one of the largest event venues in Kuala Lumpur that you will find in the city center. An impressive LED wall sets the scene as a showstopper at a staggering 11m. A connecting pre-function foyer is prefect for the celebration of ceremonies as well as for the cocktail before the banquet.
Strategy
Let us help inspire you and get the ideas flowing in our Strategy Room with big window of daylights and also the latest technology.
Studio Room
A place for customised gatherings. Our meeting venue in Kuala Lumpur is the perfect place for product launches, press launches and media interviews where we can roll two studios into one simply by removing the wall, and guests can enjoy the stunning view of Petronas Twin Towers from this room.
Studio Three
When business is the agenda, our 90sqm studio three is the perfect meeting venue in Kuala Lumpur with floor-to-ceiling windows that offer remarkable view of the city skyline.
EWOW Suite
Whether it's a brainstorming session, strategy meeting or your company live stream, do it all in the comfort of our extreme wow suite overlooking the stellar expanse of Kuala Lumpur and the Petronas Twin Towers.
W Hotels Meetings Set-Up
Energize your meetings or special events at W Hotels. Whatever the occasion, at our meeting venues in Kuala Lumpur we'll create an atmosphere that'll engage your attendees and amplify your celebrations. We'll develop a meeting space that'll inspire and get ideas flowing.
What Cool Stuff
Bask in virtually limitless technology. LCD screens, high-speed Internet access, Wi-Fi and state-of-the-art audiovisual equipment at our meeting venues in Kuala Lumpur put you within a millisecond of the world you're influencing.
Meeting Menus
Our chefs serve up creative cuisine for all receptions and meetings; you'll enjoy an array of delicious and delectable fare to complement any occasion, prepared by the master chefs of W Kuala Lumpur.
Recess™
When is Recess™? Take a business break and inspire colleagues with our Recess options. Our Meeting Specialists will help you turn your meeting into a virtual playground by creating motivating and downright fun activities.

From the 20ft tall LED screen to the WET® Deck overlooking the Petronas Twin Towers in all her glory, saying “I do” has never looked so good. W Kuala Lumpur Hotel has one of the largest ballroom event venues in Kuala Lumpur, though you can also choose to throw your bridal shower or wedding celebration in a more intimate function room and venue. Get whisked away in the heart of the city at AWAY® Spa and the WET® while our curators dive into the nitty-gritty details of your wedding. Then, when the time comes, celebrate your wedding against the backdrop of KL's beautiful city skyline. You can count on W Kuala Lumpur and our wedding curator to make all of the arrangements for an extraordinary wedding day in Kuala Lumpur. Room Set-up Examples
Banquet
Generally used for meals and sessions involving small group discussions. A five foot round table seats eight people comfortably. A six foot round table seats 10 people comfortably.

Conference and Hollow Square
Appropriate for interactive discussions and note-taking sessions for fewer than 25 people. Many hotels have elegant "boardrooms" for 10 to 20 people, equipped with full audiovisual capabilities, a writing board, cork board and a flip chart.
Room Set-up Examples
Close
E-Shape, U-Shape and T-Shape
Appropriate for groups of fewer than 40 people. These are best for interaction with a leader seated at the head of the setup. Audiovisual is usually best set up at the open end of the seating.

Theater
Appropriate for large sessions and short lectures that do not require extensive note-taking. This is a convenient setup to use before breaking into discussion or role-playing groups because chairs can be moved.

Reception
Stand-up social function where beverages and light foods are served. Foods may be presented on small buffet tables or passed by servers. May precede a meal function.

Schoolroom or Classroom
The most desirable setup for medium to large-size lectures. Requires a relatively large room. Tables provide attendees with space for spreading out materials and taking notes.
