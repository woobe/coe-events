Welcome to World Trade Centre
Our Convention Hall
An iconic building with traditional architecture steep in custom and history. Variety of halls, meeting rooms in modern settings and open spaces. The flexibility allows for tailor-made event concepts – for traditional and new formats. Located in the heart of the nation’s capital and 45 minutes from Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA). Accessible via public transport and within walking distance from malls, restaurants, entertainment centres and 3 to 5-star hotels. Dedicated and specialised team working with you hand-in-hand to ensure your event runs swimmingly. This is why we are the preferred venue for local and international conventions, concerts, weddings and other prestigious events in Malaysia.

World Trade Centre Venues
WTC is where business happens and where memories are made. Review our full listing of business
summits, exhibitions, product launches, and grand social affairs.
Dewan Merdeka
Spanning over 2,000 m² with a ceiling height of 17.7 metres, Dewan Merdeka accommodates up to 3,000 guests. Ideal for large-scale events such as conventions, banquets, and concerts, it offers a grand setting for memorable occasions.
Tun Dr. Ismail
Covering 1,003 m² with a 7.01-metre ceiling, this hall fits up to 1,000 guests. It can be divided into Hall A or B (500 m² each), each equipped with two built-in LED display screens—perfect for seminars, workshops, and mid-sized gatherings.
Tun Hussein Onn
Covering 1,003 m² with a 7.01-metre ceiling, this hall accommodates up to 1,000 guests. Split into Hall A or B (500 m² each) with two built-in LED display screens, it is ideal for corporate launches, meetings, and private events.
