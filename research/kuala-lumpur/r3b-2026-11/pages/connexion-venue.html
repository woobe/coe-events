Where Unique and Inspiring Spaces Come Alive

Our venue features 5 Pillarless Ballrooms, 15 Function Rooms, 1 Junior Ballroom, 1 Auditorium, an upscale private event space called The Oak Room and a unique Rooftop Gazebo.

123456/6

Ballroom

  • Ballroom

    A grand, luxurious space perfect for weddings, galas, and large celebrations, our ballroom offers elegance and sophistication for unforgettable events.

  • Junior Ballroom

    An intimate yet stylish space, our Junior Ballroom is ideal for smaller gatherings, weddings, and elegant receptions.

  • Function Room

    Fexible, modern spaces designed to accommodate meetings, workshops, and intimate events, offering the perfect setting for productive gatherings.

  • The Oak Room

    An exclusive, elegantly designed space perfect for high end events, private meetings, and intimate gatherings.

  • Auditorium

    Our auditorium is a state-of-the-art space designed for conferences, graduation, presentations, and performances. It provides an ideal environment for impactful and engaging events.

  • Gazebo

    Intimate gatherings and cocktail receptions find a home at our Rooftop Gazebo, a sophisticated outdoor venue which seats up to 20 pax in a verdant and peaceful setting complete with water features and a beautifully designed landscaped garden.
