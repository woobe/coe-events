Philosophy Behind Publika

Publika is a vibrant urban sanctuary that blends creative retail with arts, culture, and community. We offer unique shopping experiences through fashion, accessories, and trend-setting concepts, creating a lively space where creativity thrives.

Sustainability

At Publika, sustainability is central to our operations and collaborations with tenants. Our Tenant Fit-Out Guidelines and House Rules incorporate core green lease principles, including energy-efficient fit-outs, mandatory use of energy-saving lighting, and responsible waste management. These initiatives lower environmental impact, encourage resource efficiency, and guide tenants toward more sustainable practices.

Embedding these practices within our tenancy framework underscores Publika's strong ESG governance, advances our sustainability ambitions, and encourages tenants, shoppers, and stakeholders to collectively foster a greener, more environmentally responsible community.

As part of Publika's ongoing sustainability commitment, we have completed the initial phase of energy-efficiency enhancements across the mall, such as converting conventional lightbulbs to LEDs and installing escalator sensors to optimize power usage.
