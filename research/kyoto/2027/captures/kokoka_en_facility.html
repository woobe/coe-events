Venues for Hire

Total 9 Spaces! Event Hall, meeting rooms of various sizes, and Japanese style building are available. Can be used for various activities including meetings, recitals, and company training sessions.

Reservation online

Amazing points of Kyoto International Community House(kokoka).

We have facillities suitable for all types of events.

  • Point① Facilities with beautiful views. You can enjoy the four beautiful seasons of Kyoto.
  • Point② Excellent accessibility. A 5 or 6 minutes walk from Keage subway station.
  • Point③ Kyoto International Community House(kokoka) makes it posible to combine your event with sightseeing! This is a handy starting location to visit many tourist attractions such as Heian Jingu Shrine, Nanzenji Temple, Eikando Temple, etc.
  • Point④ Convenient drink delivery service! Coffee shop in kokoka delivers coffee, tea, and other drinks from their menu. (Requires drink fee separately)

"Community Salon" Usage Fees

  • 1 time slot: 13,000 yen
  • 2 time slots: 25,000 yen
  • 3 time slots: 35,000 yen

Payment must be made in cash on the day of use.
Advance application is required. Please apply by phone or download the application form（Excel） and submit it via email or fax.
Reservation Period: From six months to one month before the date of use


  • ・Half-charges apply for use of the Event Hall and the Special Conference Room for preparation and rehearsals.（Fractional fees of less than 100 yen will round up）
    ＊However, this is only available if you are using the facility also for the actual event.
  • ・You can only extend your time for 30 minutes, and the usage fee will be increased by 50% for those 30 additional minutes.
    　Extension is extra charge, and limited to a maximum of a half-hour. Facilities cannot be used before 9:00 and after 21:00.（Extensions of less than 30 minutes will round up to 30 minutes, and fractional usage fees of less than 100 yen will round up to 100 yen.）
  • ・Equipment hire
    There is a limited amount of equipment available for hire. Please reserve beforehand by phone or email.
    Please pay usage fees for equipment and waste disposal to kokoka on the day of use in cash.
    • List of Equipment Available at the Venue（PDF, Japanese）
  • ・Waste disposal
    We can dispose of large amounts of waste at an additional charge. (For small amounts of waste, please use the garbage bin in each room and kitchenette on each floor.)
    Fee for waste disposal: ¥500 per bag. Please ask for designated garbage bags (45L) at the reception desk. Please separate:
    (1)Flammable garbage　(2) Plastics　(3) Cans, bottles, PET　(4) Cardboard
    *Please pack cardboard items together with tape and string.
